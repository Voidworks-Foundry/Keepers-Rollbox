# Action flyout (`scripts/flyouts/action/`)

What the Action button opens (menu Action · Check · Other), offered with one or two characters selected: the menu hides it with nothing selected or three or more (`panel/menu-host.js`; one action is one character's, or one acting on another). Tabs **Attack · Maneuver · Spell**, each with the **target box** on top. Every action goes through CoC7's own paths: its attack card (`actor.weaponCheck`, the same call as its sheet; a maneuver too, with a weapon that has CoC7's Maneuver ticked), its opposed card (Opposed POW, through `roll-requests/`, as the Opposed flyout), its spell casting (`item.system.cast`). When a sheet lacks what an action needs (an unarmed weapon, a maneuver weapon, an unarmed weapon's skill), the Keeper gets a prompt naming exactly what is added or fixed (`prompt.js`); a player is told to ask their Keeper. CoC7 rolls, compares and applies every result; attacks are never rolled by Rollbox. The Keeper acts for the one selected character, or with two selected the first acts and the second is the target (made the Keeper's Foundry target; **⇅ Swap** switches them); once a character's action is spent the Keeper's act buttons read **Override** and ask first (an NPC or creature with several attacks a round spends it with the last: "Attacks: 2/3" on the Attack tab); a player (Player Rollbox, with the Actions feature) acts only for their own investigator, one action per round (an attack, Aim, a reload, a maneuver or a spell), no attack in a round their character dived for cover, and in combat only on their turn (they can open and prepare everything at any time). With no encounter running, picking a real weapon brings up combat's weapon-drawn pop-up for the Keeper (shown by the Keeper's client; a player sees nothing of it; never for the unarmed weapon or a creature), and an attack on a token with no encounter running first waits for the Keeper's About to attack, then is the first blow: the Keeper starts combat after it ([combat.md](combat.md), `fight.js`). Action imports other parts through their `index.js` (`status/`, `player/`, `combat/`, `roll-requests/`), and the dive decision from the alert's `index.js` ([alert-flyout.md](alert-flyout.md)); the maneuver goals and Build dice and the scene-unit table from `shared/` ([shared.md](shared.md)).

Files: `index.js` (the flyout, its tabs, who acts and Swap, its hooks), `target.js` (the target box and Foundry's targeting), `weapons.js` (weapons, reach, ammo, the picked-weapon note), `attack.js` (the Attack tab), `maneuver.js` (the Maneuver tab), `prompt.js` (the Keeper's prompt and the player's "ask your Keeper" line), `reload-card.js` (the reload card in chat), `spell.js` (the Spell tab), `casting-time.js` (which spells show in combat), `spell-text.js` (a spell's text beside the flyout and beside CoC7's casting window, and its two settings), `action.css`; the improvised weapon's skills are `flyouts/improvised.js`, shared with the Other flyout's Item tab ([flyouts.md](flyouts.md)). The CoC7 calls are in `roll-requests/attack.js` (documented here, since this flyout is what uses them). User guide: [Action](../guide/action.md).

**Status key:** *Confirmed* only when the build guide's Internal API registry or changelog says so. Everything is **assumed** unless stated otherwise; "Seen in e2e" marks what the end-to-end run in Foundry has shown, still to be confirmed at the table.

## Code

### `registerActionFlyout()`, `renderActionFlyout(body, context)`, `REACH_SETTINGS`, `SPELL_SETTINGS`; private `ACTION_TABS` (`index.js`)
- **How it works:** `registerActionFlyout` returns `false` unless `player/` `panelHere()` (the Keeper's client with `rollboxEnabled`, or a player's with the Player Rollbox and their own switch on); then installs Foundry's `targetToken` hook (`targetChanged`, `target.js`), the alert's `DIVE_HOOK` (`diveChanged`), `updateActor` / `createActiveEffect` / `deleteActiveEffect` (`targetUpdated`), `updateItem` (`itemChanged`), `updateToken` (`tokenMoved`, `castingMoved`), `deleteCombat` (`clearPickedOnCombatEnd`), `updateActor` (`actorChanged`), `updateCombat` (`combatChanged`), `updateScene` (`sceneChanged`), `createCombatant` / `deleteCombatant` / `deleteCombat` (`encounterChanged`), `renderApplicationV2` (`onCastingWindow`) and `closeApplicationV2` (`onCastingWindowClosed`), and calls `registerFlyout("action", { title: "Action", render: renderActionFlyout })`. `renderActionFlyout` is the menu host's render: it takes the first two characters; nothing selected, or a player without the Actions feature (`canUse("actions")`), draws nothing; otherwise an `ActionFlyout` that draws the whole flyout from scratch on every change (`draw()`), keeps each acting character's tab states (`attackState`, `maneuverState`, `spellState`) across redraws, listens for target changes until its body leaves the page, and returns `{ apply({ tab }) }`. `ACTION_TABS` is the three tabs in order. `REACH_SETTINGS` and `SPELL_SETTINGS` re-export `attack.js`'s and `spell-text.js`'s `SETTINGS` for `settings.js`.
- **Who acts** (`pairRow`, `swap`, `aimAtTarget`): with one selected, that character. With two (the Keeper's case), the first acts and the second is `target`: `aimAtTarget` makes its token (`weapons.js` `tokenOf`) the Keeper's only Foundry target (`target.js` `targetToken`) unless it already is, so CoC7's card reads it and the target box shows it, drawn with `fixed` (no Change or ×). `pairRow` draws "Acting <name>" and **⇅ Swap** above the target box; `swap()` switches actor and target, aims at the new target and redraws with that character's own tab states. The tabs are the shared `tabBar` ([flyouts.md](flyouts.md)).
- **Limited (player), and the Keeper's Override:** `limit()` → why this tab can't act now, or `null`: `"acted"` for anyone whose action is spent (combat's `hasActed`), on the Attack tab `"dived"` for a character who dived for cover this round (`hasDived`), or `"wait"` for a player whose character is in the running encounter (combat's `inCombatFor`) and whose turn it isn't (`myTurn()`: combat's `currentTurn()` actor is the acting one, by identity or id); a character outside the encounter (fled, or never joined) never waits. Its line (`LIMITS`: "You've acted this round.", "Dived for cover: no attack this round.", "Not your turn: get ready, and act on your turn.") is shown under the flyout to everyone it applies to; for a player it disables the act buttons. A **spent** character: a player's `acted` or `dived` (`GREYED`), or the Keeper's `acted`, adds `rollbox-action-acted` to the flyout and `greySpent(root)` greys it: every element beside the act buttons' path from the root gets `rollbox-action-greyed` (opacity 0.55), never one that holds an act button, and the act buttons get `rollbox-action-greyed-button` on their own, because an ancestor's opacity would keep the Keeper's Override grey while hovered; the tabs, the warning, Override's question and the result area (`KEEP_CLEAR`) stay clear. Waiting for one's turn greys only the act buttons (disabled). The act buttons are `ACT_BUTTONS`: `.rollbox-action-go`, `-aim`, `-reload`, `-load-one`, `-cast`, `-cast-hidden`. For "dived" the Keeper sees only the line, and is not greyed; for "acted" the line reads "<name> acted this round." and `offerOverride(root)` marks every act button `rollbox-action-override`: while hovered it is ungreyed (`action.css`) and reads **Override** (its own words saved and put back on leaving), and a capturing click listener on the flyout stops the button's own press and shows the prompt (`prompt.js` `promptBox`, `rollbox-action-override-prompt`) "<name> has already acted this round. Override?" **Confirm · Cancel** above the line; Confirm lets that one press through (`overrideOnce`, then the button's own `click()`), Cancel closes it. The action then spends as usual, so the next press asks again. `limited` also goes to every tab in its context: Attack, Aim, Reload, Load one and fire, Cast, Cast hidden and the maneuver button refuse to send while it is set, and each tab keeps its own buttons greyed when it redraws them (an improvised weapon typed in, a Spell update), so a limited player can't send by changing a field.
- **Waiting for the Keeper**: `limit()` returns `"keeper"` first for a player while combat's `keeperDeciding()` is true (the scene's `firstBlow` mark: About to attack or the First blow card open). The whole flyout greys as for a spent character, its line and every act button's and weapon row's hover read "Waiting for the Keeper", the act buttons and every weapon row (Attack's and Maneuver's, `.rollbox-action-weapon`) are disabled, so no weapon can be picked or changed. Check and Other aren't touched; the Keeper is never limited. `sceneChanged` (hook `updateScene`, Rollbox's flags in the change) redraws every open flyout.
- **What a character can do**: `limit()` also returns `"down"` for anyone unconscious, dying or dead (`status/` `downReason`, CoC7's own conditions, the worst one), `"bout"` for a player in a bout of madness (CoC7's `tempoInsane`; the Keeper acts for them as usual), and on Attack and Spell `"restrained"` for anyone Rollbox marks Restrained (`status/` `isHeld`; Maneuver keeps Break free, maneuver.js). Order: waiting for the Keeper, down, bout, acted, restrained, dived, waiting for one's turn. `GREYED` limits grey the whole tab for a player, their act buttons disabled with the reason as hover (`playerWarning`: "Dying", "Restrained: you can only try to break free", …). The Keeper is held to `OVERRIDDEN` ones (acted, down, restrained) the same way with **Override**: the line reads `keeperWarning` ("Deep One is unconscious.", "Belinda is restrained: they can only try to break free.", "Cecil acted this round."), each act button carries its question in `data-override` (`overrideQuestion`: "Deep One is unconscious. Override?") and its reason as hover. A player never opens the flyout while down or in a bout: their whole menu is greyed by the menu host ([panel.md](panel.md)).
- **One Override for every limit** (`offerOverride`): on the Keeper's client every button with `data-override` in the flyout (the act buttons of an overridden limit, and a tab's own greyed button, such as the maneuver one out of reach or on a goal the character can't try) reads Override while hovered, and a press asks its own question in the flyout (before the warning line, or at the end with none); Confirm lets that one press through, Cancel changes nothing.
- **Connects to:** `status/index.js` (`downReason`, `hasStatus`, `isHeld`, `BOUT_REASON`); `panel/` (`registerFlyout`; the menu host offers Action with one or two selected); `player/index.js` (`panelHere`, `canUse`); `combat/index.js` (`currentTurn`, `hasActed`, `hasDived`, `inCombatFor`, `keeperDeciding`); `alert/index.js` (`DIVE_HOOK`); `flyouts/flyout.js` (`button`, `tabBar`); `target.js` (`currentTarget`, `onTargetChange`, `targetBox`, `targetToken` and the hooks); `weapons.js` (`tokenOf`, `weaponsFor`, `pickedWeapon`, `clearPickedWeapon`, `UNARMED`); `prompt.js` (`promptBox`); `maneuver.js` (`RESTRAINED_REASON`); `utils.js` (`isKeeper`, `isActiveKeeper`); the tab files.
- **Could interact poorly with:** other modules that also react to `targetToken` (none known). **Assumed.**

### `itemChanged(item)` (`index.js`, hook `updateItem`), `actorChanged(actor)` (`index.js`, private, hook `updateActor`), `combatChanged(combat, changes)` (`index.js`, hook `updateCombat`), `encounterChanged()` (`index.js`, hooks `createCombatant`, `deleteCombatant`, `deleteCombat`), `sceneChanged(scene, changes)` (`index.js`, hook `updateScene`), `targetUpdated(document)` (`target.js`, hooks `updateActor`, `createActiveEffect`, `deleteActiveEffect`) and `diveChanged()` (`target.js`, hook `DIVE_HOOK`)
- **How it works:** An open Action flyout redraws when something it shows changes elsewhere: the acting character's weapon (`updateItem`: CoC7 loaded a round, its sheet reloaded or emptied the gun, so the ammo, the empty-gun box and Reload follow), the acting character (`updateActor`: a round note such as Acted, cleared at a new round), the turn (`updateCombat` with `turn` or `round`: a player's act buttons wait for their turn and come back with it), who is in the encounter (a combatant added or removed, the encounter deleted: in combat or not follows it), Rollbox's flags on the scene (`updateScene`: the Keeper deciding the first blow, so a player's flyout locks or unlocks), the targeted character (a status from CoC7's token HUD, such as Prone, is an active effect and doesn't fire `updateActor`), or a target's dive decision. Each open flyout registers itself and drops out once its body has left the page.
- **Connects to:** Foundry's document hooks; `ActionFlyout#draw`.
- **Could interact poorly with:** a redraw while the Keeper is typing an improvised weapon (the text is kept in the tab's state). **Seen in e2e.**

### `tokenMoved(document, changes)` (`target.js`, hook `updateToken`) and `castingMoved(document, changes)` (`spell-text.js`, hook `updateToken`)
- **How it works:** a token update with `x` or `y` (Foundry saves the move on the drop, not during the drag; while the move animates, the document's `x`/`y` and `Token#center` still give the place before it, so `shared/distance.js` measures from the document's stored `_source` place and the new place is measured at once) tells the open flyouts which token moved (`{ moved }`); an `ActionFlyout` redraws only when it is the acting character's, the target's or the second selected character's token (`involves`), so the "N ft away" line, melee reach greying, point blank and firing into melee follow the board. `castingMoved` works the line beside CoC7's casting window out again (`noteCasting` takes a function for it). CoC7's chat cards keep the distance they were made with.
- **Connects to:** Foundry's `updateToken` hook (public); `ActionFlyout#draw`; `spell.js` (`targetLine`).
- **Could interact poorly with:** a token animated by another module after the update: Rollbox measures where the document's stored data says the token is. **Seen in e2e** (the Cultist moved and Cecil moved; Cecil dragged with the mouse, the distance and Punch's reach right 120 ms after the drop).

### `clearPickedOnCombatEnd(combat)` (`index.js`, hook `deleteCombat`)
- **How it works:** installed on every client with a panel, but does its work on the active Keeper's client only (utils' `isActiveKeeper`), so it runs once with several Keepers. For each combatant's actor, clears Rollbox's `pickedWeapon` flag (the picked weapon lasts until combat ends). Returns how many were cleared; a failure is one warning.
- **Connects to:** `weapons.js` (`clearPickedWeapon`).
- **Could interact poorly with:** combat's readied-firearms code reading the flag at the same moment; harmless (combat is over). **Assumed.**

### `targetBox({ actor, weapon, tab, onChange, fixed })`, `currentTarget()`, `onTargetChange`, `setTypedTarget`, `clearTarget`, `targetToken`, `targetChanged`; private `releaseTargets`, `activateTool`, `startTargeting`, `targetFacts`, `awayText`, `playerTarget`, `diveText`, `atPointBlank`, `statusBlock`, `effectText` (`target.js`; its hooks `targetUpdated`, `diveChanged`, `tokenMoved` are above)
- **Interface:** `currentTarget()` → `{ token, text }`: the user's first Foundry target (`game.user.targets`), or the typed text (a typed target wins and releases the targets). `targetBox` → the box element; its `dataset.net` holds the net dice from `attackDice()` for the Attack tab (the slider's dice), and `dataset.toggles` CoC7's own card toggles to set, comma separated (`cover`, `pointBlank`, `noPointBlank`, `inMelee`, `outnumbered`), which the Attack tab takes as `toggles`. `onTargetChange(listener)` returns the function that stops listening. With `fixed` (two selected: the target is the second character, and Swap changes it) the box has no Change or ×.
- **How it works:** **Target a token** for the Keeper switches Foundry's token controls to the target tool (`ui.controls.activate({ control: "tokens", tool: "target" })`), so the next click targets instead of selecting (the panel keeps the acting character); on the next `targetToken` from this user, the select tool comes back (`targetChanged`). **Already targeted = use it:** the box shows the user's first Foundry target as soon as there is one (`currentTarget`), so a player who has targeted someone isn't asked; **Target a token** (none yet) and **Change** ask a player through `player/targeting.js` `pickTarget({ prompt: "Select your target", useCurrent: false })` (Cancel puts their earlier targets back), then `targetToken` (`Token#setTarget(true, { releaseOthers: true })`). A typed target (on `change`) releases every Foundry target (`setTarget(false)`), so CoC7's card has no target (CoC7 asks "proceed without a target?" unless its world setting `disregardNoTargets` is on). `targetFacts`: distance edge to edge in the scene's units (`weapons.js` `distanceBetween`, shared's `edgeDistance`, shown by `awayText` with `distanceText`: "6 ft away"), Build `system.attribs.build.value`, POW `system.characteristics.pow.value`. CoC7's own attack card shows its own distance, centre to centre, so the two can differ by the tokens' half-widths. Statuses and situation (`statusBlock`): `status/` `attackDice({ attacker, target, weapon, pointBlank, intoMelee, outnumbered })` ([status.md](status.md)): `pointBlank` (a firearm) is `atPointBlank(actor, token)`, within a fifth of the attacker's DEX in feet edge to edge (Keeper Rulebook p. 113; `feetOf`), false when the distance or DEX can't be read; `intoMelee` (a firearm) is combat's `inMeleeWithOthers(token, actor)`; `outnumbered` (a melee weapon) is combat's `isOutnumbered(target)` ([combat.md](combat.md)). One row per line (`rollbox-action-effect rollbox-action-effect-<kind>`, `data-status`, the note as `title`): the line's name, and `effectText` ("Effect: 1 penalty die", the line's own effect such as "misses only on a fumble", or "none"). The net row (`rollbox-action-net`, "On CoC7's attack card" and every line's dice, `total`) only when a line has dice or sets a toggle other than `noPointBlank`. CoC7 itself sets range difficulty, the target's Build size dice, multiple shots and automatic fire on its card, so Rollbox doesn't; half concealment (Under cover) and a fast-moving target stay the Keeper's toggles on CoC7's card. A firearm at a target with a player owner adds the dive line from the alert's `diveDecision`.

- **How it works:** `spellBody` is the spell's `system.description.value` enriched by Foundry's `TextEditor.implementation.enrichHTML` (no secrets), else its plain text (an inert `template` element). **In the Spell tab** (`textOn` in `spell.js`): with "On hover" the row's `mouseenter` shows it and `mouseleave` hides it; "On click" a click on the row (not its buttons) shows it, again hides it (kept in the tab's state across redraws); "Off" nothing. `showSpellText` puts an `aside.rollbox-spell-text.rollbox-spell-text-beside` right after the flyout in the panel's row (so it sits on the flyout's far side and wears the theme) and removes it when the flyout is hidden or redrawn (a `MutationObserver`). **While casting:** `spell.js` calls `noteCasting` (the spell and "Target: <name>, <edge distance> away", or none without a token target) just before CoC7's cast, and clears it when CoC7's cast returns. `onCastingWindow` (`renderApplicationV2`) takes a window with CoC7's class `spell-variables` (`CoC7SpellVariablesDialog`, its "Spell Costs" window) opened within 15 s of the note, with `rollboxSpellTextCasting` on, and shows the text in Rollbox's own fixed box (`div.rollbox-panel.rollbox-spell-cast-host`, made once, `followTheme`) beside the window: to its right when there is room, else to its left, level with its top; it follows the window when dragged and empties when the window leaves the page or `closeApplicationV2` fires for it. CoC7's window is never changed; Rollbox works out no cost and blocks nothing.
- **Connects to:** `spell.js`, `panel/` `followTheme`, `shared/` `edgeDistance`; settings `rollboxSpellTextTab`, `rollboxSpellTextCasting` (section Spells).
- **Could interact poorly with:** a CoC7 update renaming the casting window's class or no longer awaiting it inside `system.cast` (then no text beside it; nothing breaks): **Assumed** (CoC7 8.15 source; registry). A cast from CoC7's own sheet gets no text: the window doesn't say which spell it is for. Other modules restyling Foundry's windows: the box is placed from the window's box on screen. **Confirmed** in e2e (Foundry v14 build 370, CoC7 8.15).

### `postReloadCard(actor, weapon)`; private `reloadText`, `pronoun` (`reload-card.js`)
- **How it works:** after CoC7 reloaded the weapon, `reloadText` reads the rounds now loaded and the capacity (`system.ammo`, `system.bullets`) and writes "Cecil reloads his .38 Revolver (6/6)." (his or her when the sheet's `system.infos.sex` starts with m/male/man/he or f/female/woman/she, else their). `postReloadCard` creates a chat message on the client that reloaded: `speaker` `ChatMessage.getSpeaker({ actor })`, `whisper` the Keepers (`getWhisperRecipients("GM")`) and the character's player owners (`ownersOf`), content `<div class="rollbox-reload-card"><p>…</p></div>` (escaped). Nothing is posted with no one to whisper to (a whisper to nobody would be public). A player's client makes it itself: they are one of its readers, so the relay isn't needed. The Keeper can share it with Foundry's own Reveal.
- **Connects to:** `attack.js` (Reload, New magazine, New belt, Load one and fire); `roll-requests/` (`ownersOf`); Foundry's `ChatMessage`.
- **Could interact poorly with:** modules that restyle whispered messages (plain text, no buttons). **Assumed.**

### `startAttack`, `keeperTicks`, `grantAutoHit`, `reloadWeapon`, `loadOneRound`, `castSpell`; private `autoRolls` (`roll-requests/attack.js`; all but `keeperTicks` re-exported by `roll-requests/index.js`)
- **`startAttack({ actor, weapon, ticks })`:** `actor.weaponCheck({ uuid }, false)` (CoC7 public method; reads `game.user.targets` and makes its melee or ranged card). Not used for maneuvers. The weapon is on the sheet: an unarmed weapon the sheet lacks is added first, only after the Keeper's yes (`roll-requests/unarmed.js`, [roll-requests.md](roll-requests.md)). No weapon: nothing made, `{ started: false, reason: "no weapon" }`; `weaponCheck` missing or throwing: `started: false` with the reason and one warning. Returns `{ started, rolled, reason }`.
- **Rolled at once** (`autoRolls(actor)`): on the client that makes the card, after the toggles, Rollbox presses the card's own roll button (the melee card's `attackerRoll`, the ranged card's active target's `range-initiator-roll`), as a person would: the Keeper's client for an NPC or creature with `rollboxAutoRollNpc`, for an investigator with `rollboxAutoRollAttacks`; a player's client for their investigator with their own `rollboxRollMyAttacks` (settings declared in `roll-requests/attack.js`, [roll-requests.md](roll-requests.md)). A multi-shot's bottom Shoot already rolls every shot. When a player's toggles are set by the Keeper's client, the player's client waits until it reads them on the card's flags (CoC7 leaves its Keeper-only block out of a player's copy), then presses Roll; it gives up quietly after 15 s.
- **Ticks** (Keeper-only controls on CoC7's cards): on the Keeper's client Rollbox watches `renderChatMessageHTML` for the attacker's card (`flags.CoC7.load.as` `CoC7ChatCombatRanged` with `actorUuid`, or `CoC7ChatCombatMelee` with `attackerUuid` and `participant` 0; the same weapon; not yet rolled) and sets **one toggle per render**, 250 ms after it (`CLICK_DELAY_MS`; CoC7 redraws the card after each change and reads its flags, so a second click on the old card would be lost): Aiming (`[data-action="toggleValue"][data-set="aiming"]`, ranged), Under cover (`.ranged-targets-option.active [data-action="toggleTargetKey"][data-set="cover"]`, ranged), Reloading (`.ranged-targets-option.active [data-action="toggleTargetKey"][data-set="reloading"]`, ranged), Point blank (`[data-set="pointBlankRange"]` on the active target, ranged: CoC7 already ticks it when it makes the card, measuring centre to centre with DEX / 15 yards, so Rollbox, measuring edge to edge, ticks it only when CoC7 didn't; for mace spray, `noPointBlank`, it takes CoC7's tick off, since mace spray never gets point blank, p. 406), In melee (`[data-set="inMelee"]` on the active target, ranged), Automatic success (`[data-action="toggleValue"][data-set="isAutoSuccess"]`, melee, for a surprised target with the Keeper's automatic hit; on a player's attack the Keeper's client sets it only when it granted it itself, `grantAutoHit`, never from what the player's client asks), Outnumbered (`[data-action="togglePoolKey"][data-set="outnumbered"]`, melee), the dice slider (ranged: the active target's `input[type=range][data-set="poolModifier"]`; melee: `input[type=range][name="poolModifier"]`) moved by the dice from where the card starts, within ±2, with a `change` event. A toggle already as wanted (`switched-on`, or off for `noPointBlank`) is skipped. **Shots** (`shotSteps`, `ticks.shots` 2 or more on a ranged card, at most 10): after the toggles, CoC7's Multi. Shots (`[data-action="toggleValue"][data-set="multipleShots"]`) until on, then the active target's Shoot (`[data-action="range-initiator-shoot"]`) once per render until the card's `flags.CoC7.load.shots` holds the count, then the bottom Shoot (`.ranged-shots [data-action="range-initiator-roll"]`) once; CoC7 rolls every shot, spends the rounds and offers Roll damage. While shots are wanted a ranged card with shots counts as the attacker's card (`isAttackCard`'s `withShots`). The relay passes every tick through (`cleanTicks`, shots too; Automatic success only from `grantAutoHit`). After 15 s: "Rollbox: set Aiming, … on CoC7's attack card before Roll." (or "Rollbox: press Roll on CoC7's attack card." when only the roll is left). On a player's client the Keeper's client is asked to watch, and to fire the shots too, when any Keeper-only toggle is wanted (`relay.js` "ticks"), so every shot is added after the toggles; shots alone the player's own client fires (CoC7 gives the card's owner those buttons); `keeperTicks` is the Keeper's side, only with the Player Rollbox on and for a character the player owns.
- **`reloadWeapon`, `loadOneRound`:** CoC7's `item.system.reload()` and `addAmmunition()`, what its sheet's ammo clicks do. **`castSpell(spell, { hidden })`:** `item.system.cast(hidden)`. Each guarded: missing or throwing → `false` and one warning.
- **Connects to:** `roll-requests/card-buttons.js` (`pressControl`, `CLICK_DELAY_MS`), `relay.js` (`askKeeper`); combat's About to attack (`grantAutoHit`); CoC7's attack cards (registry).
- **Could interact poorly with:** other modules clicking CoC7's attack cards. **Assumed.**

### `action.css`
- **How it works:** Listed in `module.json` `styles` after `shared/shared.css`, which gives the buttons, tabs, rows, text boxes, labelled lines, sections, the status line and the result line. This file keeps what only the Action flyout has: the tabs, the target box (name, facts, Change and ×, the status and situation lines with the name coloured by kind and the effect on the right, the net line, the dive line), the weapon rows (name, skill and range or damage, ammo, red when empty, values; the picked row's accent border, lighter background and detail lines), the improvised block, the in-combat Reload line (each button its words over what it costs), the "Attacks: 2/3" line, the maneuver goals (a grid, two rows of three; a greyed goal), the Set Restrained button and Build line (red when impossible), the spell rows and their buttons (the first-casting red outline, the pointer for "On click"), the spell's text box (beside the flyout, and in its own fixed host beside CoC7's casting window), the two-selected row ("Acting <name>" and Swap, `rollbox-action-pair`), the Keeper's prompt, and the greyed look of a spent character (`rollbox-action-greyed`, `rollbox-action-greyed-button`, and `rollbox-action-override` ungreyed while hovered). Every rule is scoped under `.rollbox-panel` and uses the panel's colour variables.
- **Connects to:** `panel/panel.css`, `shared/shared.css`; the theme stylesheets.
- **Could interact poorly with:** Foundry's global `button` and `input` styles: overridden under `.rollbox-panel` in the `modules` cascade layer (BUILD-GUIDE "The panel and CSS"). **Assumed.**

## Settings
Declared here, registered by `settings.js` in Gameplay: `rollboxMeleeReach`; in Spells (`spell-text.js`): `rollboxSpellTextTab` (On hover · On click · Off, default On hover) and `rollboxSpellTextCasting` (default on), both personal. `index.js` hands them to `settings.js` as `REACH_SETTINGS` and `SPELL_SETTINGS`. Read with `option()`. Whether an attack card is rolled at once follows `rollboxAutoRollNpc`, `rollboxAutoRollAttacks` and `rollboxRollMyAttacks` (declared in `roll-requests/attack.js`). A player's flyout follows `rollboxPlayerActions` through `canUse`.

## What Rollbox stores on actors
- `flags.keepers-rollbox.pickedWeapon`: the picked weapon's item id, or `"unarmed"`. Cleared when combat ends.
- `flags.keepers-rollbox.castSpells`: item ids of spells the character has cast (no more first-casting roll).
- On a firearm item, `flags.keepers-rollbox.reloadKind` (`magazine` | `rounds` | `belt`), saved the first time Rollbox reloads it when the kind is sure ([shared.md](shared.md)).
- Punch and Fighting items, added only after the Keeper's yes (with Maneuver on when added for a maneuver); they stay on the sheet. A weapon's Maneuver switched on (`system.properties.mnvr`) after the Keeper's yes; it stays on. Maneuver (Brawl) and Maneuver (Fighting) items on a sheet work as maneuver weapons.
- On a public chat message, the maneuver notice's `flags.keepers-rollbox.maneuver` (`roll-requests/maneuver.js`).
