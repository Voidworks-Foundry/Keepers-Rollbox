# Opposed roll (`scripts/flyouts/opposed/`)

The flyout the Keeper's Opposed button opens (offered with two characters selected, three or more, or nothing selected), and that a right click on a characteristic of the character sheet, or on a Check row or tile, opens with that side filled: beside the menu, like every flyout, while the menu stays and the character panel shows the characters; the view has no sheets of its own. Each side picks one check (Characteristic or Skill, with skills missing from the sheet at their base value) and up to two bonus or penalty dice; then Create opposed card makes CoC7's own opposed card through `roll-requests/`, which also clicks its Start rolls so an NPC side is rolled for the Keeper. Each player then rolls their own side on the card. Cancel closes the flyout; the "⇅ Swap sides" button between the two sides changes which character is CoC7's first side (attacker). The view builds no request text and rolls nothing itself. Registered on the Keeper's client, or a player's with the Player Rollbox on, with the Rollbox switch on; a player's Other flyout also draws it as its **Opposed** tab when the Keeper allows it ([other-flyout.md](other-flyout.md)), where the button reads **Make opposed card** (the card is still made by the Keeper's Rollbox, since CoC7 needs a GM for it). Files: `index.js` (registration and the open roll), `sides.js` (the model), `view.js` (the view), `opposed.css`. User guide: [Opposed](../guide/opposed.md).

**A side can wait for its character.** Started from one character's value, the other side reads, in red, **"Opposed roll started. Select another character."** (for a player, in their Opposed tab or when a right click on their sheet or a Check row opens it: "Opposed roll started. Target your opponent.", with a **Target your opponent** button). It fills from the next character selected (shift-click their token, or click it: the first side stays even when its character is deselected), from a Shift + Left click on another character's characteristic, which also makes the card (the panel's `fillsWaitingSide`, [panel.md](panel.md)), or from **⌖ Pick a target** in the side's header (Rollbox's target picker, Foundry's Target tool). With nothing selected both sides wait with "Pick who rolls this side." A filled side shows its name with a ✕ that empties it (a player's own side has none). **Several against one**: opened with three or more selected (and no starting side), they all go on the top side, the opponent is picked; such a side lists each character with its ✕ (`dropMember`: off the side, which becomes a side of one when one is left) and **⌖ Add a character**, uses one check for all (every characteristic and skill without values, as the Check flyout's for several), and **Create opposed cards (N)** makes one CoC7 opposed card per character against the one opponent, like Send to each. Started from a right click with three or more selected, the first side fills and the second waits. A side whose character is deselected empties again with the same line, and the flyout stays open (it is registered `staysOpen`). Shift + Right with one character: CoC7 8.15 can't finish a one-sided opposed card made through Rollbox's route (BUILD-GUIDE "Cards: opposed": `addActor` resets roles, the card compares only with one attacker and one defender, and joining a card needs `runRoll` with `C/OP`, which throws), so Opposed opens instead, with that side picked, and waits for the second.

**Status key:** *Confirmed* only when the build guide's Internal API registry or changelog says so. Everything is **assumed** unless stated otherwise.

## What the Keeper sees

In the flyout, under its title "Opposed roll", top to bottom:

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| The view | a block per side (or, for a side still waiting, its role tag and the red waiting line): a header with its role on CoC7's card ("Attacker" for the top block, in the accent colour; "Defender" for the bottom one, in blue), the name and the picked check in a small box ("STR 40", "Stealth 50 +1" with a bonus die; nothing until picked); tabs Characteristic / Skill, the list for the open tab (one pick, each row with its value), a search box on the Skill tab, Dice −2 … +2, and "Not on the sheet: CoC7 adds it at its base value when the card is made." when the pick is missing from that sheet; between the two blocks a larger "⇅ Swap sides" button with a short rule each side; then the footer: the tie line once both sides have a check (until then, in red, "Pick a check for both sides." or "Pick a check for Guard."), Cancel and Create opposed card (a player's: Make opposed card; highlighted, disabled until both sides have a check); then what happened. No tooltips, no "VS", no rules paragraph |

The sides start in selection order. CoC7's opposed card marks its first side attacker (crossed swords) and its second defender (shield) (`newGroupMessage`, `apps/chat-opposed-message.js`); the top block's character is the first side, and Swap sides lets the Keeper choose. Cards with no roles were wanted; CoC7 8.15 can't make one: it resets each side to no role as it adds it, then makes the first attacker and the second defender, and compares the rolls only when one side is each (with both at none the card shows a disabled "An opposed card needs an attacker and defender" instead of Start rolls). So the tags and Swap stay (BUILD-GUIDE "Cards: opposed"). Outside combat the roles don't change who wins. There is no difficulty (the other side's roll is the difficulty, p. 92) and no Push (p. 90). Sanity isn't offered: a Sanity roll is never opposed. There is no Send to chat or Copy link (Rollbox copies no links; an opposed link clicked with tokens selected would make one card per token anyway).

## Code

### Files
- **How it works:** `index.js` registers the flyout and keeps the open roll (`carried`: the sides, the selection they were drawn for, and the view drawn last), with `renderOpposedRoll`, `opposedStart` and `opposedWaiting`; `sides.js` is the model (plain data and skill-values' rows: the sides, how they start and follow the selection, each side's rows, `whoRolls`, `tieText`, `missingText`); `view.js` draws it (`OpposedView`). `index.js` imports the panel (`registerFlyout`); the panel imports nothing from here: Opposed hands it `opposedStart` and `opposedWaiting` through `registerFlyout`'s `start` and `waiting`.
- **Connects to:** `panel/` (`registerFlyout`). **Could interact poorly with:** nothing. **Assumed.**

### `registerOpposedRoll()` (`index.js`, called from `main.js` at `ready`)
- **How it works:** Returns `false` unless `player/` `panelHere()`: the Keeper's client with `rollboxEnabled` on, or a player's with the Player Rollbox and their own switch on. Otherwise forgets any carried sides and calls `registerFlyout("opposed", { title: "Opposed roll", render: renderOpposedRoll, staysOpen: true, start: opposedStart, waiting: opposedWaiting })`: the menu host draws it in the flyout beside the menu, like every flyout ([panel.md](panel.md), `registerFlyout`), and keeps it open (and lets `open("opposed", …)` reach it) while anything is selected, even when its button isn't offered, so a deselected side waits instead of the flyout closing. Installs no hooks.
- **Connects to:** `panel/` (`registerFlyout`); `player/index.js` (`panelHere`).
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing. **Assumed.**

### `renderOpposedRoll(stage, { actors, everyone, close, selectionChanged, start, targeting, pickTarget })` (`index.js`) and `OpposedView` (`view.js`)
- **How it works:** The menu host calls it when the flyout opens and again when the selection changes while it is open; a player's Other flyout calls it for its Opposed tab (with `targeting`). `targeting` defaults to true for a non-GM user (`game.user.isGM !== true`), so a player's Opposed opened by the menu host (a right click on their sheet or a Check row) targets too. Nothing selected draws nothing. Each call builds the view from scratch, but the **sides** live in a module-level `carried` state: new when the flyout opens (`selectionChanged` false), kept across redraws for a new selection. Returns a controller `{ apply(start) }`, which the menu host keeps while the flyout is open.
  - **A side** is `{ actor, group, picked, anchored, tab, pick, dice, search }`; `actor` null is a side waiting for its character; `group` the other characters of a side of several (`members(side)` is `[actor, ...group]`; `withMembers` makes a side of none, one or several, picking the check again when it changes between one and several); `picked` the characters put there with the picker (kept when the selection changes). `anchored` marks the character the roll was started from (`start.first`). `renderOpposedRoll` takes `everyone` (the Keeper's players list: nothing selected) as no characters.
  - **Opened** (`freshSides`): with `start.first`, that character is the top side (CoC7's first, attacker), anchored, with its pick; with exactly two selected the other is the second side, otherwise the second waits. Without `start.first`, the two selected characters in selection order (the Opposed button); one: it with the second side waiting; three or more: all on the top side (several against one); none: both waiting.
  - **A new selection** (`refreshSides`): a side of several becomes everyone selected who isn't on the other side, plus those added with the picker; a side of one whose character is no longer selected empties, unless it is anchored or picked; then each empty side, top first, takes the other of exactly two selected characters, or else a character newly added to the selection. So with three or more selected the second side waits until the Keeper adds the opponent, a swap and the picks survive a selection change, and two other characters selected after the Opposed button replace both (unanchored) sides.
  - **Started from a value** (`start = { first, picks, create }`, built by `opposedStart`, or by a Check row's opposed shortcut): `picks` maps each character's `actorKey` to a row key ("characteristic:str"); `startPick(actor, picks)` finds that row in the side's `checkChoices` and opens its tab with it picked. `create` (Shift) calls `create()` at once, which makes the card only when both sides have a character and a check: a one-sided card is never made.
  - **A waiting side** (`waitingBlock`): the role tag and the red line (`rollbox-opposed-waiting`): the Keeper's reads "Opposed roll started. Select another character." while the other side has a character, else "Pick who rolls this side.", with **⌖ Pick a target** in the header (`target(index)`: `pickTarget({ prompt: "Target who rolls this side", useCurrent: false })`; the side is marked `picked`, so it stays when the selection changes); with `targeting` (a player's Opposed), the line "Opposed roll started. Target your opponent." and a **Target your opponent** button (`target(index)`, label `TARGET_PROMPT`) that awaits `pickTarget({ prompt: "Target your opponent", useCurrent })` (default `player/targeting.js`; a token the player has already targeted is used without asking unless its character is already a side) and puts the token's character on that side; a cancel (null), a failure (logged), or a character already on a side changes nothing. Create opposed card is greyed out; the footer's line stays empty (the waiting line says it).
  - **A player's opponent** (`isOpponent`, `opponentBlock`; [DESIGN.md, "Opposed roll"](DESIGN.md#opposed-roll)): with `targeting`, a side whose character isn't the player's own (`own`) is drawn as `.rollbox-opposed-side-opponent`: the label, the name with its ✕ and the hint "Your Keeper picks what <name> rolls." (`rollbox-opposed-keeper-picks`), and nothing else: no tabs, list, values or dice, so an NPC's sheet is never shown. The labels read **You** and **Opponent** (`roleText`, `PLAYER_ROLES`) instead of Attacker and Defender, which are set on CoC7's card once the Keeper adds the opponent (the player Attacker, roll-requests `setRolesWhenJoined`); Swap sides is hidden. `update()` names only the player's own missing check and never writes the tie line. `requests()` goes to `playerRequests()`: the player's side with its check and dice, the opponent's `{ actor, open: true }` (roll-requests' `opposedRequest`), only once both sides have a character and the own side a check. After the card is made the result adds "Your Keeper adds <name>'s side and starts the rolls." (`rollbox-opposed-keeper-adds`).
  - **No sheets:** the view is only `.rollbox-opposed-card` in the flyout's body; the character panel already shows the characters ([panel.md](panel.md)).
  - **Lists** (`drawList`, `row`, `filter`): each side's rows are `checkChoices(actor)` from `skill-values/`, the Check flyout's rows, under the shared `CHECK_TABS`: STR … EDU; skills trained highest first, the divider "Untrained · base value", then the rest A–Z at base value. Each row is a button (`role="option"`) showing the name and the value (opposed rolls compare at Regular, so no Hard or Extreme values). A row missing from the sheet has no tooltip ([DESIGN.md, "Flyouts"](DESIGN.md#flyouts): no tooltips inside flyouts); the side's own note says it once picked. The search box is the shared `filterRows`: it hides skill rows whose name doesn't hold the text (accents and case ignored) and the divider while searching; its text stays when switching tabs and across redraws.
  - **Picks:** one per side (the picked `checkChoices` row), kept when switching that side's tab; the picked row has `aria-selected="true"`. Each side's check is `{ kind: "characteristic", name: "str" }` or `{ kind: "skill", name, cocid }`, as `roll-requests/` takes it.
  - **Dice** (`diceRow`): a row of buttons −2, −1, None, +1, +2 per side (Keeper Rulebook p. 91: at most two either way), the shared `segmented` row in the shared `diceLine` ("± Dice", hover "Bonus (+) or penalty (−) dice"), labelled "<name>: bonus or penalty dice" for screen readers.
  - **Built before the first redraw:** `build()` makes both sides (`sideBlock`, split into `head`, `tabs` (the shared `tabBar`), `search` (the shared `searchBox`, labelled "Search <name>'s skills"), `diceRow`, or `waitingBlock`), the swap block and the footer, then draws each side's list and calls `update()` once. A tab click draws that side's list and calls `update()`; a swap, a side filled or a starting state redraws the whole view (`redraw`).
  - **Header pick:** `update()` writes the side's pick and dice into its header box ("STR 40", "STR 40 +1"), with an `aria-label` spelling the dice out ("STR 40, 1 bonus die"); hidden until a pick.
  - **Who rolls** (`whoRolls`): from roll-requests' `ownersOf` (CoC7's `actor.owners`): no owner → "Keeper rolls (NPC), by Start rolls"; owners, none online (`User#active`) → "Keeper rolls (player offline), by Start rolls"; otherwise "<players online> rolls on the card"; owners unreadable → nothing. CoC7's Start rolls rolls every side with no player owner online (`rollNoPlayers`, `apps/chat-opposed-message.js`), so the first two are rolled when Rollbox clicks it. It is shown only in the result after Create.
  - **Create:** Create opposed card is disabled until both sides have a pick, while a card is being made (no double cards), and once the card is made until a side's character, check or dice changes (`madeNow`: the picks when the card was made, `madeKey`, against `picksKey()`, each side's character uuid, pick key and dice; `create()` also refuses then), so the same card can't be made twice by a second click. The footer is the shared `sendSection` ([flyouts.md](flyouts.md)), Cancel then Create (its words "Create opposed card" on the Keeper's client, "Make opposed card" on a player's, `isKeeper`), with Cancel marked `data-keep` so it is never greyed out, and the tie line as its status line (`rollbox-opposed-tie`). The title is the flyout's own ("Opposed roll").
- **Right click**: a right click on a side's name (`head`), or on a name in a group side (`groupNames`), targets that character's token (shared's `rightClickTargets`).
- **Connects to:** `skill-values/` (`checkChoices`, `groupSkills`, `skillKey`); `panel/` (`registerFlyout`); `player/index.js` (`panelHere`, `pickTarget`, `targetedToken`); the Other flyout (a player's Opposed tab); `roll-requests/` (`opposedRequest`, `send`, `ownersOf`); `shared/` (`rightClickTargets`); `flyouts/flyout.js` (`CHECK_TABS`, `actorTag`, `button`, `glyphButton`, `diceChoices`, `diceLine`, `diceText`, `filterRows`, `noneSent`, `runSend`, `searchBox`, `segmented`, `sendSection`, `showResult`, `tabBar`); `utils.js` (`NO_VALUE`, `actorKey`, `uniqueActors`, `element`, `isKeeper`, `warn`).
- **Could interact poorly with:** Foundry's global `button` and `input` styles: every rule is scoped under `.rollbox-panel`, in Foundry's `modules` cascade layer (BUILD-GUIDE "The panel and CSS"). **Assumed.** A change to a character while the view is open (e.g. HP lost) is drawn on the character panel; the view itself is redrawn only when the selection changes. **Assumed.** The Keeper's picker switches Foundry's tool to Target and leaves the picked token targeted (as Action's target box does). **Confirmed** in e2e.

### `OpposedView#apply(start)`, `#findActor(key)`
- **How it works:** A starting state while the view is open (the menu host's `open("opposed", start)` reaches it through the controller): each character in `picks` (found among the selected characters and the sides, `findActor`) gets that check on its own side if it has one, else on an empty side, else on the side the roll wasn't started from; the character named `first` becomes anchored. The view is drawn again, and with `create` the card is made when both sides have a check. This is how a shift-click on another character's value fills the waiting side.
- **Connects to:** `opposedStart`; the panel's `openOpposed` and the Check flyout's row shortcut.
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing. **Assumed.**

### `opposedStart(actor, key, { create, first })` and `opposedWaiting()` (`index.js`, exported)
- **How it works:** `opposedStart` builds the starting state for a value clicked on the character sheet: `{ first: actorKey(actor), picks: { [actorKey(actor)]: key }, create }`, without `first` when `first: false` (a shift-click filling the other side). `opposedWaiting()` is true while the view drawn last is still in the page and a side waits for its character, so the panel sends a Shift + Left click on a characteristic to Opposed instead of the Check flyout (`fillsWaitingSide`).
- **Connects to:** `panel/index.js` (`onValue`, `fillsWaitingSide`, `openOpposed`; [panel.md](panel.md)).
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing (no Foundry calls). **Assumed.**

### The sides (`sides.js`): `emptySide()`, `sideFor(actor, picks, anchored)`, `withMembers(side, list)`, `members(side)`, `isGroup(side)`, `sameCharacter(a, b)`, `onSide(side, actor)`, `known(value)`, `choicesFor(side)`, `startPick(actor, picks)`, `freshSides(actors, start)`, `refreshSides(state, actors)`, `whoRolls(actor)`
- **How it works:** The model the view draws, plain data. `emptySide()` is a side waiting for its character (`{ actor: null, group: [], picked: [], anchored: false, tab, pick: null, dice: 0, search: "" }`); `sideFor` a side for one character, `anchored` when the roll was started from it, with its starting pick (`startPick`: the row key in `picks` for the character's `actorKey`, found in `checkChoices` under the shared `CHECK_TABS`, with that tab). `withMembers` gives the side for a list of characters (none: empty; the first is its character, the rest its `group`), picking the check again when it changes between one and several or to another character, and keeping only the `picked` keys still on it. `members` is `[actor, ...group]`, `isGroup` a side of several, `sameCharacter` compares by `actorKey`, `onSide` whether a character is on a side, `known` a value that isn't null or undefined. `choicesFor`: one character's `checkChoices` (with values); a side of several, every characteristic and every skill A–Z without values (`groupSkills`, keys from `skillKey`), as the Check flyout's for several. `freshSides` and `refreshSides` are the opening and new-selection rules above. `whoRolls` is described under "Who rolls" above.
- **Connects to:** `skill-values/` (`checkChoices`, `groupSkills`, `skillKey`); `roll-requests/` (`ownersOf`); `utils.js` (`actorKey`, `uniqueActors`); `flyouts/flyout.js` (`CHECK_TABS`).
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing (no Foundry calls but CoC7's `actor.owners` through `ownersOf`, and Foundry's `User#active`). **Assumed.**

### `missingText(sides)` (`sides.js`)
- **How it works:** While Create opposed card is greyed out with both characters there, the footer's line (the shared `sendSection`'s `ready()`) says what is missing, in red: "Pick a check for both sides.", "Pick a check for <name>." or, for a side of several, "Pick a check for the group." On a player's flyout only their own side is checked (the opponent's check is the Keeper's). Once both sides have a check, the line becomes the tie line (`tieText`, written with the status line's `note()`). Every flyout's send buttons stay greyed out until everything required is filled in, with the missing item named.
- **Connects to:** `update()`; the shared status-line style ([shared.md](shared.md)).
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing (pure text). **Confirmed** by tests only.

### `tieText(sides)` (`sides.js`)
- **How it works:** Keeper Rulebook p. 91: on equal success levels the higher skill or characteristic wins, and if those are equal too it's a stalemate or both re-roll. CoC7's opposed card shows only "Tie" outside combat (BUILD-GUIDE Gap 7), so the view shows, in the footer above the buttons (the shared `statusLine`, `note()`: plain, not red; it is worked-out information, not a summary of what will be sent), "On a Tie: Guard wins, higher value (Spot Hidden 60 vs Stealth 50, p. 91)." or "On a Tie: equal values (50), so a stalemate or both re-roll (p. 91)." It compares the two values only, never dice or success levels: CoC7 still does all the comparing on its card. Empty when a value is unknown.
- **Connects to:** the picks' values from `skill-values/`. Never on a player's flyout: it would show the opponent's value.
- **Could interact poorly with:** a player character's missing skill is shown at Rollbox's base value; CoC7 adds it from its compendium at CoC7's base, which matched Rollbox's table for every skill ([skill-values.md](skill-values.md)). **Assumed.** CoC7's house rule `opposedRollTieBreaker` only changes the order of combat cards, not this. **Assumed.**

### Creating the card (`OpposedView#requests`, `#create`, `#showResult`)
- **How it works:** `requests()` is one `opposedRequest({ sides: [{ actor, check, dice }, …] })`, or with a side of several one per character of that side against the other side's character (the same check and dice for each). Create → `send(request, [])` for each, the results added together (each character once); `send()` makes one CoC7 opposed card with both sides at Regular difficulty, clicks Start rolls on the Keeper's screen (a player's card goes through the Keeper's Rollbox, [roll-requests.md](roll-requests.md), "The Keeper relay"), and checks first that CoC7 can find any skill missing from a sheet (CoC7 adds it to that sheet when it makes the card, NPCs too, by design). [roll-requests.md](roll-requests.md), "Opposed". The result line then shows:
  - a card made (`showResult` with its own heading and lines, [flyouts.md](flyouts.md)): "Opposed card made:" ("Opposed cards made:" for several), then one line per side, "Alice: Test rolls on the card" and "Guard: Keeper rolls (NPC), by Start rolls". Cancel now reads Close; the view stays open so the tie line is at hand.
  - no card: "Not sent:" with each of `send()`'s `skipped` reasons, e.g. "Guard: CoC7 can't find Pilot (Boat)" (also shown by `send()` as a notification), or the reason alone for the whole card, in the accent colour (`rollbox-opposed-warning`); or "CoC7's roll links aren't available; nothing was sent."
- **Connects to:** `roll-requests/index.js` `send()` and `sendOpposed`.
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing beyond `send()`'s own notes (CoC7 needs one side attacker and the other defender, which it sets itself; if Start rolls can't be clicked the Keeper is told to click it). **Assumed.**

### Cancel
- **How it works:** Calls the host's `close()`: the flyout closes (in a player's Other flyout, Other closes); the menu and the character panel were there all along. Nothing is sent. Deselecting everything closes it too (the host's rule); any other selection keeps it open (`staysOpen`).
- **Connects to:** `panel/menu-host.js`.
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing. **Assumed.**

### Swap sides (`OpposedView#swapBlock`, `#swap`)
- **How it works:** A larger "⇅ Swap sides" button between the two sides. Reverses the two sides, each keeping its character, tab, pick, dice and search text (a waiting side moves too), and draws the whole view again: the top block reads "Attacker", and Create opposed card sends the new top character as CoC7's first side (its roll string first, and the actor `_onLinkActorClick` is called with). Disabled while a card is being made. Nothing is sent. A result line from an earlier card is cleared; Close stays Close.
- **Connects to:** `roll-requests/` `sendOpposed` (side order).
- **Could interact poorly with:** a selection change while the view is open keeps the sides (a swap survives it) unless a side's character leaves the selection. **Assumed.** In combat CoC7's attacker and defender matter, but Rollbox never makes combat cards (`combat: false`). **Assumed.**

### `opposed.css`
- **How it works:** Listed in `module.json` `styles` after `shared/shared.css`, which gives the tabs, dice buttons, Cancel and Create buttons, the search box, the divider, the hint, the Dice line and the result line their look. This file keeps what only the Opposed roll has. Uses the panel's colour variables from `panel.css`; every class starts `rollbox-opposed-` and every rule is scoped under `.rollbox-panel`. The view is a column in the flyout (scrolling past 70% of the window height like every flyout); each side's list scrolls past about 120px. The Defender tag is the theme's blue (`--rollbox-defender`), the Attacker tag the accent colour; the Swap button is larger than the other buttons. A waiting side's line (`rollbox-opposed-waiting`) is the theme's red (`--rollbox-bad`, as the status line's missing text), and a player's Target your opponent button (`rollbox-opposed-target`) centres its crosshair beside the words. The Keeper's Pick a target (`rollbox-opposed-pick-target`) and Add a character (`rollbox-opposed-add`) are small buttons with the crosshair; the ✕ (`rollbox-opposed-clear`) is a small round button that turns red on hover; a side of several lists its characters (`rollbox-opposed-group`, `-member`). The name doesn't stretch, so the ✕ sits right after it and the picked check at the header's right end. Nothing inside the card, a side or a list may shrink (`flex-shrink: 0`): the rows are buttons with `min-height: 0`, and in a column flex box that hits its max-height they would shrink below their text and overlap.
- **Connects to:** `panel/panel.css` (`.rollbox-flyout`), `shared/shared.css`.
- **Could interact poorly with:** Foundry's light interface theme: the view follows Rollbox's own Theme setting, not Foundry's. **Assumed.**
