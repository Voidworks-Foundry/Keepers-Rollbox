# Panel (`scripts/panel/`)

The Rollbox panel, beside Foundry's players list (the latency and FPS card). Its character panel (always 200px wide at 100%) shows the character sheet for one selected character, the two-character sheet for two, and the character cards for three or more; with nothing selected the Keeper's lists each player's investigator (the players list); another part can draw it instead (combat's turn order). Beside it, the menu: Check and Other always, Action with one or two selected, and Opposed above them with any selection but one, the players list included (Sanity is a tab of Check, Luck and Assist tabs of Other). On the character sheet each characteristic, SAN and Luck is a button that opens its flyout, with CoC7's sheet shortcuts; one character's status cards sit above the sheet. Its look follows the personal Theme (Foundry, Art Deco or CoC7 minimal; Foundry follows Foundry's own Dark or Light), Panel colour, transparency and size settings, and its place among Foundry's windows the Panel layer setting. With "Allow moving the panel" on (the default) the Keeper can drag it anywhere by a card header. Rollbox takes no keys: every control is out of the Tab order and the panel listens to no keyboard event outside its own text boxes. It runs on the Keeper's client, and on a player's when the Keeper gives players a panel (`player/`, [player.md](player.md)), each only with the user's own switch on. Files: `index.js` (hooks, selection, the players list and the no-investigator message, the settings' onChange entry points), `appearance.js` (theme, Panel colour, transparency and size applied to the panel and to anything that wears its look), `auto-hide.js` (the `AutoHide` class), `position.js` (where the panel sits, dragging it, the menu's side), `layer.js` (the Panel layer), `placement.js` (the arithmetic of placing it), `theme.js` (the themes, which one can show, and the colour maths), `themes/` (the Art Deco and CoC7 minimal stylesheets, and `coc7.js`: CoC7's image URLs and their check), `sheet.js` (the character sheet, for one or two characters), `cards.js` (the character cards and the players list, with their vertical bars), `status-cards.js` (the status cards above the sheet), `menu-host.js` (menu, flyout, `registerFlyout`), `sheet-clicks.js` (what a click on a sheet value or a Check row means), `panel.css` (the panel and the Foundry theme's Dark and Light palettes), `position.css` (the menu and flyouts turned left or down), `sheet-values.css` (the values as buttons), `settings.js`.

User guide: [Getting started](../guide/getting-started.md) (the panel and its menu), [Shortcuts](../guide/shortcuts.md) (clicks on the sheet's values), [Appearance](../guide/appearance.md) (theme, colour, transparency, size, moving, layer, auto-hide).

**Status key:** *Confirmed* only when the [Internal API registry](DEVELOPING.md#internal-api-registry) says so or the maintainer checked it at the table, as marked on the entry; everything else is **assumed**.

## Code

### `registerPanel()` (`index.js`, called from `main.js` at `ready`)
- **How it works:** First takes down what an earlier call set up (`tearDown`): the hooks it installed (kept as `[name, id]` with the `Hooks` they were installed on, taken off with `Hooks.off`), a pending frame, auto-hide, the old panel's `PanelPlace#destroy()` and its root, and the window's `resize` and `keydown` listeners; then every piece of the part's state is back to `freshState()` (one `state` object: the panel, the frame, the shown characters, the players list flag, the card key, the content and idle providers, auto-hide, active, selecting, the closed message, the hooks). So calling it twice installs everything once. Then returns `false` without a `game.user`, and unless this is the Keeper's client or the Keeper has given players a panel (`player/` `playerRollboxOn()`) and the user's own `rollboxEnabled` is on (read guarded, `appearance.js` `readSetting`). The Keeper test comes first, so a player with the Player Rollbox off reads nothing else and runs no panel code. Otherwise resets `appearance.js` (`resetAppearance`), installs its hooks ("Hooks, overrides, keybindings", below) and the window's `resize` and `keydown` (Escape) listeners, asks for a redraw and returns `true`. That first redraw draws whatever applies at `ready`: on the Keeper's client the players list (nothing is selected yet) or nothing; on a player's client their own investigator, or the no-investigator message ("A player's panel", below). The panel's DOM is built the first time it shows.
- **Connects to:** `utils.js` (`isKeeper`); `appearance.js` (`readSetting`, `resetAppearance`); `player/` (`playerRollboxOn`); setting `rollboxEnabled` (user scope).
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing at start-up. **Assumed.**

### Selection and redraw (`scheduleRedraw`, `selectedActors`, `redraw`, `onActorUpdate`, `onTokenUpdate`, `MOVE_KEYS`, `index.js`)
- **How it works:** `controlToken`, `createToken`, `deleteToken`, `deleteActor`, `canvasReady`, `updateActor` (for a shown character) and `updateToken` (`onTokenUpdate(token, changes)`, private: for a shown token's character, since an unlinked token's character changes with its token, through its actor delta; an update whose changed keys are only `_id`, `x` and `y`, `MOVE_KEYS`, is a token moving and redraws nothing, since nothing the panel draws depends on where a token stands; anything else goes on to `onActorUpdate(token.actor)`) only ask for a redraw on the next animation frame (`requestAnimationFrame`); further calls before it runs are absorbed, so a box-select that fires the hook once per token draws once. The redraw reads `canvas.tokens.controlled`, takes each token's `actor`, leaves out tokens without one and keeps each character once (by uuid, so two tokens of one linked actor show once; utils' `sameActor` decides whether a changed actor is a shown one; unlinked tokens have their own actors and show separately), in selection order. On a player's client the selection is always their own character (`playerActors`, below). None: another part's content when one is set (`setPanelContent`, below); else on the Keeper's client the players list ("The players list", below) while there is an investigator to list; else, on a player's client, the no-investigator message; else the panel hides and any open flyout closes. One: the character sheet. Two: the two-character sheet (`pairSheet`, below). Three or more: the character cards, drawn over an invisible character sheet of the first character (`sheetGhost`, below). No upper limit. The menu host gets the characters first (below; with `everyone` while the players list shows), then the card is drawn (`drawCard`), so the character cards' columns match the flyout that is open after the change. A guarded read: if the canvas or its token layer is missing, nothing is selected.
- **Connects to:** `sheet.js`, `cards.js`, `menu-host.js`; `player/` (`investigators`); Foundry's `canvas.tokens.controlled`.
- **Could interact poorly with:** modules that control tokens in bulk (e.g. selecting every token of a folder): still one redraw per frame. **Assumed.** `updateActor` from another user's change while a flyout is open: the card is redrawn, the flyout is not (the selection didn't change). **Assumed.**

### `setPanelContent(provider, { idleActors })`, `clearPanelContent()`, `refreshPanel()` (`index.js`)
- **With nothing selected:** while a provider is set the panel still shows (combat's turn order stays visible); the menu then acts for the characters `idleActors()` returns (combat: the current turn's character on the Keeper's client), or for nobody (an empty menu). The provider is called with `(actors, { onValue })`: `onValue` is the panel's own value-click handler, so a sheet the provider draws works like the panel's (for the character the menu acts for; another character's sheet says "select <name> to roll for them"). `refreshPanel()` asks for a redraw (a Player Rollbox feature changed); nothing on a client without a panel.
- **How it works:** Lets another part (combat's turn order) draw the character panel instead of the character sheet, two-character sheet or character cards, without editing the panel's files. `setPanelContent(provider)` keeps the function, redraws the showing panel at once and asks for a full redraw (the panel may now show); it returns `false` and changes nothing for anything that isn't a function. On every redraw while the panel shows, `drawCard` calls `provider(actors)` with the shown characters: an element it returns replaces the character panel's content; `null`, anything that isn't an element, or a throw (logged with `warn`) draws the panel's own content for that redraw. A flyout's column change doesn't redraw provided content (only a new selection or an actor update does). The menu and flyouts are unaffected. `clearPanelContent()` drops the provider and redraws the panel's own content. `registerPanel()` clears it.
- **Connects to:** `combat/index.js`, which sets `combat/turn-order.js`' `turnOrderContent` while an encounter runs and clears it otherwise ([combat.md](combat.md)); `drawCard`; `utils.js` (`tellKeeper`, for "select <name> to roll for them").
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing. **Confirmed** by tests only.

### `statusCards(actor)`, `placeStatusCards(container, actor)`, `statusIcon(id)`, `STATUS_ICONS` (`status-cards.js`)
- **How it works:** `statusCards` returns `div.rollbox-status-cards` with one `div.rollbox-status-card` per status (`getStatuses`, `status/index.js`), or `null` when there are none. Top to bottom: Rollbox's own states, the others, the serious ones nearest the sheet (the reverse of `getStatuses`). Each card has `rollbox-status-<kind>` (`serious`, `other`, `rollbox`) for its colour, `data-status`, its icon (`statusIcon`: the status board's 24×24 stroke path in the card's text colour), its name, "Rollbox" on Rollbox's own, on the Keeper's client a small × on a state Rollbox set itself (`ROLLBOX_STATUSES`, Restrained: `clearButton`, `.rollbox-status-clear`, aria-label "Clear Restrained"; a click calls `status/` `setRollboxStatus(actor, id, false)` and the panel redraws as the character changes), an `aria-label` "Name: meaning", and a hover box (`.rollbox-status-tip`, role `tooltip`) with the name in bold and what it means (no "set by" line). `placeStatusCards` puts the stack first in the container. `drawCard` (`index.js`) calls it for one shown character only (never on the two-character sheet, the character cards or combat rows), which includes a player's own character on their panel; combat's turn order calls `statusCards` itself for the current turn's full sheet (`combat/turn-order.js`). The stack is drawn above the character panel, out of its layout (`status/status.css`: the character panel is `position: relative` while it holds one, the stack `position: absolute; bottom: calc(100% + 4px)`), so the sheet, the menu and the flyouts keep their size and place; the hover box opens on the menu's side (left with `rollbox-menus-left`). Text only, never HTML.
- **Connects to:** `status/` (`getStatuses`, `setRollboxStatus`, `ROLLBOX_STATUSES`, [status.md](status.md)); `drawCard`; `combat/turn-order.js` (through `index.js`); `status/status.css` (the × too).
- **Could interact poorly with:** a panel near the top of the screen: the stack grows up past it, and the panel's keep-in-view doesn't measure it. **Assumed.** Text set by CoC7 or Foundry statuses isn't shown: the names and meanings are Rollbox's. **Assumed.**

### `attach()`, `onPlayersRendered()`, `keepInView()`, `onResize()` (`index.js`)
- **How it works:** All of them ask `PanelPlace#place()` (below) to put the panel where it belongs. `attach()` runs before the panel shows on a redraw, and leaves a panel that is already placed and watched alone (`PanelPlace#inPlace`), so a redraw reads no layout before it draws; `onPlayersRendered` (the `renderPlayers` hook: Foundry redrew its players list, e.g. someone logged in or out) places it (always: Foundry may have rebuilt the list) and, while the players list shows, asks for a redraw so its "offline" marks follow; after showing it, `redraw()` calls `PanelPlace#redrawn()`, which places it again only where nothing watches its size (no `ResizeObserver`: the tests' jsdom); in Foundry the size watcher (`watchSize`, below) places it when the redraw changed the panel's, its menu's or its flyout's size, and not otherwise (placing right after each redraw would force a layout every time). `keepInView()` runs when a flyout opens, closes or says its columns changed (the menu host's `onCardChange`), when auto-hide changes the card, and on the window's `resize` event (one listener, added by `registerPanel()` wherever the panel is set up), so a moved panel stays on screen and an open flyout is shifted into view.
- **Connects to:** `position.js`; Foundry's `renderPlayers` hook; the browser's `resize` event.
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing known. **Assumed.**

### `PanelPlace`, `MENU_SIDES`, `DEFAULT_MENU_SIDE` (`position.js`)
- **How it works:** `MENU_SIDES` are the choices of "Menu and flyouts open to the" (`auto` Automatic, `right` Right, `left` Left; re-exported from `index.js` for the settings) and `DEFAULT_MENU_SIDE` is `auto`. One `PanelPlace` per panel, built with the saved `rollboxMovable`, `rollboxOffset` (the spot), `rollboxMenuSide` and `rollboxPanelLayer` (`layerInUse`, `layer.js`).
  - **Beside the players list** (`dockBeside()`, `watch()`; the default place, Keeper and players): with moving off, or with no spot, the panel goes into Foundry's `#interface`, absolutely positioned at the Panel layer's level (`zIndexFor`; Bottom is the left column's level, under every window), never inside Foundry's left column. `placement.js` `besideList` puts its bottom edge as far from the screen's bottom as the players list's bottom edge, and its left edge that same distance to the right of the list. The list's box is `listRect`: `#players` together with its children (build 370: `#players-active` and `#players-inactive` are 200px wide inside a narrower `#players`), so the panel sits beside what is drawn, not over it. The list grows upwards as players join, so the panel doesn't move up; it follows the list's width through a `ResizeObserver` on `#players`, the `renderPlayers` hook and the window's `resize`. It is scaled from its bottom-left corner by Panel size times `--ui-scale` (`scaleTransform`), with the menu opening to the right. If `#players` is missing it falls back to `dock()`.
  - **Fallback** (`dock()`): only when Foundry's `#interface` or `#players` is missing: a sibling just before `#players` in `#ui-left-column-1` (not inside `#players`: Foundry replaces its contents on each render). If `#players` is missing too, the panel isn't shown and one warning is logged.
  - **Moved** (`float()`, `setSpot()`): the panel is appended to Foundry's `#interface` (build 370: the element holding the UI columns, `position: relative`, never drawn again by Foundry, with no stacking layer of its own, and narrowed by the camera dock, which Foundry puts beside it) and positioned **inline**: `position: absolute`, `z-index` from the Panel layer (`zIndexFor`, below), `margin: 0`, and `left`/`right` and `top`/`bottom` from the spot's corner. The transform is inline too: `scale(calc(var(--ui-scale) * size))` from that corner (`scaleTransform`), because outside the column the panel doesn't get Foundry's UI scale from it. Nothing about where it is depends on a stylesheet having loaded.
  - **The spot** is `{corner, x, y}`: the corner of the screen quarter the card was dropped in and its distance from that corner (`placement.js`). Measuring from the near corner keeps the panel near the same edges when the window is resized and lets a taller card grow away from the edge; the drop itself doesn't move the panel (no snapping). A spot is clamped to the screen each time it is placed (`clampSpot`), but the saved spot isn't changed, so the panel goes back when the window grows again.
  - **Checks:** a spot that can't be read (`normalSpot`: no known corner, not two finite numbers, below 0) is ignored. When the panel is first drawn at a saved spot, a spot that would put it off the screen is ignored (`onScreen`). After every placement the panel's own box is compared with where it was put (`samePlace`, 2px); if it isn't there, its layout failed, and it goes back beside the players list. Each ignores the spot for the session and logs one warning; the saved value stays. The position is inline and the landing checked because Foundry reads `module.json` (and so its `styles`) only when a world launches: a position that depended on a stylesheet could be missing after an install, leaving the panel off screen with that spot saved.
  - **Menus' side and direction** (`mark()`): beside the players list, the flyouts open to the right and grow up. Moved: `rollbox-menus-left` when "Menu and flyouts open to the" is Left, or Automatic on the right half; `rollbox-grow-down` on the top half. `position.css` does the rest. `mark()` also sets the Panel size's transform (`scaleTransform`): moved, from the anchored corner; beside the players list (`dockBeside` calls it again with `left bottom`), from the bottom-left corner, both times `--ui-scale`; only in the fallback `dock()` (inside the left column, which gives Foundry's UI scale) is it the Panel size alone, and none at 100%.
  - **Following the sidebar** (`sidebarReach`, `watchSidebar`): a panel dropped on the right half keeps its distance from Foundry's sidebar (`#sidebar`, `SIDEBAR_ID`). On release the spot also saves `reach`: how far the sidebar reached in from the screen's right edge then (`sidebarReach`: the area's right edge minus the sidebar's left edge; null with no sidebar or one of no width). `float()` places the spot `placement.js` `followSidebar(spot, reachNow)` gives, so the panel moves out when the sidebar expands and back when it collapses; a right-side spot saved without `reach` takes the sidebar's reach the first time it is placed. A `ResizeObserver` on the sidebar (`watchSidebar`, made once) places the panel again while it sits on the right half and is shown. Overlapping the chat is allowed: only the distance is kept.
  - **Keeping an open flyout in view** (`inView()`): after placing a moved panel, the boxes of the panel, `.rollbox-side` (menu and flyout) and `.rollbox-menu` (whose extra buttons can reach past the side's box when it grows down) are measured together (`partsBox`, the box around those that are drawn, also used by `shiftIntoView` beside the players list; `unionOf` is the box around a list of rects, also `listRect`'s); if they run past an edge, the panel is shifted just far enough (the card itself kept on screen) without changing the saved spot, so it goes back when the flyout closes.
  - **Dragging** (`pressed`, `move`, `lift`, `release`): only with moving allowed, only the main button, only on a header (`HANDLE`: a character sheet's header in the character panel, either one on the two-character sheet, or the character cards' "N selected" row), never on a button (the open-sheet button), or **anywhere on the combat box** (`COMBAT_BOX`, combat's `.rollbox-turn-order` in the character panel): its rows, full card and buttons included, only a text box (`NO_DRAG`) keeps its press (`dragsFrom`). The press listens to `pointermove`, `pointerup` and `pointercancel` on the document until it ends. Under 4px of movement it is a click and changes nothing. Past it, `lift()` moves the panel into `#interface` exactly where it is on screen (anchored top-left while dragging), checks it landed there (otherwise the drag is called off and the panel goes back beside the players list), and adds `rollbox-dragging`. Each move clamps the card's box to the screen and switches the flyouts' side at once as the panel's centre crosses the screen's middle (`dragSide`: Automatic follows the half, Right or Left stay as chosen), as it will be on release. After a drag the browser's click on what was pressed is swallowed (`swallowClick`: one capture-phase `click` listener on the document, removed on the next tick if no click comes), so a drag never selects a row or presses a button. On release the spot is measured from the quarter's corner (`spotOf`), with the sidebar's `reach` on the right half, the panel is placed and the spot saved to `rollboxOffset`.
  - **The wheel while held** (`wheel`; Keeper and players): from the press (`pressed`) to its release, a capturing `wheel` listener on the window (`{ capture: true, passive: false }`, removed in `cancel`) takes every wheel turn: `preventDefault` and `stopImmediatePropagation` (Foundry's map zoom listens on the window, bubbling, so it never sees it), then one Panel size step (`onWheelScale`, `appearance.js` `stepScale`: ±10 within 80–250, up is bigger; applied at once, not saved). Lifted for a drag, the panel scales from the corner it would be anchored to if dropped there (`spotOf(press.box)`'s corner): the drag's box is resized and moved so that corner stays put, and kept on screen (`clampBox`); not lifted yet, from its own corner and placed again. On release the new size is saved (`onScaleDone`, `saveScale`: `rollboxScale`, as Configure Settings saves it), and a press that only scaled swallows its click. Without a press the wheel is untouched.
  - **Placed only when a size changes** (`watchSize(nodes)`, `redrawn()`, `inPlace()`): `buildPanel` hands it the panel, its side, menu and flyout; a `ResizeObserver` on them places the panel (`place()`) whenever one changes size. Its callback runs after the browser's layout and before paint, so the panel is never seen out of place, and a redraw that changes no size reads no layout. `redrawn()` places it only without the observer; `inPlace()` (in `#interface`, floating, watched) lets `attach()` skip placing before a redraw.
  - `setMovable(on)` (the setting's onChange): the spot is forgotten and the panel goes back beside the players list. `reset()` (Reset position): the same, moving left on. `setMenuSide(side)`: placed again with the new side. `setLayer(layer)` (Panel layer's onChange): placed again at once at the new level.
  - **Top when active** (`toFront()`): a `pointerdown` on the panel (a capture listener, so a button that redraws the panel still counts) takes the next number from Foundry's window counter (`nextFront`, `layer.js`) and sets it as the panel's `z-index`, only while the panel is in `#interface` (beside the list or moved).
  - `behind(event)`, Top when active: a capture `pointerdown` on the document. A press on a Foundry window (`.application`, `.window-app`) whose `z-index` is at or under the panel's sets the panel to that number, so the window is drawn on top. Foundry's `bringToFront` doesn't give a new number to the window that is already its front window, so without this a press on the window that was in front before the panel would leave it under the panel. Reads the window's `z-index` only; never changes it.
  - `destroy()`: removes the document's `pointerdown` listener (`behind`, added on the panel's own document), ends a press (`cancel`) and disconnects the `ResizeObserver`s (players list, sidebar, sizes). `index.js` `tearDown` calls it when `registerPanel` runs again; the panel's own listeners go with its root.
  - **Exported helpers:** `scaleTransform(root, origin)` (the Panel size's transform, times `--ui-scale` in `#interface`, from the anchored corner; none at 100% in the fallback dock), `rescale(root)` (the same again after a size change), `forgetSpot()` (saves `rollboxOffset` as `{}`; a failure is one warning). `saveSpot` (private) saves a dropped spot the same way.
- **Connects to:** `placement.js`; `layer.js`; `index.js`; settings `rollboxMovable`, `rollboxOffset` (client scope), `rollboxMenuSide` and `rollboxPanelLayer`; `game.settings.set`; Foundry's `#players` and its children, `#interface`, `#sidebar`, `--ui-scale` (build 370); the browser's `ResizeObserver`.
- **Could interact poorly with:** modules or themes that restyle, move or replace `#interface`, `#players` or the left column: the panel follows `#players` beside the list and `#interface` when moved; if a moved panel doesn't land where it is put, it goes back beside the list. **Assumed.** With the Bottom layer, Foundry windows (`--z-index-window`) and the token HUD draw above a moved panel, like the UI columns. **Assumed.** A module that moves or restyles Foundry's `#sidebar`: a right-side panel follows its left edge, whatever it is. **Assumed.** Beside the players list the panel doesn't follow anything that moves or hides Foundry's left column as a whole (a module that collapses or animates `#ui-left`); it follows the list's size and the window. A module that adds something wide inside `#players` moves the panel right with it (`listRect` takes every child). **Assumed.** CoC7 adds the world era inside `#players`; Rollbox doesn't touch it. **Assumed.** **Confirmed** at the table, with the panel docked above the players list rather than beside it: docked with moving off; drag and drop in every quarter, the flyouts' side and direction, reload and a smaller window, Menu and flyouts open to the, Opposed and Assist on the right, Panel size 150% at the top right, Reset position, dragging from the Opposed sheets, menus kept on screen, players see nothing.

### `placement.js`
- **How it works:** Plain arithmetic, no Foundry or DOM: `clampBox` (keep a box on screen; a box larger than the screen keeps its top-left on it), `onScreen`, `samePlace` (2px), `nearestCorner` (by which half the centre is in, across and down), `spotOf` and `boxAt` (a box's spot from its quarter's corner, and back), `clampSpot` (a spot moved the least to stay on a smaller screen, from the same corner), `normalSpot` (a saved spot, or null; a valid `reach`, a finite number 0 or more, is kept), `cornerSides`, `followSidebar(spot, reach)` (a right-side spot with its `reach` moved across by how much further, or less far, the sidebar reaches now, never past 0; a left-side spot, or either reach unknown, stays as it is), `besideList(list, area)` (the default place: `left` and `bottom` inside the area, the list's distance from the area's bottom used as both the bottom margin and the gap to the list's right, never below 0).
- **Connects to:** `position.js`.
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing. **Confirmed** by the tests.

### `onTearDown()` (`index.js`, the `canvasTearDown` hook)
- **How it works:** On a scene change Foundry clears the selection without firing `controlToken`, so the panel hides at once and any pending redraw is cancelled. `canvasReady` then draws whatever is selected on the new scene (normally nothing).
- **Connects to:** Foundry's `Canvas#tearDown` (build 370, `canvasTearDown`).
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing known. **Assumed.**

### Panel layer: `PANEL_LAYERS`, `DEFAULT_PANEL_LAYER`, `normalLayer`, `layerInUse`, `forwardOnSelect`, `zIndexFor`, `nextFront` (`layer.js`); `applyLayer(layer)`, `noteSelection`, `bringForward` (`index.js`)
- **How it works:** The setting `rollboxPanelLayer`: **Bottom** (`bottom`), **Top** (`top`), **Top when active** (`active`, the default) or **Top when active or selected** (`activeOrSelected`). `normalLayer` reads any other value as the default. The levels come from Foundry's own variables (build 370 `foundry2.css`): Bottom is `var(--z-index-app, 30)`, the UI columns' level, under every window (`.application` is `--z-index-window`, 100, and each window brought forward gets a higher number); Top is `calc(var(--z-index-tooltip, 9999) - 3)`, above every window and under the tooltip (9999), the tour overlays (tooltip − 2 and − 1) and notifications (99999), while right-click menus (popovers) and modal dialogs (`showModal`) are in the browser's top layer, above everything. Top when active starts at `var(--z-index-window, 100)` and, on each press on the panel, takes `++ApplicationV2._maxZ` (`nextFront`, capped at 99999 as Foundry caps its older windows), the counter Foundry's `ApplicationV2#bringToFront` uses, so it interleaves with real windows: the panel goes in front when pressed, and a window clicked later goes in front of it. `layerInUse` turns Top when active into Bottom when `foundry.applications.api.ApplicationV2._maxZ` isn't a finite number (an internal of Foundry's: guarded, one warning). The panel is in `#interface` at every layer (`PanelPlace`, above); the layer sets only its `z-index`. The flyouts are inside the panel, so they follow its layer. `applyLayer` (the setting's onChange) applies it at once, no reload. **Top when active or selected** stacks exactly as Top when active (`layerInUse` returns `active` for it, so `position.js` treats them alike), and the panel also comes forward whenever the Keeper selects tokens: `forwardOnSelect(layer)` is true only for it and only while Foundry's counter is there; the panel's `controlToken` hook notes a selection (`noteSelection`, a token controlled, not released), and the next redraw, after placing the panel, calls `PanelPlace#toFront()` (`bringForward`, `index.js`), exactly what a press on the panel does. An actor update redraws without bringing it forward. Without the counter it works as Bottom, like Top when active.
- **Connects to:** `position.js`; `settings.js`; Foundry's `foundry.applications.api.ApplicationV2._maxZ` (internal, registry) and its `--z-index-app`, `--z-index-window`, `--z-index-tooltip` variables.
- **Could interact poorly with:** a module that raises windows by its own counter instead of Foundry's: with Top when active the panel may stay under (or over) those windows until pressed. **Assumed.** A very long game session in which Foundry's counter passes 9996: windows raised after that are above a Top panel, as they are above Foundry's own tooltips. **Assumed.** Foundry's own UI (sidebar, scene controls, token HUD) is in the columns' layer or the canvas, so a floating panel at Top or Top when active is above it where they overlap. **Assumed.**

### `pairSheet(actors, { onValue, onDeselect })` and `pairOrder(actors)` (`sheet.js`)
- **How it works:** Two selected: one character sheet with both characters. `pairOrder` puts a player character (one with a player owner, roll-requests' `ownersOf`) on top and an NPC at the bottom; two of a kind A–Z by name (`localeCompare`, case and accents ignored). Top: the first character's header (portrait, name, "occupation · owner", open-sheet button), then HP/MP and SAN/Luck. Middle: one set of the eight characteristics; each tile (`.rollbox-pair-tile`) shows its label, the top character's value, a thin line (`.rollbox-pair-rule`) and the bottom character's value. Bottom: the second character's HP/MP and SAN/Luck, in the same order as the top's, then its header. Everything of the bottom character (its header, its stats block and its eight values) carries `rollbox-second`, drawn in tan (`--rollbox-second`), so the two are told apart. On the panel's own sheet each characteristic number is a button (aria-label "Check STR, Harvey"), and SAN and Luck are buttons per character ("Sanity, Harvey", "Luck, Harvey"); `onValue(event, value, right, actor)` gets the character whose number was clicked, so the shortcuts are the one-character sheet's (`sheet-clicks.js`) and a right click starts Opposed with that character's side. A flyout opened this way is for the selection, as from the menu. The headers are drag handles, as on the one-character sheet. With `onDeselect` (the panel's own sheet: `drawCard` passes `deselect`) each header's portrait is a deselect button (`deselectButton`): while hovered, a × in a circle the portrait's size, and a click deselects that character. HP and MP open nothing and don't light up on hover (in every theme); the characteristic numbers light up in every theme.
  - **Right click**: each character's header and HP/MP · SAN/Luck block targets that character's token (shared's `rightClickTargets`); the shared characteristics belong to no one character, and a value's own right click stays its own (Opposed).
- **Connects to:** as `characterSheet`; `drawCard`, `onValue` and `deselect` (`index.js`).
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing known. **Assumed.**

### `characterSheet(actor, { onValue, onDeselect })` (`sheet.js`)
- **How it works:** One character: portrait (`actor.img`, or Foundry's mystery-man), name, occupation (`system.infos.occupation`) and owner joined by " · " (an occupation-less character shows only the owner; an NPC shows "NPC", e.g. "Steward · NPC"; this line is 10px and has its full text as its tooltip, `title`), and a button that opens the full sheet (`actor.sheet.render(true)`, guarded). HP and MP as value/max with a bar; SAN with a bar against its max (99 if the sheet has none); Luck with a bar against 99; the eight characteristics, four to a row, in sheet order. Values come from `skill-values/` (`characteristics`, and `attributes` found by key, `lck` and `san`, never by their order) and `system.attribs.hp` / `.mp`; a missing value shows "—" (`NO_VALUE`, `utils.js`). All text is set with `textContent`, so a name with HTML in it shows as typed. A name too long for the card is cut off with "…", so the full name is also its hover tooltip (`title`), here and on the character cards (`nameLine`). With `onDeselect` (the Keeper's panel with one selected, `drawCard`) the portrait is the deselect button (`deselectButton`): hovering the header shows the same round ✕ as on the two-character sheet and the character cards, and a click deselects the character, leaving the nothing-selected view. A player's sheet and the turn order's sheets have none.
  - **Clickable values** (`onValue`): given `onValue` (the panel's own sheet, `drawCard`), each characteristic, SAN and Luck is a `<button type="button">` (`cellOf`, `valueButton`) instead of a `<div>`, looking like a plain cell (`sheet-values.css`), with `aria-label` "Check STR" … "Check EDU", "Sanity" and "Luck", `data-value` (the characteristic's key, or `san` / `luck`) and no tooltip. A click calls `onValue(event, value, false)` and a right click (`contextmenu`, whose browser menu is prevented) `onValue(event, value, true)`, with `value` `{ kind: "characteristic", key }`, `{ kind: "san" }` or `{ kind: "luck" }`. HP and MP stay plain. Without `onValue` (the invisible `sheetGhost`) nothing is clickable. An exception to the design's rule ([DESIGN.md, "Panel"](DESIGN.md#panel)) that the cards show information only: nothing is sent from the card; the flyout that opens sends.
  - **Parts only the CoC7 themes show** (`levelsOf`, `statValue`, `derivedLine`): each characteristic's value and its half and fifth share one wrapper (`.rollbox-characteristic-numbers`, `display: contents` in the Foundry theme, so the cell lays out as a plain value), the half and fifth in `.rollbox-characteristic-levels` (from skill-values' `characteristics`, rounded down); HP and MP's "12/12" is split into `.rollbox-stat-now` and `.rollbox-stat-of` ("/12"), so its text is unchanged; every stat gets a `.rollbox-stat-band` with its maximum (HP and MP's max, SAN's max or 99, Luck 99; a dash with none), and the sheet ends with `.rollbox-derived`: MOV, Build and DB from `system.attribs.mov`, `.build`, `.db` (`value`; a dash when missing). `panel.css` hides the levels, the band and the derived line; Art Deco shows the levels and the band, CoC7 minimal the levels and the derived line. They carry `aria-hidden`, so a screen reader reads the same sheet in every theme. Only on the one-character sheet (and its copies: the turn order's full sheet, the cards' ghost); the two-character sheet's tiles are unchanged.
  - **Right click targets**: a right click anywhere on the sheet targets the character's token (shared's `rightClickTargets`: the token the character stands for, `tokenFor`; Foundry's `Token#setTarget(true, { releaseOthers: true })`), the browser's menu kept away. A value's right click (Opposed) stops at the value (`valueButton`), so it doesn't also target. The turn order's full card is this sheet, so it targets the same way. The HP, MP, SAN and Luck lines under the values are 2.6px tall (the Foundry theme's; the CoC7 themes draw their own blocks).
- **Connects to:** `skill-values/index.js`; `roll-requests/index.js` (`ownersOf`); shared's `rightClickTargets`; CoC7's data paths ([DEVELOPING.md, "Skills and weapons"](DEVELOPING.md#skills-and-weapons)); `onValue` in `index.js` (below).
- **Could interact poorly with:** CoC7 sheets that hide characteristics or attributes, or creatures without HP/MP/SAN: the missing values show as dashes. **Assumed.** A sheet that can't render (the sheet class missing or throwing): one warning (`warn()`, `utils.js`), nothing else. **Assumed.**

### `characterCards(actors, columns, { onDeselect, onPick })`, `meters(actor)`, `METER_CHOICES`, private `METERS`, `shownMeters`, `verticalBar` (`cards.js`); `deselectButton(actor, className, onDeselect)` (`sheet.js`); `deselect(actor)`, `selectOnly(actor)` (`index.js`)
- **Meters**: `METERS` are the four bars, HP, MP (each against its maximum), SAN (against starting SAN) and Luck (out of 99); `shownMeters()` gives those ticked in the personal setting `rollboxCardMeters` (a list of `METER_CHOICES` keys, read as a `Set` from Foundry; every meter when it can't be read; none when none is ticked). The character cards' default columns, the players list and `meters(actor)` (the turn order's rows) use them. The heading row of a bar card has the rows' side padding (4px, `panel.css`), so each heading sits centred over its bar (e2e, three themes). The setting's `onChange` redraws the panel.
- **How it works:** Three or more characters: a header ("N selected" and one heading per value column) and one character card each with a small portrait, name, "occupation · owner" ("NPC" for a character with no player owner; the line's full text is its tooltip) and the column values. Information only (every control lives in the open flyout), with one exception: the portrait is a button (`sheet.js` `deselectButton`, shared with the two-character sheet's headers: `.rollbox-deselect`, aria-label "Deselect Harvey"; without `onDeselect` it is the plain portrait); while the card is hovered the portrait gives way to a small × (`panel.css`), and a click calls `onDeselect(actor)`. The panel's `deselect` (`index.js`) releases every controlled token of that character with Foundry's public `Token#release()` (a throw is one warning); its `controlToken` hook redraws the panel and an open flyout with the smaller selection; the ×'s click stops there, so it never also reaches the card. **A click elsewhere on a card** (`onPick`, `rollbox-character-card-pick`, a pointer cursor) selects only that character: the panel's `selectOnly` keeps that character's selected tokens with Foundry's public `Token#control({ releaseOthers: true })` (the first; any other token of theirs with `releaseOthers: false`), so every other token is released, and the panel redraws for them alone. **A right click on a card** targets that character's token (shared's `rightClickTargets`; the selection stays). The columns follow the open flyout: by default the ticked meters as vertical bars (`shownMeters()`: HP, MP, SAN and Luck unless the personal setting leaves some out; Luck is there for the group Luck roll, the lowest Luck rolls, p. 89-90), with no flyout and with Check (any tab, Sanity included), Opposed or Other on any tab but Assist; with Other on its Assist tab, Assist's own column (`otherColumns` in `flyouts/other/index.js`, which returns Assist's `assistColumns`, [assist.md](assist.md): "STR" or "SIZ", the Use value for participants and the Against value for targets). A column is `{ label, value(actor), className? }` (a flyout's, drawn as a plain number) or `{ label, bar(actor), className }` (a default one, drawn by `verticalBar`); cells get `rollbox-cards-value` plus the column's class (`rollbox-cards-hp`, `rollbox-cards-mp`, `rollbox-cards-san`, `rollbox-cards-luck`). A value that is missing, or a column that throws, shows "—". **`verticalBar({ value, max })`**: a bordered column (`.rollbox-vbar`) filled from the bottom (`.rollbox-vbar-fill`, its `height` the value's share of the maximum, 0 to 100%; full when there is no maximum, empty with no value), the number inside (`.rollbox-vbar-value`, white with a shadow; a dash with no value). HP and MP are read against their maximums (`system.attribs.hp`, `.mp`), SAN against starting SAN, which is POW (Keeper Rulebook p. 34; 99 without a POW), Luck against 99. A card with any bar column gets `rollbox-cards-bars`, which narrows the columns to the bars' width, `--rollbox-meter-width` (17.6px, every theme; `panel.css`); the bars fit the row's height, so ten rows still show before the list scrolls. **`meters(actor)`**: the same ticked bars side by side (`.rollbox-meters`) without a heading row, each bar's hover text naming it ("HP 12"); the turn order's rows use it ([combat.md](combat.md)). Names run right up to the first value column: each column is `--rollbox-col`, `calc(1.8 * var(--font-size-12, 12px))`, just wide enough for three digits (SAN 99, a STR of 100) in tabular figures at the cards' 12px font, and the name takes all the room before it, cut with "…" only where it meets it. The header grid has one such track per column; a longer heading ("STR/SIZ") reaches left into the title's room. The list shows ten rows before it scrolls, sized by rows, not pixels: `--rollbox-row-height` on the list is a row's padding plus its two lines at a fixed line height (`calc(6px + 1.25 × (--font-size-12 + --font-size-10))`, so it follows Foundry's font size), every row is exactly that tall, and the list's `max-height` is `10 × --rollbox-row-height + 9 × --rollbox-row-gap`.
- **Connects to:** as `characterSheet`; `MenuHost.columns` and `drawCard` (`index.js`) choose the columns; `playersList` (below) uses the same rows and bars; combat's `turn-order.js` (`meters`); shared's `rightClickTargets`; Foundry's `canvas.tokens.controlled`, `Token#release()` (`deselect`) and `Token#control()` (`selectOnly`). Auto-hide also draws them, without `onDeselect`, for one or two characters (below).
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing known. **Assumed.**

- **How it works:** The Keeper's panel with nothing selected, while the personal setting `rollboxShowPlayers` ("Show the players when nothing is selected", on by default; `showsPlayers`, read guarded) is on and no other part's content is set (during an encounter combat's turn order shows instead). The entries are `player/` `investigators()` ([player.md](player.md)): each non-GM user's assigned character (`User#character`) that they own, once each, in Foundry's user order, with the first player it is assigned to. Text only (`textContent`).
- **Connects to:** `player/` (`investigators`); `cards.js`' rows and bars; `menu-host.js` (`everyone`); Foundry's `User#character`, `User#active`, `Actor#getActiveTokens`, `Token#control`; `panel.css` (`.rollbox-players-row`, `.rollbox-players-away`, `.rollbox-players-none`).
- **Could interact poorly with:** a character assigned to a player who doesn't own it isn't listed (`investigators` keeps only owned characters), so "Send to all players" leaves it out. **Assumed.** An unlinked investigator: a row's click selects the first token Foundry finds for the actor. **Assumed.** A player connected twice, or reconnecting: "offline" follows Foundry's `User#active`. **Assumed.**

### `registerFlyout(id, { title, render, staysOpen, columns, start, waiting })`, `flyoutStart(id, ...args)`, `flyoutWaiting(id)` (`menu-host.js`; `registerFlyout` re-exported from `index.js`)
- **How it works:** How each flyout part plugs in: `id` is `action`, `check`, `other` or `opposed`; `title` replaces the flyout's default heading; `render(body, { actors, everyone, close, selectionChanged, start, setTitle, cardChanged, open })` fills the flyout's body, which is emptied first. It is called when the flyout opens and again when the selection changes while it is open (so it must build from scratch), but not when only a shown character's data changes. `actors` is a copy of the selected characters (every listed investigator while the players list shows, with `everyone` true: the flyout acts for all of them, "Send to all players"); `close()` closes the flyout; `selectionChanged` is false when the flyout opens and true when it is drawn again for a new selection, so a flyout can carry its own choices over (Assist keeps its toggles, number, targets and label while tokens are shift-clicked); `cardChanged()` asks the panel to draw the character cards again, for a flyout whose `columns` changed. `start` is the starting state the flyout was opened with (`MenuHost#open(id, start)`, a click on the character sheet), or undefined; `setTitle(text)` changes the flyout's heading and `aria-label` while the flyout is open (the Other flyout's "Item check" / "Luck check"). `open(id, start)` is the host's own `open`, so a flyout can open another (a Check row's Right click opens Opposed). `render` may return a controller `{ apply(start) }`: the host keeps it while the flyout is open and hands it a later starting state (Check, Other, Opposed and Action do). `columns(actors)` (Other on its Assist tab): the character cards' value columns while the flyout is open (see "Character cards columns" below). `staysOpen` (Opposed): the flyout stays open while anything is selected, even when the selection no longer offers its button, and `open(id, start)` opens it then (a right click with one or three-plus selected; its empty side waits); no button is then shown pressed, and the button comes back pressed when it is offered again. With nothing selected (the players list included) it closes like any flyout. Every flyout, Opposed included, opens beside the menu: there is no other layout. Registering an id again replaces the earlier flyout. An unknown id or a missing `render` is refused with a warning (returns `false`). A `render` that throws leaves the flyout with its title and logs one warning.
- **Start and waiting:** a flyout may hand the panel `start` (builds its starting state for a value clicked on the sheet) and `waiting` (whether it waits for a value to be clicked); the panel reads them with `flyoutStart(id, …)` (null when not registered or none) and `flyoutWaiting(id)` (false then). Opposed hands over `opposedStart` and `opposedWaiting`, so the panel imports no flyout and no import cycle runs between them.
- **Connects to:** every flyout part, which calls it from their own `register…()`.
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing outside Rollbox. **Assumed.**

### Menu buttons' height and place (`panel.css`, `position.css`)
- **How it works:** One fixed height, set in CSS and never by a script: `--rollbox-button-height` on `.rollbox-panel` is worked out from two constants, `calc((var(--rollbox-sheet-height) - 2 * var(--rollbox-button-gap)) / 3)`, so three buttons and the two gaps between them equal the one-character sheet at 100% Panel size (`--rollbox-sheet-height`, 182px, measured in Chromium with Foundry's own stylesheet and fonts; `--rollbox-button-gap`, 6px, which the menu's `gap` also uses): 56.67px. The menu is aligned to the character panel's bottom, so the fixed buttons always fill the bottom 182px (Check and Other, with Action above them when it shows) and the extra button reaches up above them; a taller two-character sheet, card list or flyout never stretches or squishes them (`flex: none`). When the menu grows down (a moved panel in the top half), it is aligned to the character panel's top and pulled up by `--rollbox-extras` (how many extras, set by `MenuHost#update`) buttons and gaps (`position.css`), so Action, Check and Other still sit where they always do and the extra button reaches above the card.
- **Connects to:** `.rollbox-menu-button { height: var(--rollbox-button-height); flex: none }`, `.rollbox-menu { gap: var(--rollbox-button-gap) }`; the Panel size scales them with the rest of the panel.
- **Could interact poorly with:** a Foundry font or font size that makes the character sheet taller or shorter than 182px: the three buttons then no longer exactly match it (they keep their size). **Assumed** until a look check.

### Character cards columns (`drawCard`, `index.js`; `MenuHost.columns`, `menu-host.js`)
- **How it works:** `redraw()` sets the shown characters, updates the menu host, then calls `drawCard(true)`. `drawCard` draws the character sheet for one character and the two-character sheet for two (a flyout opening or closing doesn't redraw either); for three or more it asks `MenuHost.columns(actors)`: the open flyout's own `columns` (Other on its Assist tab), or null for the default meters (`shownMeters()`). A flyout `columns` function that throws gives the default with one warning. The host calls `drawCard()` (its `onCardChange`) when a flyout opens or closes, and whenever a flyout calls its render context's `cardChanged()` (Other does when its tab changes, and Assist on every change). Without `force`, the cards are drawn again only when the characters or the column headings or values changed, so typing in a flyout doesn't rebuild them or reset their scroll.
- **Connects to:** `characterCards`, `MenuHost`, `registerFlyout`'s `columns`.
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing outside Rollbox. **Assumed.**

### `MenuHost` (`menu-host.js`)
- **How it works:** `new MenuHost(side, onCardChange)` owns the menu (`.rollbox-menu`) and the flyout, side by side in `.rollbox-side`. `update(actors, ids, { everyone })` redraws the buttons for the selection, extras first (above), and sets `--rollbox-extras`; with `everyone` (the players list) it counts as nothing selected: Check and Other, with the Opposed extra above them, and `staysOpen` doesn't keep a flyout open without its button; a change of `everyone` counts as a new selection; with `ids` (a player's panel, `player/` `playerMenu()`) it draws only those of the fixed three, in order, with no extras; an open flyout stays open while its button is still offered, or while anything is selected for a `staysOpen` flyout (`offers`), and is redrawn only if the selection changed (with `selectionChanged: true`; utils' `sameActor`, so a token's actor made again counts as the same character); otherwise it closes. Clicking a button opens its flyout beside the menu and sets the button's `aria-pressed="true"`; clicking the same button again closes it; clicking another switches. The menu stays visible whatever is open. Until a flyout is registered its flyout shows only its title.
  - **`open(id, start)`**: opens a flyout with a starting state, from a click on the character sheet. Returns `false` when the flyout isn't offered for this selection (`offers`: its button is shown, or it is `staysOpen` with anything selected). When that flyout is already open and its render returned a controller, the state goes to `controller.apply(start)` (so the Keeper's options stay; a throw is one warning) and the card is told; otherwise the flyout is drawn from scratch with `start` in its context. `close()` and every redraw forget the controller.
  - **The flyout's heading** (`syncHeading`, `watchHeading`): `data-heading` on the flyout holds the open tab's name (the selected `.rollbox-check-tab`, `.rollbox-other-tab` or `.rollbox-action-tab`: Skill, Luck, Attack…), else the menu's (`heading`, else its label: "Opposed roll" for Opposed, whose tabs are per side). Each flyout switches its own tabs, so a `MutationObserver` on the flyout follows `aria-selected`; without one it follows each full redraw. The Art Deco theme draws it under the ornament's peak, except on Action, Check and Other, whose tabs sit there instead.
  - **A player's menu greyed whole**: `update(…, { greyed })`, from the panel's `redraw` with `status/` `playerBlock(actor)` for a player (never the Keeper): while their investigator is unconscious, dying, dead ("Unconscious", "Dying", "Dead") or in a bout of madness ("Bout of madness: the Keeper controls you"), every button is disabled with `rollbox-menu-greyed` and the reason as its hover, an open flyout closes, `offers` refuses every flyout and the panel's sheet clicks open nothing (`onValue`). The panel redraws on `updateActor` and the active-effect hooks, so it follows CoC7's conditions at once. The dying CON roll comes in its own pop-up (combat.md, `dying.js`).
  - **A player held while the Keeper decides**: `update(…, { greyed, keep })`: the panel's `redraw` asks `playerHold()` (set by combat's `holdWhileDeciding` through `setPlayerHold(hold)`; nothing by default) when no `playerBlock` applies, and passes its reason ("Waiting for the Keeper") with `keep: ["action"]` (`HOLD_KEEPS`): every button but Action greyed, `offers` refuses all but Action, an open Check or Other closes, sheet clicks open nothing. Action stays open with its own lock, so a player's attack waiting for the Keeper's answer stays in view (combat.md, `waiting.js`).
- **Connects to:** `registerFlyout`; `status/index.js` (`playerBlock`, through the panel); combat's `holdWhileDeciding` (`setPlayerHold`).
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing known. **Assumed.**

### Clicks on the character sheet's values: `onValue`, `fillsWaitingSide`, `openOpposed` (`index.js`); `shortcut`, `rowShortcut`, `howClicked` (`sheet-clicks.js`)

### Panel transparency: `TRANSPARENCY`, `applyTransparency(value)`, `setOpacity` (`appearance.js`, re-exported from `index.js`)
- **How it works:** The setting `rollboxTransparency` (0–70%, default 25) sets one CSS variable, `--rollbox-opacity`, on the panel: 25% see-through is `75%`. - **Connects to:** `settings.js` (`onChange`); `panel.css`; Foundry's settings `onChange` (called on this user's client when the value changes, build 370 `client-settings.mjs`).
- **Could interact poorly with:** browsers without `color-mix()` (Foundry v14's Electron and current browsers have it): the backgrounds would be dropped. **Assumed.** The pressed and hovered buttons stay solid on purpose. - **Connects to:** `settings.js` (`onChange` of both); `index.js` (`stylePanel`, `resetAppearance`); `flyouts/alert/being-attacked.js` (`followTheme`); `panel.css` (the Foundry palettes and the parts only the CoC7 themes show); `themes/art-deco.css`, `themes/coc7-minimal.css` (below); every part's CSS, which only uses the panel's `--rollbox-*` variables, so the flyouts, status cards, turn order and Opposed follow the theme; Foundry's `#interface` class and `foundry.utils.getRoute`; CoC7's installed files.

### Panel size: `SCALE`, `applyScale(size)`, `setScale`, `stepScale(direction)`, `saveScale(size)` (`appearance.js`, `applyScale` re-exported from `index.js`)
- **How it works:** The setting `rollboxScale` (80–250%, step 10, default 100) scales the whole panel element (the character panel, the menu and the flyout are all inside it) with `transform: scale()` by the size times Foundry's UI scale (`position.js`, `scaleTransform`): from `left bottom` beside the players list, from its spot's corner when moved. - **Connects to:** `settings.js` (`onChange`); `panel.css`.

  - **The Foundry theme's two palettes**, on `.rollbox-panel` (Dark, the default) and `.rollbox-panel.rollbox-theme-light` (Light, while Foundry's interface is Light), every part's CSS uses only these (the CoC7 themes redefine the same variables, above): `--rollbox-bg-solid` and `--rollbox-bg` (the Panel colour, solid and at the transparency's alpha; Light lets half as much through: `calc(50% + opacity / 2)`), `--rollbox-text`, `--rollbox-text-strong`, `--rollbox-text-muted`, `--rollbox-placeholder`, `--rollbox-disabled`, `--rollbox-border`, `--rollbox-border-hover`, `--rollbox-accent` (borders and fills) and `--rollbox-accent-text` (text in the accent colour, e.g. the Attacker tag), `--rollbox-bad` (the red of what's missing), `--rollbox-target` (Assist's coral), `--rollbox-defender` (the Defender tag's blue), `--rollbox-box` (an empty tick box or radio), `--rollbox-cell` (button and cell fill), `--rollbox-track` (bar tracks) and `--rollbox-pressed` (a pressed button or selected row: the solid colour on Dark, tinted with the accent on Light) and `--rollbox-second` (the bottom character on the two-character sheet: tan, `#d9b88a` on Dark, `#6b4a20` on Light, WCAG relative luminance 0.51 and 0.08, so 4.5:1 or more on any colour `shownColor` allows). Dark keeps Foundry's own players-card and scene-control colours (`--color-cool-5`, `--color-cool-4`, `--color-cool-3`, `--color-warm-2`) and fixed light text (`--color-light-1/2/4`), with fallbacks. Every text colour has at least 4.5:1 contrast on each theme's default solid colour and on any colour `shownColor` allows (checked with a script; the worst case is the placeholder at the luminance limit); disabled text and the empty tick box at least 3:1 on the defaults. The values are in the file.
  - The flyout's `max-height` is `calc(70vh / var(--rollbox-scale, 1))`. The HP, MP, SAN and Luck bars use `--rollbox-bar-hp`, `--rollbox-bar-mp`, `--rollbox-bar-san` and `--rollbox-bar-luck` (fixed colours, the same under both themes). `.rollbox-flyout-title` is a flyout's title.
- **Connects to:** Foundry's stylesheet variables (build 370 `foundry2.css`). Foundry loads module styles into its `modules` cascade layer, after its own and CoC7's.
- **Could interact poorly with:** UI scale: the panel multiplies `--ui-scale` in itself (`scaleTransform`). **Assumed.** Modules that put something beside the players list, where the panel sits. **Assumed.**

### A player's panel: `playerActors`, `onUserUpdate`, `alertSlot` (`index.js`)
- **How it works:** With the Keeper's Player Rollbox on (`player/` `playerRollboxOn()`, on by default), `registerPanel()` also runs on a player's client (with their own Rollbox switch on). The panel then shows `ownCharacter(game.user)` only (their assigned investigator, when they own it), whatever is selected, draws at once, and draws again on `updateUser` for this user (a new assigned character; `onUserUpdate`, which on the Keeper's client redraws for any user, for the players list). With none, the no-investigator message (above). Its menu is `update(actors, playerMenu())`: only the buttons with an allowed feature; sheet clicks pass `offersClick()` (a click on SAN does nothing for a player). With the Player Rollbox off, a player's client reads only that switch and installs nothing.
- **Connects to:** `player/` (`playerRollboxOn`, `ownCharacter`, `playerMenu`, `offersClick`); Foundry's `updateUser` hook.
- **The player's menu height:** `MenuHost.update(actors, ids)` marks a player's menu `rollbox-menu-fill` with `--rollbox-buttons` (how many), and `panel.css` shares the sheet's height between them, so one or two buttons fill it as the Keeper's three do.
- **The alert's place:** `alertSlot()` is `.rollbox-alert-slot` beside the menu (null while the panel is hidden); `main.js` hands it to the being-attacked alert (`setAlertHost`).
- **Flyouts:** Action, Check, Other and Opposed register on the player's client too (`player/` `panelHere()`), their tabs narrowed by the player's features (a player's Check has no Sanity tab).
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing known. Seen working in Foundry in the end-to-end check (the demo's Player with Helen assigned); **Assumed** at the table.

### Auto-hide: `AutoHide` (`schedule`, `wake`, `stop`, `watch`, `compact`), `AUTO_HIDE` (`auto-hide.js`); `applyAutoHide` (`index.js`)
- **How it works:** One `AutoHide` per panel, made by `buildPanel` with two callbacks: `redraw` (draw the card again and keep it in view) and `visible` (whether the panel shows). `drawCard` reads its `compact`; a new selection calls `wake()`, hiding the panel `stop()`. With `rollboxAutoHide` on, after `rollboxAutoHideAfter` seconds (1 to 300, default 10) without the pointer over the panel or a press on it, the one- or two-character sheet drops to the compact character card (`characterCards` for those characters, as several selected get; `cardKey` `compact`). Hovering over the panel (`pointerenter`), pressing it (`pointerdown`) or a new selection brings the full sheet back and starts the wait again; leaving it (`pointerleave`) starts the wait. A provider's content (combat's turn order), the players list, the no-investigator message and three or more characters are never changed. Both settings' `onChange` (`applyAutoHide`) apply at once. Keeper and players alike.
- **Connects to:** `cards.js`; `appearance.js` (`readSetting`); `settings.js` (`onChange`); `keepInView` (a moved panel placed again for the new card's size).
- **Could interact poorly with:** using a flyout counts as using the panel (the pointer is over it), so the card stays full while a flyout is used. **Assumed.**

## Hooks, overrides, keybindings
- Hooks (Keeper's client, switch on; a player's client with the Player Rollbox on): `controlToken`, `createToken`, `deleteToken`, `canvasReady` (redraw next frame); `canvasTearDown` (hide at once); `renderPlayers` (put the panel back in its place, and redraw the players list); `updateUser` (a player's own assignment on their client; any user on the Keeper's, for the players list); `updateActor` (redraw if that character is shown); `updateToken` (`onTokenUpdate`: redraw if the token's character is shown, unless the update only moved the token); `deleteActor` (redraw next frame, so a deleted character leaves the panel); `createActiveEffect`, `updateActiveEffect`, `deleteActiveEffect` (redraw if the effect's character is shown, `effectActor`: the effect's parent, or the item's owner for an effect on an item; CoC7's status icons are active effects, so a status card can change without an actor update); a second `controlToken` (`noteSelection`: Top when active or selected). All Foundry public hooks. Also the window's `resize` event (keep a moved panel on screen, the default one beside the players list), a `ResizeObserver` on `#players` while the panel sits beside it, a capture `pointerdown` on the panel (Top when active: `toFront`) and on the document (Top when active: `behind`), the panel's `pointerenter`, `pointerleave` and `pointerdown` (auto-hide) and, while a header is pressed, the document's `pointermove`, `pointerup` and `pointercancel`. No keyboard listener: Rollbox takes no keys from the page.
- Foundry's Dark or Light switch has no hook of its own: the panel watches `#interface`'s `class` with a `MutationObserver` (`watchScheme`, above).
- Class overrides: none.
- Keybindings: none registered; the only key read is Escape (below). Every control Rollbox draws is out of the Tab order (`tabIndex = -1`, set by `element()` in `utils.js` for buttons, inputs, selects and text areas), so Tab never stops on the panel; a click still focuses a text box, which takes typing.
- **Escape** (`onEscape`, `index.js`; a capture-phase `keydown` listener on `window`, added by `registerPanel`): while a flyout is open and the personal setting `rollboxEscapeClosesFlyout` is on (the default), Escape closes the flyout (the host's `close()`) and stops the event, so Foundry's own Escape (closing a window, releasing the tokens, its main menu) doesn't also run for that key press. With no flyout open, or the setting off, the event is left alone; a text box in another window keeps its own Escape. While a target pick's prompt shows (`player/` `picking()`), Escape is the prompt's: it cancels the pick and the flyout stays. Not a Foundry keybinding (`game.keybindings`), so it doesn't show in Foundry's Configure Controls. **Confirmed** in e2e (Foundry v14 build 370).

## Settings (`settings.js`)
Two exports of settings data. `PANEL_SETTINGS` holds the switch (the **Rollbox panel** feature at the top of the settings page), Show the players and Escape; `APPEARANCE_SETTINGS` the look and placement settings below, from Theme to the hidden offset. `settings.js` lays them out in the Rollbox panel section (Look, Placement, Behaviour, Menus, with the shared Simplified dice UI, [shared.md](shared.md)), which greys out while the switch is off ([settings.md](settings.md)). `durationText(seconds)` is how Auto-hide after's value reads beside its slider ("10 s", "1 min", "2 min 30 s"). Every key is permanent once released. What the Keeper and players see of them: the user guide's [Appearance](../guide/appearance.md) and [Settings](../guide/settings.md) pages.
- The feature switch `rollboxEnabled` ("Rollbox panel"; hint "Your Rollbox panel beside the players list. Off: nothing of Rollbox shows on your screen.", right for the Keeper and a player alike; user scope, Boolean, default true, Personal note). Requires a reload, because `registerPanel()` and every flyout's `register…()` (through `player/` `panelHere()`) read it once at `ready`.
- `rollboxEscapeClosesFlyout` ("Escape closes the flyout"; hint "Pressing Escape closes an open Rollbox flyout. With no flyout open, Escape does what Foundry does."; user scope, Boolean, default true, Personal note, no reload; read at each key press). Approved key.
- `rollboxShowPlayers` ("Show the players when nothing is selected"; hint "The Keeper's panel lists each player's investigator while no token is selected, with Check and Other for all of them. Off: the panel shows only while tokens are selected (or a combat runs)."; user scope, Boolean, default **on**; Personal note; `requiresReload: false`, applied at once through `onChange` (`refreshPanel`)). Only the Keeper's panel reads it, so it is `keeperOnly`: hidden on a player's settings page.
- `rollboxTheme` ("Theme"; hint "How the panel looks; the menus and buttons stay the same. Foundry: Foundry's own look, Dark or Light as Foundry's interface is set. Art Deco: CoC7's decorated character sheet. CoC7 minimal: CoC7's compact dark sheet. The two CoC7 looks use CoC7's own images and fonts, and show as Foundry without them."; user scope, String with `choices` `{ foundry: "Foundry", artDeco: "Art Deco", coc7Minimal: "CoC7 minimal" }`, which Configure Settings shows as a drop-down; default `foundry`; Personal note; `requiresReload: false`, applied at once through `onChange` (`applyTheme`); `reset: true`). The three values are permanent once released; any other saved value reads as `foundry`.
- `rollboxColor` ("Panel colour"; hint "Foundry theme only. Background colour of the panel, its buttons and its flyouts: it is shown darker or lighter where the text needs it, and lightened when Foundry's interface is Light. The CoC7 themes use CoC7's own colours."; user scope, `foundry.data.fields.ColorField` (not nullable), which Configure Settings shows as Foundry's colour picker (a text box and a colour swatch), or a plain String if the field is ever missing (`colorType`); default `#0b0a13`; Personal note; `requiresReload: false`, applied at once through `onChange` (`applyColor`); `reset: true`; `foundryOnly: true`, so Configure Settings greys it out while a CoC7 theme is chosen).
- `rollboxTransparency` ("Panel transparency"; hint "Foundry theme only. How see-through the panel, its buttons and its flyouts are. Text stays fully readable. The CoC7 themes are solid."; user scope, Number, `range` 0–70 step 5, which Foundry v14 shows as a slider (`SettingsConfig` turns `type: Number` with `range` into a `NumberField` with min, max and step); default 25; Personal note; `requiresReload: false`, applied at once through `onChange` (`applyTransparency`); `reset: true`; `foundryOnly: true`).
- `rollboxScale` ("Panel size"; hint "Size of the panel, its buttons and its flyouts."; user scope, Number, `range` 80–250 step 10 (a slider); default 100; Personal note; `requiresReload: false`, applied at once through `onChange` (`applyScale`); `reset: true`).
- `rollboxMovable` ("Allow moving the panel"; hint "Drag the panel by its header (the name row) to put it anywhere on the screen; it stays where you drop it. Reset position, or turning this off, puts it back beside the players list."; **client** scope (per screen), Boolean, default **on**; Personal note; no reload (`applyMovable`); `button` **Reset position** (`resetPosition`), which `settings.js` puts beside the tick box).
- `rollboxMenuSide` ("Menu and flyouts open to the"; hint "Which side of the panel the menu and flyouts open on. Automatic: towards the middle of the screen. Beside the players list they always open to the right."; user scope, String with `choices` `{ auto: "Automatic", right: "Right", left: "Left" }`, default `auto`; Personal note; no reload (`applyMenuSide`)).
- `rollboxPanelLayer` ("Panel layer"; hint "Whether Foundry's windows (sheets, journals, roll dialogs) cover the panel. Bottom: windows always cover it. - `rollboxCardMeters` ("Meters on the small cards"; hint "Which bars the small character cards show: the cards when several characters are selected, and each combatant's row in the turn order.
