# Flyout alert (`scripts/flyouts/alert/`)

The red flyout on a player's screen when a CoC7 attack card names their character as the target (Player Rollbox on). Melee: "You are being attacked" with Dodge · Fight back · Counter · No response, each answered in one click: Rollbox presses CoC7's own choice on its defender card (Fight back and Counter pick from CoC7's own lists), then the card's roll button. Maneuver (Rollbox's maneuver notice): the same four, answered through Rollbox, which then makes CoC7's opposed card (No response: CoC7 rolls the attacker's maneuver alone) and, on a player's client, presses their own row's roll on it; the Keeper gets this alert too, for a target no player answers for. Firearms: "You are being shot at" with Dive for cover (CoC7's Dodge roll; on a success Rollbox notes Dived for cover and the Keeper's Rollbox ticks Under cover on the shooter's card) or Stand your ground, before the shot is rolled. CoC7's cards are only read, and its buttons only pressed, as the player or the Keeper could. The shooter's Action flyout shows the decision ([action-flyout.md](action-flyout.md)). A target the Keeper decided is **surprised** (About to attack, [combat.md](combat.md)) gets no alert at all, and the active Keeper's Rollbox answers No response for them. Files: `index.js` (the public surface), `being-attacked.js` (the hooks), `alerts.js` (what every alert shares), `choices.js` (Fight back and Counter), `melee.js`, `maneuver.js`, `shots.js`, `alert.css`. User guide: [Statuses and alerts](../guide/statuses-and-alerts.md).

**Status key:** *Confirmed* only when the build guide's Internal API registry or changelog says so. Everything is **assumed** unless stated otherwise; "Seen in e2e" marks what the end-to-end run in Foundry has shown, still to be confirmed at the table.

## What the player sees

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Where | Beside their menu in their own panel (`setAlertHost`); without a panel showing, in Rollbox's own box above Foundry's players list, in their Theme |
| Melee | "You are being attacked", a × (closes it for that card), "Cultist of Dagon attacks Helen.", **Dodge** · **Fight back** · **Counter** · **No response**, one row. Dodge and No response roll at once. Fight back opens one button per melee weapon (the weapons CoC7's own Fight back lists: melee weapons whose skill is on the sheet, the unarmed weapon first); a pick rolls. Counter opens the maneuver weapons (the Punch first and picked; shown only when there are two or more) and the six goals with the Build line ("Build: 1 penalty die.", "Build: no penalty dice.", "Build: they are 3 or more bigger; a maneuver can't move them." with the goals greyed, "Build: unknown, the Keeper decides the dice."); a goal rolls. One answer's options are open at a time. An answer greys the buttons: "Dodge: rolling on CoC7's card…"; the alert closes once CoC7's card is rolled. If the card never shows the choice: "Dodge: roll it on CoC7's card in chat." (buttons back), or "Choose Dodge on CoC7's card in chat." when the card's button isn't on the page. Dodge is greyed without a Dodge skill (as CoC7's). Fight back with no weapon CoC7 would list, and Counter with no fighting skill to counter with, stay clickable, with "No weapon" or "No maneuver weapon" as their hover, and ask (below, "Nothing to fight back or counter with"). The **Keeper** sees the same melee alert, titled "Deep One is being attacked" ("Cecil attacks Deep One."), for an NPC or creature targeted by CoC7's melee card, in or out of combat, and answers it the same way |
| Maneuver | On Rollbox's maneuver notice for their character: "You are being attacked", a ×, the notice's sentence ("Cultist of Dagon attempts to restrain (maneuver) Helen."), **Dodge** · **Fight back** · **Counter** · **No response**. Fight back opens one button per melee weapon (the unarmed weapon first, then the rest A–Z), two to a row; Counter opens the maneuver weapons (the Punch first and picked, a whip with Maneuver on after it; shown with two or more) and the six goals with the Build line (the goals greyed when the Build makes a maneuver impossible). Without a weapon with Maneuver on, Counter stays clickable with "No maneuver weapon" as its hover and asks; Fight back likewise without a melee weapon. An answer greys the buttons, "Dodge: making CoC7's opposed card…", then "Dodge: CoC7's opposed card is in chat; your side is rolled once the Keeper starts the rolls." (Rollbox presses the player's own Roll dice on the card once Start Rolls has run); No response reads "No response: CoC7 rolls the maneuver…" and the alert closes once it is answered (or "No card: <why>." and the buttons back). The **Keeper** sees the same alert, titled "Deep One Hybrid is being attacked", for a target no player answers for (an NPC, an investigator whose players are all offline, or any target with the Player Rollbox off), and after an answer reads "CoC7's opposed card is in chat, Start Rolls pressed." |
| Shot at | "You are being shot at", a ×, "Cultist of Dagon is shooting at Helen.", "Dive for cover: a Dodge roll. On a success the shooter gets 1 penalty die and you lose your next attack.", **Dive for cover** (highlighted) · **Stand your ground**. After Dive: "Rolling Dodge…", then "You dived for cover: the shooter gets 1 penalty die. You lose your next attack.", "The dive failed: the shot comes as it is.", "You dived for cover. No encounter is running, so ask the Keeper to tick Under cover.", "No Dodge roll came back; ask the Keeper." or "CoC7 couldn't roll Dodge; ask the Keeper." |
| Closing | when CoC7's card is answered (melee) or the shot rolled, when the card or notice is deleted, on ×, or on Stand your ground; a maneuver's alert closes on every screen but the answering one once the notice is answered |

## Code

### `index.js` and the files
- **How it works:** The alert's public surface: re-exports `DIVE_HOOK`, `diveDecision`, `registerBeingAttacked` and `setAlertHost` from `being-attacked.js`. Other parts import the alert only from here. The files: `being-attacked.js` registers the hooks and routes a player's card to the melee or shot alert (`onCard`), and re-exports what the tests read (`readAttack`, `setAlertHost`, `setPlayerAccess`, `DIVE_HOOK`, `diveDecision`); `alerts.js` holds what every alert shares (the alerts shown, dismissed and being answered, `access`, `readAttack`, `sameCharacter`, the host, `frame`, `say`, `alertButton`, `closeAlert`, `showAlert`, `controlList`); `choices.js` Fight back and Counter as both answering alerts offer them (`fightAndCounter`: the weapons, the maneuver weapons, goals and Build line; `askForAttack`); `melee.js` the melee alert, the Keeper's melee alert and a surprised target's No response; `maneuver.js` the maneuver alert and its closing; `shots.js` the shot-at alert, Dive for cover, `diveDecision` and Under cover on the Keeper's side. CoC7's card buttons are pressed through `roll-requests/` (`pressCardButton`, `pickFromCardList`, `rollWhenChosen`, [roll-requests.md](roll-requests.md)).
- **Connects to:** `main.js` (`registerBeingAttacked`, `setAlertHost`); the Action flyout (`DIVE_HOOK`, `diveDecision`, [action-flyout.md](action-flyout.md)).
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing. **Assumed.**

### `registerBeingAttacked()` (`being-attacked.js`, called from `main.js` at `ready` through `index.js`)
- **How it works:** On a player's client returns `false` and installs nothing unless the Player Rollbox switch is on (`playerRollboxOn`, `player/index.js`) and their own Rollbox switch is on (`panelHere`). Clears the alerts shown, dismissed and being answered. On every client it installs `createChatMessage` / `updateChatMessage` (`onNotice`: the maneuver alerts; `onOpposedCard`: a maneuver's alert closes once CoC7's opposed card has its result) and `deleteChatMessage` (close the alert); the Keeper's client stops there while the Player Rollbox is off. With it on, on every client it also installs: `updateActor` (`onNotesChanged`) and `createChatMessage` / `updateChatMessage` / `deleteChatMessage` (`onShotCard`), which announce dive decisions (`DIVE_HOOK`, below). On the Keeper's client, always (before the Player Rollbox check): `createChatMessage` and `updateChatMessage` (`onKeeperCard`: the melee alert for an NPC or creature); with the Player Rollbox on also `updateActor` (`onDived`, Under cover after a dive). On a player's: `createChatMessage` and `updateChatMessage` (`onCard`: show, keep or close its alert). Returns `true` when installed.
- **Connects to:** `player/index.js` (`playerRollboxOn`, `panelHere`, `ownCharacter`); Foundry's chat message and actor hooks.
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing known. **Assumed.**

### `readAttack(message)` (`alerts.js`, re-exported by `being-attacked.js`)
- **How it works:** Reads CoC7's card data (`message.flags.CoC7.load`, 8.15), read only and guarded (a throw is "no attack"). A melee defender card: `as: "CoC7ChatCombatMelee"`, `participant: 1` (CoC7 makes it after the attacker rolls), with `targetUuid`; open while `responded` isn't true and its dice pool has no rolled die (`dicePool.rolledDice[].rolled`). A ranged card: `as: "CoC7ChatCombatRanged"`, its `targets[].uuid` in order (their offsets on the card) and the shooter `actorUuid`; open while `weaponRolled` isn't true. Returns `{ kind: "melee" | "shot", id, attackerUuid, targets, open, load }`. Uuids are compared with dots (CoC7 stores some with slashes). Anything else (the attacker's melee card, checks) is `null`.
- **Connects to:** CoC7's card flags (registry).
- **Could interact poorly with:** a CoC7 update that renames these fields: no alert. **Assumed.**

### `sameCharacter(uuid, actor)`, `actorOf(uuid)` (`alerts.js`)
- **How it works:** `actorOf` is the character a card's uuid names: utils' `actorFromUuid` (Foundry's `fromUuidSync` guarded), a token's actor for a token. A card's uuid names the character (`sameCharacter`) when it is its uuid, ends in `Actor.<id>` (a token's synthetic actor), or resolves through `actorOf` to it or to an actor with its id.
- **Connects to:** Foundry's `fromUuidSync`.
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing known. **Assumed.**

### What every alert shares (`alerts.js`): `alerts`, `dismissed`, `answeredSurprise`, `answering`, `access`, `showAlert`, `closeAlert`, `frame`, `say`, `whoAttacks`, `alertButton`, `controlList`
- **How it works:** `alerts` maps a CoC7 card's or maneuver notice's message id to its shown alert (`{ node, line }`); `dismissed` holds the cards closed with × or Stand your ground (never drawn again); `answeredSurprise` the cards and notices answered No response for a surprised target (each answered once); `answering` the maneuver notices this client answered (their alert stays to say where to roll). `access` holds `player/index.js`'s `playerRollboxOn`, `ownCharacter` and `panelHere` (`setPlayerAccess` replaces them). `showAlert(id, shown)` appends the alert to its host (below, `setAlertHost`) and remembers it; `closeAlert(id, dismiss)` removes it, with `dismiss` for good. `frame(id, title, subtitle)` builds the `section.rollbox-alert` (role `alert`, `data-message-id`) with its title, its × (`aria-label` "Close", closing for good) and the subtitle line, and returns it with an `aria-live` line (`say(line, text)` writes it, hidden when empty). `whoAttacks(attack, me, verb)`: "Cultist of Dagon attacks Helen." from the card's attacker, or nothing. `alertButton` is a `type="button"` button; `controlList()` collects an alert's buttons so an answer can grey them all.
- **Connects to:** `melee.js`, `maneuver.js`, `shots.js`, `choices.js`, `being-attacked.js`; `panel/` (`followTheme`); Foundry's `#interface`.
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing known. **Assumed.**

### The player's alert (`onCard` in `being-attacked.js`; `meleeAlert`, private `fightBackWeapons`, `counterSkill` in `melee.js`; `fightAndCounter` in `choices.js`; `shotAlert`, private `dive`, `dodgeSkill`, `waitForDodge`, `isDodgeResult` in `shots.js`)
- **How it works:** For each card `readAttack` reads: only when one of its targets is the player's own character (`ownCharacter(game.user)`). A card no longer open closes its alert; an alert already showing, or one the player closed, isn't drawn again. Each alert is a `section.rollbox-alert` (role `alert`) with its title, a × that closes it for good, a line saying who attacks (`whoAttacks`), and a line (`aria-live`) for what happened.
  - **Melee** (`meleeAlert`): each answer is one click, in two presses on CoC7's defender card, as the player would make them (roll-requests' card presses). First the choice: **Dodge** presses CoC7's `dodge` and **No response** its `setNoResponse` (`pressCardButton`); a **Fight back** weapon presses `setFightBack` on the card's chat-log copy, waits for CoC7's dropdown (`#dropdown-<message id>`) and presses that weapon's `setItem` entry (`pickFromCardList`); a **Counter** goal does the same under `setManeuvers` with the picked weapon's fighting skill (`counterSkill`: the weapon's own skill when CoC7 marks it `properties.fighting`, else the character's `fightingSkill`). Then the roll: `rollWhenChosen` watches `renderChatMessageHTML` for that card until its flags show the choice (`itemUuid` ending in the chosen item's id, or `isNoResponse`) and presses CoC7's `targetRoll` (tried every `CLICK_DELAY_MS` up to `CLICK_TRIES`, since CoC7 attaches its listeners after its own async render); after `CHOICE_WAIT_MS` (15 s) without it, the line says to roll on the card and the buttons come back. A choice already showing (a second press) rolls at once. Fight back's list is `fightBackWeapons` (`meleeWeapons` whose skill `weaponSkillItem` finds: what CoC7's own list holds); Counter's is `counterWeapons` (the Punch first; with none, the unarmed weapon), drawn as a pick row (`pickRow`, the picked one `rollbox-alert-picked`) only with two or more; then the goals (`MANEUVER_GOALS`) and the Build line (`buildText` from shared's `maneuverDice(defender's Build, attacker's Build)`, each Build from shared's `buildOf`; all in `choices.js` `fightAndCounter`, which the maneuver alert uses too: penalty dice as a negative number, `impossible`, `known`; [shared.md](shared.md)). The goal is for the Keeper to apply; Rollbox doesn't send it. With 3 or more Build below the attacker the goals are greyed.
  - **Shot at** (`shotAlert`, `dive`): **Dive for cover** greys both buttons and asks CoC7 to roll the character's Dodge (`callActor(actor, "skillCheck", <Dodge skill name>, true)`: the skill CoC7 marks `system.isDodge`, else "Dodge", which CoC7 finds or adds at its base). `waitForDodge` returns `{ promise, cancel }`: the promise waits (up to `DODGE_WAIT_MS`, 2 minutes) for this user's next `CoC7Check` card for the character (`sameCharacter`) through roll-requests' one check-card watch (`watchCheck`, `mine`), which reads its result with `checkResult` (CoC7's `game.CoC7.messageResults(message)`, the first rolled result), taking only a Dodge result (`isDodgeResult`: a skill result whose `key`, the skill item's uuid, resolves through `fromUuidSync` to an item CoC7 marks `system.isDodge`, or named as the Dodge skill); another check card for the character (a Spot Hidden rolled meanwhile) is passed over and the wait goes on. When CoC7 can't roll the Dodge, `cancel` ends the watch at once. A success: `markDived` (combat's round notes, the one source for Dived for cover); with no encounter running nothing can be noted and the line asks the player to have the Keeper tick Under cover. **Stand your ground** notes `stood` (`markNote`) and closes the alert for that card.
- **Connects to:** `combat/index.js` (`markDived`, `markNote`, `hasDived`, `roundNote`, `NOTES_FLAG`), `shared/index.js` (`MANEUVER_GOALS`, `maneuverDice`), `roll-requests/index.js` (`callActor`, `checkResult`, `ownersOf`, `pressControl`, `CLICK_DELAY_MS`, `CLICK_TRIES`, `meleeWeapons`, `counterWeapons`, `unarmedOnSheet`, `weaponSkillItem`, `fightingSkill`, and for maneuvers `readManeuverNotice`, `noticeText`, `answerManeuver`, `rollOwnSide`), `panel/index.js` (`followTheme`); CoC7's `actor.skillCheck`, `game.CoC7.messageResults` (registry) and a skill result's `key` read as the item's uuid with `fromUuidSync`.
- **Could interact poorly with:** CoC7's Fight back and Maneuver open their list (a dropdown) beside the card's copy in the chat log (`.chat-scroll`); with no such copy on the page (the chat log not rendered) the line says to choose on CoC7's card. **Assumed.** CoC7's dropdown closes on a mouse leaving it; a script's press makes no mouse move, so the list stays until CoC7's redraw. **Assumed.** **Seen in e2e** (each of the four answers rolled on the defender card in one click). CoC7 giving a skill result's `key` as something other than the item's uuid: no result reads as the Dodge and Dive for cover waits until it stops. **Assumed.** A second Dodge of the character's rolled by the same player during the wait would be read as the dive's. **Assumed.**

### The Keeper's melee alert (`onKeeperCard`, `keeperDefends`; `melee.js`)
- **How it works:** On the Keeper's client, each CoC7 melee defender card (`readAttack` kind `melee`) whose target (`actorOf` of its `targetUuid`, a token's actor for an unlinked token) is an NPC or a creature (`keeperDefends`: CoC7's actor type `npc` or `creature`) gets `meleeAlert(attack, target, true)`: the player's melee alert titled "<name> is being attacked", with the same four answers pressed on CoC7's defender card (`pressCardButton`, `pickFromCardList`, `rollWhenChosen`). Shown in or out of combat (nothing reads the encounter). Closes as the player's does: once CoC7's card is answered, on ×, or when the card is deleted. Each Keeper sees it. An investigator target never gets it (their player answers, or the Keeper on CoC7's card).
- **Connects to:** `meleeAlert` (above); Foundry's chat message hooks; CoC7's defender card (registry).
- **Could interact poorly with:** two Keepers answering the same card: CoC7 takes the first choice; the second's roll button press waits for a render that shows its choice and gives up after 15 s. An NPC owned by a player: the Keeper still gets the alert (the player's own alert shows only for their assigned character). **Assumed.** **Seen in e2e** (the spear Deep One punched by Cecil, in combat with the Keeper acting and out of combat with the Player's attack; No response answered on CoC7's card).

### The maneuver alert (`onNotice`, `maneuverAlert`, `keeperAnswers`; `maneuver.js`)
- **How it works:** `onNotice` reads Rollbox's maneuver notice (`roll-requests/` `readManeuverNotice`). Answered: its alert closes, except on the client that answered it (`answering`), which keeps it to say where to roll. Not answered: on a player's client, shown when the target is their own character (`sameCharacter`); on the Keeper's, when `keeperAnswers(notice, target)`: the Player Rollbox is off, or no player owner of the target (`ownersOf`) is logged in (`User#active`) **with the target as their own Rollbox character** (`player/` `ownCharacter`: owned and assigned; `sameCharacter` against the notice's target), the only player whose client shows the alert. An owner online without the target assigned gets no alert, so the Keeper answers then (otherwise nobody would, and CoC7's opposed card would never be made). Each Keeper sees it; the first answer wins (the notice then reads answered). `maneuverAlert`: **Dodge** answers `{ answer: "dodge" }`; **Fight back** opens a row of buttons, one per `roll-requests/` `meleeWeapons(target)`, each answering `{ answer: "fight", weaponId }`; **Counter** opens the maneuver weapons (`counterWeapons(target)`, the Punch first and picked; a pick row with two or more), the goals (each answering `{ answer: "counter", weaponId }` with the picked weapon; the goal is only said on the line) and the Build line (`maneuverDice(target's Build, attacker's Build)`), the goals greyed when the Build makes it impossible; with no maneuver weapon (or no melee weapon for Fight back) the button asks instead (`askForAttack`, below); **No response** answers `{ answer: "none" }` and, once answered, closes the alert (CoC7 rolls the maneuver alone). Opening one answer's options closes the other's. On a player's client an answer other than No response first starts `rollOwnSide(target, attacker)` (stopped again on a refusal), which presses the player's own row's Roll dice on CoC7's opposed card once Start Rolls has run. The answer goes to `roll-requests/` `answerManeuver` (the Keeper's client makes CoC7's card; a player's asks the Keeper's through the relay); a refusal puts the buttons back with the reason.
- **Connects to:** `roll-requests/index.js` (`readManeuverNotice`, `noticeText`, `answerManeuver`, `rollOwnSide`, `meleeWeapons`, `counterWeapons`, `ownersOf`); `choices.js` (`fightAndCounter`); `shared/` (`MANEUVER_GOALS`, `maneuverDice`); combat (`surpriseOf`, `clearSurprise`); Foundry's `User#active`.
- **Could interact poorly with:** two Keepers answering the same notice at once: the second is refused ("the maneuver was already answered") once the first's answer is saved; both could pass in the same instant. **Assumed.** A player who reloads their page while a notice waits doesn't see its alert again (alerts come from new or changed messages only); the Keeper can then answer from theirs only if the player is offline. **Assumed.** **Seen in e2e** (the Keeper's alert for the Cultist and the Deep One, the Player's for Cecil; Dodge, Fight back, Counter, No response; the Player's own row rolled by Rollbox).

### Nothing to fight back or counter with (`askForAttack`, `addToSheet`, `NO_WEAPON`, `NO_MANEUVER_WEAPON`; `choices.js`, used by `meleeAlert` and `maneuverAlert` through `fightAndCounter`)
- **How it works**: **Fight back** with no weapon to list (the melee alert: no melee weapon whose skill CoC7 finds; the maneuver alert: no melee weapon) stays clickable with "No weapon" as its hover; **Counter** likewise with "No maneuver weapon" (the maneuver alert: no maneuver weapon; the melee alert, which counters with CoC7's Maneuver list of fighting skills: only when CoC7 has no fighting skill to list). A press calls `askForAttack`: on the Keeper's alert, the Action flyout's own questions (`prompt.js` `promptBox`, class `rollbox-alert-prompt`) above the alert's line: Fight back "Deep One has no weapon to fight back with. Add **Fighting** to its sheet?" (an investigator's sheet is named), or, for an unarmed weapon whose skill CoC7 can't find, "Link it to Fighting (Brawl)?"; Counter "Switch Maneuver on for Cecil's Punch?" or "Add **Punch** to …'s sheet? Its Maneuver is switched on." A creature whose fighting skill Rollbox can't tell gets the Skill list. **Add**, **Fix** or **Switch on** changes the sheet (`roll-requests/` `addAttacks`, `setWeaponSkill`, `switchManeuverOn`; the item stays) and carries on: Fight back with the new weapon (CoC7's own list, or the maneuver answer), Counter's goals opened with it. **Cancel** changes nothing. On a player's alert the line says what is missing and to ask their Keeper ("Your sheet has no weapon to fight back with. Ask your Keeper to add a Punch."; Counter: "Your Punch has Maneuver off. Ask your Keeper to switch it on." or the add line); nothing is added.
- **Connects to:** `roll-requests/` (`missingAttacks`, `addAttacks`, `fightingSkills`, `fightingSkill`, `setWeaponSkill`, `switchManeuverOn`, `unarmedOnSheet`); `flyouts/action/prompt.js` (`promptBox`, `addLine`, `askKeeperText`); CoC7's Fight back list, built from the sheet when it opens (`setFightBack`: melee weapons whose skill is on the sheet), so the new weapon is in it.
- **Could interact poorly with:** a creature with no fighting skill at all: the added weapon has no skill, so CoC7 can't list it (the line says to answer on CoC7's card). **Assumed.**

### Closing once CoC7 has the result (`onOpposedCard`; private `opposedDone`, `closeWhenDone`; `maneuver.js`)
- **How it works**: `opposedDone(message)` → `true` once CoC7's opposed card (`flags.CoC7.load.as` `CoC7ChatOpposedMessage`) has every side rolled (`game.CoC7.messageResults`, one result per side, each `isRolled`), whatever the outcome. `onOpposedCard` (`createChatMessage`, `updateChatMessage`, every client) closes the alert of any shown maneuver whose notice names that card (`card`), and drops it from `answering`; `onNotice` also checks the named card when the notice changes, since Start Rolls can roll both sides before the notice names its card. No response closes the alert as soon as CoC7 has rolled the maneuver alone.
- **Connects to:** `roll-requests/` (`readManeuverNotice`), CoC7's opposed card (registry).
- **Could interact poorly with:** a tie waiting for the Keeper's Advantage: both sides are rolled, so the alert closes before Advantage is pressed (Rollbox's Keeper presses it, `onOpposedRender`). Assumed; seen in e2e (both failing, the Keeper on both sides and a player answering).

### A surprised target (`noResponse` in `melee.js`, `answeredSurprise` in `alerts.js`; in `onCard`, `onKeeperCard`, `onNotice`)
- **How it works**: combat's `surprisedBy` / `surpriseOf` read the target's `flags.keepers-rollbox.surprised` (the Keeper's About to attack decision for this attacker, at most ten minutes old). A player's client (`onCard`) shows no melee or shot alert for it. On the Keeper's clients `onKeeperCard` skips the Keeper's melee alert, and the active Keeper (`noResponse`) presses CoC7's `setNoResponse` on the open defender card (retried every `CLICK_DELAY_MS`, since the card is drawn a moment after `createChatMessage`), then its `targetRoll` through `rollWhenChosen`, once per card, and clears the mark (`clearSurprise`). A maneuver notice (`onNotice`) gets no alert anywhere; the active Keeper answers `answerManeuver(id, { answer: "none", bonus })` (one bonus die when the Keeper picked it) and clears the mark. With Automatic hit CoC7 makes no defender card, so nothing is pressed.
- **Connects to:** combat (`surprisedBy`, `surpriseOf`, `clearSurprise`), roll-requests (`answerManeuver`), CoC7's defender card (registry).
- **Could interact poorly with:** the Keeper answering the defender card by hand at the same moment (CoC7 takes the first choice). Assumed; seen in e2e (One bonus die on the spear Deep One's attack on Cecil; the Cultist's maneuver).

### Pressing CoC7's card buttons
- **How it works:** The alert presses CoC7's buttons only through `roll-requests/` (`pressCardButton`, `pickFromCardList`, `rollWhenChosen`, `cardCopies`, all in `card-buttons.js`; [roll-requests.md](roll-requests.md)).
- **Connects to:** `roll-requests/index.js`.
- **Could interact poorly with:** see roll-requests.md. **Assumed.**

### Under cover on the Keeper's side (`onDived`; `shots.js`)
- **How it works:** `updateActor` on the Keeper's client: when the change sets the round note's `dived` (`flags.keepers-rollbox.roundNotes.dived`) to true, and this is the active Keeper (utils' `isActiveKeeper`: Foundry's `game.users.activeGM`, so it happens once with several Keepers), Rollbox looks through the last 50 chat messages, newest first, for a ranged card not yet rolled with that character as a target; if Under cover (`cover`) isn't already in that target's `poolKeys`, it presses CoC7's own `toggleTargetKey` / `cover` button in that target's `.ranged-targets-option[data-offset]`. CoC7 offers that toggle to the Keeper only (`keeper-only-control`), which is why the player's client can't. If the button isn't on the page: "Rollbox: Helen dived for cover; tick Under cover on the shooter's card."
- **Connects to:** CoC7's ranged card (registry); Foundry's `game.users.activeGM`, `game.messages`.
- **Could interact poorly with:** CoC7's toggle also makes that target the card's active one. **Assumed.** A dive noted with no open shot at the character ticks nothing. **Assumed.**

### `diveDecision(actor)`, `DIVE_HOOK`, `onNotesChanged(actor, changes)`, `onShotCard(message)`; private `openShotAt`, `recentMessages`, `announce` (`shots.js`; `diveDecision` and `DIVE_HOOK` re-exported by `being-attacked.js` and `index.js`)
- **Interface:** `diveDecision(actor)` → `"dived" | "stood" | "pending" | null`, on any client. `DIVE_HOOK` is `keepers-rollbox.diveDecision`, called with `(actor, decision)` (Foundry's `Hooks.callAll`).
- **How it works:** "dived" when the round note says dived; "stood" when it says stood; "pending" when the Player Rollbox is on, the character has a player owner, and an open shot (a ranged card not yet rolled, among the last 50 messages) targets them; otherwise null. The hook fires (`announce`, `Hooks.callAll` with each character's new decision) when a round note changes (`onNotesChanged`: `updateActor` with the `roundNotes` flag set or removed) and when a shot card is posted, changed or deleted (`onShotCard`, for each of its targets).
- **Connects to:** the Action flyout's target box (`flyouts/action/target.js`), which shows it and redraws on the hook; combat's round notes.
- **Could interact poorly with:** out of an encounter there are no round notes, so only "pending" or null. **Assumed.** **Seen in e2e** (waiting, dived and stands their ground on the shooter's target box).

### `setAlertHost(provider)`, `setPlayerAccess(answers)` (`alerts.js`, re-exported by `being-attacked.js`), `DODGE_WAIT_MS` (`shots.js`, private)
- **How it works:** `setAlertHost(() => element)` draws the alerts inside another element (`main.js` passes the panel's `alertSlot`, beside the player's menu, null while the panel is hidden); without it, or when it fails or returns nothing, they go in Rollbox's own box, `div.rollbox-panel.rollbox-alert-host` in Foundry's `#interface`, fixed above the players list, which wears the player's own Theme like their panel (`panel/` `followTheme`). `setPlayerAccess({ playerRollboxOn, ownCharacter, panelHere })` replaces `player/index.js`'s answers (the tests' stub; `panelHere` defaults to true); `null` puts them back. `DODGE_WAIT_MS` is how long Dive for cover waits for CoC7's Dodge card.
- **Connects to:** `main.js`; `panel/index.js` (`alertSlot`, `followTheme`); `player/index.js`.
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing known. **Assumed.**

### `alert.css`
- **How it works:** Listed in `module.json` `styles`. The alert's red frame, title and ×, the who-attacks line, the note, the action buttons (four share one row evenly), the weapons, Counter's block with its picked weapon marked (`rollbox-alert-counter`, `rollbox-alert-picked`), the goals and Build line, and the outcome line; Rollbox's own box (`rollbox-alert-host`) fixed above the players list. Every rule is scoped under `.rollbox-panel` (the own box carries that class), so the theme and colour variables apply.
- **Connects to:** `panel/panel.css`, `shared/shared.css`; the theme stylesheets.
- **Could interact poorly with:** Foundry's players list moving or changing height: the own box may overlap it. **Assumed.**

## Hooks, overrides, keybindings
- `createChatMessage`, `updateChatMessage`, `deleteChatMessage` on the Keeper's client always (the maneuver alerts, their closing once CoC7's opposed card has its result, and the melee alert for NPCs and creatures), and with the rest on a player's client with their Rollbox switch on.
- `createChatMessage`, `updateChatMessage`, `deleteChatMessage`, `updateActor` (every client with the Player Rollbox on, a player's only with their Rollbox switch on): the alerts on a player's client, Under cover on the Keeper's, `DIVE_HOOK` on both. Temporary per Dive for cover: `createChatMessage` / `updateChatMessage` (removed once the Dodge result is read or the wait ends). Temporary per melee answer: `renderChatMessageHTML` (`rollWhenChosen`, removed once the roll is pressed or after 15 s); per maneuver answer on a player's client: `renderChatMessageHTML` (`rollOwnSide`, removed once pressed or after 60 s).
- Calls its own hook `keepers-rollbox.diveDecision` (`Hooks.callAll`).
- Class overrides: none. Keybindings: none.

## Settings
None of its own. Follows the Player Rollbox switch (`rollboxPlayerRollbox`) and, on a player's client, their Rollbox switch (`rollboxEnabled`); the Keeper's maneuver alerts show whatever the switches say (with the Player Rollbox off the Keeper answers every maneuver).
