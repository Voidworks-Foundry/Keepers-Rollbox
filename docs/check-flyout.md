# Check flyout (`scripts/flyouts/check/`)

The flyout the Check button opens, in three sections with space between them: what (tabs, search, list), how (options), send (status line and buttons). Three tabs, **Characteristic · Skill · Sanity** (Sanity is a Check tab, with no button of its own). The first two list what the selected characters can roll; ticking one is a check, ticking two or more is a combined roll; the options set difficulty, bonus or penalty dice, Blind and a label. The Sanity tab is its own request with its own sections ([sanity-flyout.md](sanity-flyout.md)). There is no Attribute tab: Luck and the Group Luck roll are the Other flyout's ([other-flyout.md](other-flyout.md)), and Idea and Know are INT and EDU checks with a label ("Idea", "Know"). The lists show as rows or, with the List style setting on Tiles, as tiles. A click on a characteristic or SAN on the character sheet opens the flyout with that one ticked or on the Sanity tab, or sends at once (`apply`, below); the rows and tiles take CoC7's sheet shortcuts too (`rowShortcut`). One send button ("Send to all players", "Send to <name>", "Send to each (N)", or "Roll for NPC(s)"; no Send to chat and no Copy link button; Ctrl + click on a row copies its link) goes through `roll-requests/`, which builds CoC7's own links and rolls NPCs for the Keeper: the flyout builds no request text and rolls nothing itself. With nothing selected the Keeper's Check goes to every investigator (`everyone`, from the menu host). The Keeper's, with the Rollbox switch on; a player's with the Player Rollbox: their own investigator only, tabs **Characteristic · Skill** (no Sanity tab: Sanity checks are the Keeper's; `tabsFor`), title "Make a check", and **Make check**, which has CoC7 make the check on the player's own client at once, without its window (roll-requests `makeCheck`), Regular with no dice, Blind, Hidden or label; a player without the Combined checks feature ticks one check at a time. Files: `index.js`, `characteristic.js`, `skill.js`, `sanity.js`, `check.css`; the shortcut mapping is `panel/sheet-clicks.js` (taken through `panel/index.js`); the small DOM helpers are `flyouts/flyout.js` ([flyouts.md](flyouts.md)) and the shared controls and generic looks `shared/` ([shared.md](shared.md)). User guide: [Check](../guide/check.md), [Shortcuts](../guide/shortcuts.md).

**Status key:** *Confirmed* only when the build guide's Internal API registry or changelog says so. Everything is **assumed** unless stated otherwise.

## What a player sees

Their own investigator only (one character). Title "Make a check" ("Make a combined check" with two or more ticks, Combined checks allowed); tabs Characteristic · Skill (those the Checks feature allows); no Difficulty, Dice, Blind or Label (`howRows(…, { own: true })` gives an empty place; difficulty and dice are the Keeper's call, on the Keeper's requests); the button **Make check**. A click rolls it straight into chat, Regular with no bonus or penalty dice and no window, and the result reads "CoC7's check, in chat:" and the investigator's name (`OWN_RESULT`); a combined check posts CoC7's combined card, which waits for their roll. Shift + click on a row (or on a characteristic of their sheet) makes it at once the same way.

No help lines or tooltips: no Hidden hint, no Blind reason, no "not on the sheet" tooltips.

There is no Pushed option (pushing happens from the Push button on CoC7's result card, BUILD-GUIDE Gap 3).

## Code

Files: `index.js` keeps the flyout (tabs, which one it opens on, ticks, the list style, the rows' shortcuts, options, sending) and decides which tabs to show; each tab's own code is beside it, `characteristic.js`, `skill.js` and `sanity.js` (the Sanity tab, [sanity-flyout.md](sanity-flyout.md)).

### `tabsFor(user)` (exported) and private `openingTab(tabs, selectionChanged)` (`index.js`)
- **How it works:** `tabsFor(user)`: the tabs the Check flyout shows for this user, in order. The Keeper (`user.isGM`) gets all three (`TABS`: `CHECK_TABS` from `flyouts/flyout.js`, Characteristic and Skill, then Sanity). A player gets Characteristic and Skill when the Player Rollbox allows `"checks"` (`canUse(feature)` from `player/index.js`, through `TAB_FEATURES`), and never Sanity (Sanity checks are the Keeper's; a tab without an entry in `TAB_FEATURES` is never offered to a player); with none, `renderCheckFlyout` draws nothing. `openingTab`: the tab the flyout starts on. Drawn again for a new selection (`selectionChanged`), the tab it was on; opened, the `rollboxCheckOpensOn` setting's tab (`option()`), or with "Last used" the tab chosen last. The last tab is a module variable set by every `showTab` (Characteristic before any), so it lasts until the page reloads. A tab the user doesn't have falls back to their first.
- **Connects to:** `CHECK_TABS`; `player/index.js` (`canUse`); `utils.js` (`option`); `characteristic.js`, `skill.js`, `sanity.js`.
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing. **Assumed.**

### `SETTINGS` (`index.js`, exported): the Rollbox panel section's Menus
- **How it works:** `declareSettings()` (`utils.js`) records the settings shown in the Rollbox panel section's Menus group, both personal (user scope, the personal-setting note): `rollboxCheckOpensOn` ("Check opens on": Characteristic, Skill or Last used; default Characteristic) and `rollboxListStyle` ("List style": List or Tiles; default List; hint "How characteristics and skills are shown in the flyouts: a list, or tiles to click."). The group also holds the Action flyout's spell-text settings. The flyout reads them with `option(key)`, which answers with these defaults until `settings.js` has registered them. Both are read when the flyout is drawn, so a change shows the next time it opens.
- **Connects to:** `utils.js` (`declareSettings`, `option`, `PERSONAL`); `settings.js`.
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing. **Assumed.**

### `registerCheckFlyout()` (`index.js`, exported, called from `main.js` at `ready`)
- **How it works:** Returns `false` unless `player/` `panelHere()`: the Keeper's client with `rollboxEnabled` on, or a player's with the Player Rollbox and their own switch on (the Keeper is checked first, so a player with the Player Rollbox off reads nothing else); a player's flyout shows only their allowed tabs (`tabsFor`). Otherwise `registerFlyout("check", { title: "Request a check", render: renderCheckFlyout })`. Installs no hooks.
- **Connects to:** `panel/` (`registerFlyout`); `player/index.js` (`panelHere`).
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing. **Assumed.**

### `renderCheckFlyout(body, { actors, everyone, start, selectionChanged, setTitle, open })` (exported) and `CheckFlyout` (`index.js`)
- **How it works:** `everyone` (from the menu host): nothing is selected and `actors` are every player's investigator; the flyout then lists like several characters (no values), says so in two lines (`whoLine`) and its button reads "Send to all players". `own` is set for a player (`!isKeeper()`): their own check, made rather than requested (below). The menu host calls it when the flyout opens and again when the selection changes while it is open; each call builds a new `CheckFlyout` from scratch (ticks and options start empty, the tab is `openingTab`'s), then applies `start` if given (below). Nothing selected, or a user with no tab, draws nothing and returns nothing; otherwise it returns a controller `{ apply(start) }` the menu host keeps while the flyout is open, so another click on the character sheet reaches the open flyout instead of redrawing it. `setTitle` (the menu host's) sets the heading on every `update()`: "Request a check", "Request a combined check" with two or more ticks, "Request a Sanity check" on the Sanity tab; a player's "Make a check" and "Make a combined check". `open` is the menu host's `open(id, start)`, for a row's opposed shortcut. `combinedAllowed` is `canUse("combined")` (`player/`; always true for the Keeper), read once when the flyout is built.
  - **Tabs:** the shared `tabBar` (`flyouts/flyout.js`, [flyouts.md](flyouts.md)); `showTab(id)` marks the tab (`aria-selected`, the bar's `select`), shows its panel, and swaps the parts: on Characteristic and Skill the names line, the options and the check send section; on Sanity the Sanity tab's SAN list, loss, cause and its own send section (`SanityTab`). Each is built once and hidden while the other tab shows, so switching keeps what was set on both.
  - **Sections:** a muted line with the names (`whoLine`, `rollbox-flyout-who`; with `everyone` the two short lines), then `rollbox-section` blocks for what (tabs and lists), how (options) and send; the spacing is `shared/shared.css`.
  - **Lists:** `checkChoices(actor)` from `skill-values/` ([skill-values.md](skill-values.md)), the same rows Opposed lists: with one character its characteristic and skill rows with values; with several, `checkChoices(null)`'s characteristic rows without values and `groupSkills(actors)` for the skills. `row(key, check, name, values)` draws each: with List style a `<label>` with a tick box and the values (`levelsNode`); with Tiles (`rollboxListStyle`) a button (`.rollbox-check-tile`, `aria-pressed` when ticked) with the name, the value large and "30 · 12" under it, in a grid (`list(kind)`: `.rollbox-check-tiles-characteristic`, four columns; `-skill`, three). `values` is the row itself for one character, a short note ("base 1", "40%") for an added skill, or nothing for several. Every row and tile drawn for a key is kept (`rowNodes`), and `mark(key)` shows its tick on all of them, so a skill both listed and added stays in step.
  - **Row shortcuts** (`row`, `rowAction`): a click or right click (`contextmenu`) on a row or tile goes through `rowShortcut(howClicked(event, right))` (`panel/sheet-clicks.js`, below). A plain left click on a list row is left to the tick box's own click; every other click is cancelled (`preventDefault`), so a modifier click never toggles the tick box and a right click never opens the browser's menu. Then: `tick` turns the tick on or off; `add` ticks it; `send` sends just this check as a plain Regular one (`sendOne`; the ticks and options stay; a player: makes it); `copy` (Ctrl + Left) copies this check's CoC7 roll link with the flyout's difficulty, dice, Blind and label (`copyLink`: roll-requests `requestLink` for the first selected character, then Foundry's `game.clipboard.copyPlainText`, guarded, and a "Link copied" notification; nothing ticked or sent; the Keeper's only, a player's flyout does nothing; a link that can't be made or a refused clipboard says "The roll link couldn't be copied." in the result area); `combinedNow` ticks it and sends the ticks at once with the flyout's options; `opposed` / `opposedNow` open Opposed (`opposed(key, create)`) with this check on the first selected character's side, through the host's `open("opposed", { first, picks, create })` ([opposed-flyout.md](opposed-flyout.md)), only with one or two selected; otherwise the result area says "Opposed can't be opened for this selection." A player not allowed Opposed (`canUse("opposed")` false) gets "You can't start an opposed roll." (`NO_OPPOSED`) in the result area instead, and nothing opens. Rollbox reads no keys: only the mouse event's modifier flags.
  - **Ticks** are kept in a `Map` in the order ticked, keyed by kind and name ("characteristic:str", skill-values' `skillKey` for a skill), so they survive switching tabs. Without `combinedAllowed` (a player without Combined checks) a new tick replaces the old one (`tick` clears the others first), so only one check is ever ticked; `add` and Alt + Right do the same. Each holds the check as `roll-requests/` takes it: `{ kind: "characteristic", name: "str" }` or `{ kind: "skill", name, cocid, value? }`.
  - **Options** (`optionsBlock`): the combined all/any row (`segmented`, shown with two or more ticks) with the **clear button** after it (`clearButton`, `rollbox-check-clear`: Font Awesome's `fa-arrow-rotate-left`, drawn with shared's `glyphButton` ([shared.md](shared.md)), hover text and accessible name "Unpick every check"), which unticks every ticked check on every tab (`untickAll`) and redraws, so the combined row hides and the send button greys out again, the shared Difficulty, Dice and Blind rows (`howRows`, [flyouts.md](flyouts.md)) and a Label text box. A player's (`own`): the combined row (with Combined checks allowed) and `howRows(…, { own: true })`, which draws no rows (an empty, hidden place), so no Difficulty, Dice, Blind or Label. `update()` redraws only what depends on them (pressed buttons, Blind's state, the title, the main button's words and its `data-combined` mark with two or more ticks, which Art Deco shows as "Combined · " in place of the title, the status line and the send buttons), so typing in the label or the search box keeps its focus.
  - **Blind** is disabled and shown off while two or more checks are ticked (CoC7's combined card is always public, Gap 5); the Keeper's choice comes back if they untick down to one.
  - **Status line** (the shared `sendSection`'s `ready()`): only in red, while nothing is ticked ("Tick a check to request it."), with the buttons greyed out; hidden otherwise.
- **Connects to:** `skill-values/` (`checkChoices`); `roll-requests/` (`checkRequest`, `send`, `makeCheck`, `requestLink`); Foundry's `game.clipboard.copyPlainText`; `panel/index.js` (`registerFlyout`, `rowShortcut`, `howClicked`; the menu host's `open`, `setTitle`, `everyone`); `player/index.js` (`canUse("checks")`, `canUse("combined")`, `canUse("opposed")`); `flyouts/flyout.js` (`CHECK_TABS`, `OWN_RESULT`, `button`, `glyphButton`, `howRows`, `levels`, `levelsNode`, `line`, `runSend`, `segmented`, `sendLabel`, `sendSection`, `showResult`, `tabBar`, `whoLine`); `utils.js` (`actorKey`, `element`, `isKeeper`, `option`).
- **Could interact poorly with:** Foundry's global `button` and `input` styles: every rule is scoped under `.rollbox-panel` and Foundry loads module styles into its `modules` cascade layer, after its own (BUILD-GUIDE "The panel and CSS"). **Assumed.** An update to a character while the flyout is open (e.g. a skill improved) doesn't redraw the flyout, so its values are those of when it was drawn (the menu host's rule, so a half-filled flyout isn't lost). **Assumed.**

### Sending (`CheckFlyout#request`, `#send`, `#sendOne`, `#dispatch`)
- **How it works:** `request()` is `checkRequest({ checks, all, difficulty, dice, blind, label })`; Blind is sent only with one check, and never for a player (`own`), whose label is always empty. The Label goes after the check's name in the link, "Hard Spot Hidden · notice the knife" or "Spot Hidden + Listen · notice the knife"; `roll-requests/` does that ([roll-requests.md](roll-requests.md)), so the flyout passes the text as typed. `sendOne(check)` is `checkRequest({ checks: [check] })`: Regular, no dice, not blind, no label. The request goes to every character the flyout is for (every investigator with `everyone`). The button is greyed out while nothing is ticked and while a send is running (`runSend`, no double sends). Its words are `sendLabel(actors, { everyone })`, or "Make check" for a player. The Keeper's click → `send(request, actors)` (whispers, or private rolls for NPCs); there is no other mode. The result under the button is `showResult` with "Check": "Check sent to:" with "Alice (Player 1)", "Bob (Player 2)" on their own lines; "Rolled for you:" with "Guard"; "Not sent:" with "Carl: CoC7 can't find Pilot (Boat)"; or "CoC7's roll links aren't available; nothing was sent." The flyout stays open with its ticks, so the same request can be sent again.
  - **A player's Make check** (`dispatch` with `own`): `makeCheck(request, actor)` (roll-requests, [roll-requests.md](roll-requests.md)) for their own investigator: CoC7's own actor check with `fastForward` on the player's client rolls at once, with no window, and posts its card in the table's roll mode; a combined check posts CoC7's combined card. Not awaited past CoC7 being asked. The result is `OWN_RESULT` ("CoC7's check, in chat:" and the name), or "Not sent:" with the reason.
- **What each request does** (player characters, NPCs, combined, missing skills): [roll-requests.md](roll-requests.md), "What happens for each request".
- **Connects to:** `roll-requests/index.js` (`checkRequest`, `send`, `makeCheck`).
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing beyond `send()`'s and `makeCheck()`'s own notes. **Assumed.**

### A starting state from the character sheet (`CheckFlyout#apply`)
- **How it works:** `apply({ tab, tick, add, plain, send })`, from a click on a characteristic or SAN on the character sheet (`shortcut`, below); through `renderCheckFlyout`'s `start` when the flyout opens, or the controller when it is already open. `tab: "sanity"` shows the Sanity tab and hands `plain` and `send` to `SanityTab#apply` ([sanity-flyout.md](sanity-flyout.md)); nothing else is read. Otherwise `tick` is a Characteristic row's key ("characteristic:str"). Unless `add`, every other tick is cleared first (a left click: only that one ticked; again while open switches it, keeping the options); with `add` (Alt + right click) it is added to the ticks, so two or more make a combined roll; without `combinedAllowed` (a player without Combined checks) `add` clears the others too, so only that one is ticked. The Characteristic tab is shown. `plain` puts the options back to a Regular check with no dice, not blind and no label, as CoC7's sheet rolls a Shift click; `send` (true) then sends the ticks (a player: makes the check). A key that isn't a Characteristic row ticks nothing.
- **Connects to:** `panel/` (the menu host's `open(id, start)` and controller); `#send`; `SanityTab#apply`.
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing. **Assumed.** A table check passed every characteristic shortcut; the Sanity-tab starts are untested at the table.

### `shortcut(value, how)`, `rowShortcut(how)` and `howClicked(event, right)` (`panel/sheet-clicks.js`)
- **How it works:** Pure. `shortcut` maps a click on the character sheet's values to a flyout and its start (the panel's `onValue` calls it, [panel.md](panel.md)); `rowShortcut` maps a click on a Check row or tile to an action; `howClicked` reads the mouse event's `shiftKey`, `ctrlKey` or `metaKey`, and `altKey`. Both maps are CoC7's sheet shortcuts one for one (its `CoC7.ToolTipSkill`, `ToolTipKeeperSkill`, `ToolTipSanity`, `ToolTipKeeperSanity`), except that a plain Left click on a row keeps its menu meaning (tick it). SAN and Luck are never a side of an opposed roll or part of a combined one: their opposed and combined shortcuts open their own place.

    | Click | Characteristic on the sheet | SAN on the sheet | Luck on the sheet | Row or tile in Check |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | Left | Check, only this ticked | Check, Sanity tab | Other on Luck | tick / untick (`tick`) |
    | Shift + Left | Check, only this ticked, sent at once as a plain Regular check. While Opposed has a side waiting: fills that side and makes the card (the panel's `fillsWaitingSide`) | Check, Sanity tab, a plain SAN roll sent at once (CoC7's SAN attribute check, as its sheet's Shift click; no Sanity card) | Other on Luck, a plain Luck check sent at once | just this check sent now, Regular (`send`) |
    | Ctrl + Left | as Left: Check, only this ticked | Check, Sanity tab | Other on Luck | copy its roll link (`copy`, CoC7's copy-a-link shortcut; the Keeper's only) |
    | Shift + Ctrl + Left | as Left: Check, only this ticked | Check, Sanity tab | Other on Luck | tick / untick (`tick`) |
    | Alt + Left (with or without Shift or Ctrl) | nothing | Check, Sanity tab | nothing | nothing |
    | Right | Opposed, this character as the first side (`{ opposed }`, the panel's `openOpposed`) | Check, Sanity tab | Other on Luck | Opposed, this as the first side (`opposed`) |
    | Shift + Right | the same, the card made once both sides have a check | Check, Sanity tab | Other on Luck | Opposed, card made once both sides have a check (`opposedNow`) |
    | Alt + Right | Check, added to the ticks | Check, Sanity tab | Other on Luck | added to the ticks (`add`) |
    | Shift + Alt + Right | added and the ticks sent at once | Check, Sanity tab | Other on Luck | added and the ticks sent at once (`combinedNow`) |
    | Ctrl + Right | nothing | Check, Sanity tab | nothing | nothing |
    | Ctrl + Alt + Right | Check, added to the ticks (Alt is read before Ctrl) | Check, Sanity tab | Other on Luck | added to the ticks (`add`; Alt is read before Ctrl, as on CoC7's sheet) |

  Ctrl is Ctrl or Cmd (`howClicked`); Alt is Option on a Mac. The rows' table applies to characteristics and skills alike. Rollbox copies no links, so CoC7's Ctrl copy-link shortcuts open the flyout as a plain click on the sheet and do nothing on a row. A player's sheet click does only what the Player Rollbox allows (`offersClick`, [player.md](player.md)); a player without Combined checks gets one tick at a time, and a row's right click without Opposed says "You can't start an opposed roll."
- **Connects to:** `panel/index.js` (`onValue`, `fillsWaitingSide`, `openOpposed`); `CheckFlyout#apply`, `#row`, `#rowAction`; the Other flyout (`{ section: "luck" }`).
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing. **Assumed.**

### `characteristicPanel(flyout)` (`characteristic.js`, exported)
- **How it works:** The Characteristic tab: one tickable row (or tile) per characteristic (`flyout.choices.characteristic`), with its values for one character. Builds with the flyout's own `panel()`, `list("characteristic")` and `row()`, so ticks, shortcuts and the list style stay the flyout's.
- **Connects to:** `CheckFlyout` (`index.js`).
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing. **Assumed.**

### `SkillTab` (`skill.js`, exported): the skill list, its search, `otherSkill(text, known)` (not exported) and "+ Add" (`filter`, `addOther`, `addOtherWithValue`, `addExtra`)
- **How it works:** `new SkillTab(flyout).build()` returns the Skill tab's panel: the search (the shared `searchBox`, "Search skills"), the list (one character: `flyout.choices.skill`, trained first, the divider "Untrained · base value" before the first untrained skill, then the rest at base value; several: `groupSkills`, A–Z, no values; Tiles: the same as a three-column grid) and the value line asked for an unknown base. Skills added by "+ Add" go in a list of their own under it (`.rollbox-check-extra`, rows or tiles). It ticks through the flyout's `tick()` and `ticks`. The search is the shared `filterRows`; when it matches no listed row, the list's first row is "+ Add <typed name>" (a button), with the base values for one character when a group base is known (skill-values' `withLevels`). Clicking it, or Enter in the search while it shows, adds the skill, then empties the search so the whole list shows again:
  1. A name the Skill tab already lists (skill-values' `findSkillRow`: accents, case and spaces ignored): that row is ticked (only reachable by Enter, since a listed name matches the search).
  2. Otherwise a specialisation of an open-ended skill (skill-values' `groupBase`: Art/Craft 5, Language 1, Lore 1, Pilot 1, Science 1, Survival 10; Keeper Rulebook p. 56): added as a ticked row under the list showing "base 1", sent as `{ kind: "skill", name, value }`.
  3. Otherwise the Keeper is asked "No base value known for X. Value to roll an NPC at:" (a number box, 0–100, and Add), and the skill is added at that value ("40%"); a value out of range says "Type a value from 0 to 100." in red.
  The value is used only for an NPC that lacks the skill (rolled privately at it). A player character's request goes by name: CoC7 adds the skill to the sheet if it can find it, and if it can't, `send()` skips that character and tells the Keeper.
- **Connects to:** `skill-values/` (`comparable`, `findSkillRow`, `groupBase`, `groupSkills`, `skillKey`, `withLevels`); `flyouts/flyout.js` (`button`, `filterRows`, `levelParts`, `searchBox`); `roll-requests/` (the check's `value`).
- **Could interact poorly with:** a typed name holding `, : [ ] { } # &` can't go in a CoC7 link; `send()` skips it with a notice. **Assumed.**

### `check.css`
- **How it works:** Listed in `module.json` `styles` after `shared/shared.css`, which gives the buttons, tabs, rows of buttons, lists with tick boxes (`rollbox-list`, `rollbox-row`), text boxes, labelled lines, hints, the divider, sections, the Blind toggle, the loss line, the dice picker, the status line, the action buttons and the result line, and keeps anything `[hidden]` hidden. This file keeps what only the Check flyout has: the tab row and its tabs, the "+ Add" row, the row's name and values, the value box for a typed skill, the option buttons' padding so "Extreme" fits beside the Blind toggle, the italic hint and the red error line; the tiles (`.rollbox-check-tiles`, four or three `minmax(0, 1fr)` columns so the flyout keeps its width, names cut with "…", a ticked tile in the pressed fill with the accent border); and the Sanity tab's SAN list (scrolling past about 140px) and presets. Every rule is scoped under `.rollbox-panel` and uses the panel's colour variables. The lists (rows or tiles) scroll past about 230px; the flyout itself scrolls past 70% of the window height.
- **Connects to:** `panel/panel.css`, `shared/shared.css`; the theme stylesheets (`panel/themes/`).
- **Could interact poorly with:** Foundry's light interface theme: the flyout follows Rollbox's own Theme setting ([panel.md](panel.md)). A Foundry update that stops sizing its tick-box glyph with `--checkbox-size`: the lists would scroll a little early. **Assumed.**

## Hooks, overrides, keybindings
- Hooks: none (the menu host calls `render`).
- Class overrides: none.
- Keybindings: none. Enter in the skill search adds the typed skill while "+ Add" shows; there is no form, so Enter never submits anything else. The row shortcuts read only a mouse event's modifier flags; `contextmenu` is cancelled on Rollbox's own rows and tiles only.

## Settings
Registered by `settings.js` in the Rollbox panel section's Menus group: `rollboxCheckOpensOn` and `rollboxListStyle`, personal (`SETTINGS`, above). The section greys out while its feature switch, Rollbox panel (`rollboxEnabled`, in the Features box at the top of the page), is off. A player's tabs follow the Player Rollbox settings through `canUse` (`rollboxPlayerChecks`, and `rollboxPlayerCombined` for combined checks; read by `player/index.js`, not here). `rollboxPlayerSanity` stays registered (hidden from Configure Settings) but gives a player nothing: they have no Sanity tab.
