# Assist (`scripts/flyouts/other/assist.js`)

The Other flyout's **Assist** tab ([other-flyout.md](other-flyout.md); Assist has no button of its own): Physical Human Limits, Keeper Rulebook p. 88–89 (summary p. 407). Several characters put a characteristic together (any of the eight; Physical Human Limits uses STR and SIZ) against something too big for any one of them: a rafter to lift, a door to hold shut, a creature to brace against; or one character against a typed value, which then sets the difficulty (p. 83, 88: e.g. Fist of Yog-Sothoth, Use CON against a typed STR). Rollbox works out who helps and who rolls, asks the rollers for an ordinary characteristic check at the worked-out difficulty, and posts one public Assist card saying who helps and who rolls. It doesn't read the rolls back: the Keeper reads the rollers' cards, and one success is enough. The Keeper's tab is never greyed out: with fewer than two characters in it (nothing selected included) it offers **⌖ Add a character**, Rollbox's target picker. A player's Other flyout offers Assist when the Keeper allows it (`canUse("assist")`): a player can only **help another investigator** (`player-assist.js`, `assist-group.js`, below). Files: `assist.js` (the Keeper's tab, the card, the character-card column), `assist-split.js` (the maths: who helps and who rolls), `assist-group.js` (a player's group on the helped investigator, the Keeper's side of joining and leaving), `player-assist.js` (a player's tab), `assist.css`. No settings. User guide: [Other](../guide/other.md).

**Status key:** *Confirmed* only when the build guide's Internal API registry or changelog says so. Everything is **assumed** unless stated otherwise.

## What the Keeper sees

A tab like the other flyouts' tabs, in sections with space between them. Every control is inside it: the character cards beside it only show each character's value (below, `assistColumns`). Top to bottom:

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Number to beat | a large editable value line ([shared.md](shared.md)), starting at 0. With targets marked it shows their Against values added together, captioned "STR against Zombie A + Zombie B"; with none, the typed number, captioned "STR against SIZ" (Use against Against). Typed text that isn't a whole number 0–999 turns red. Typing a number unmarks every target; unmarking the last target brings the typed number back |
| Use, Against | each the eight characteristics as tiles, four to a row (the Check flyout's Tiles style), under the small labels "Use" (what the characters put in, STR at first) and "Against" (which value of a target counts, STR at first; with no target it only names the typed number's characteristic). A marked target without the Against value stops counting until Against is switched back |
| Characters | every selected character in character-card order, then those added with the picker, then the helpers waiting in a selected investigator's group: a narrow target button on the left, just wide enough for its crosshair (`fa-crosshairs`, accessible label "<name>: target"), the name and the value that counts (the Use value, or the Against value for a target), and a ✕ on the right that takes that character out of the assist: the row goes, until the flyout opens afresh or the picker adds them again. With fewer than two characters in, a last row **⌖ Add a character**. Only the target button marks or unmarks a character (and typing a number unmarks all): a target's row is tinted coral, it takes no part, and the targets' Against values add up to the number to beat. CoC7 creatures (actor type `creature`) start marked when they are selected (a character without the Against value isn't marked, and its target button is greyed out); the Keeper can unmark them. A character without the Use value shows "—" and takes no part |
| Result | "Assisting": one line per helper, name and "−20" ("No one" when nobody helps); "Rolling": one line per roller, name and "STR 60"; then one highlighted line, "Regular STR" on the left and "90% one succeeds" on the right. Or, instead, one red line saying what's missing or impossible, with Send greyed out: "Type the STR to beat." (typed text refused), "Target someone, or type the STR to beat." (no target and 0), "Include at least one more character." (nobody left to take part after taking people out and marking targets), "Add a character who takes part." (nobody in at all), "Too heavy even together." (no split works). One character against a typed number is enough. The characteristic in them is the Against one |
| Label | optional text, placeholder "Label (optional), e.g. lift the rafter" |
| Send | the send button (highlighted), named for the rollers with the shared `sendLabel` ("Send to Player (Helen)", "Send to each (2)", "Roll for Belinda" / "Roll for NPCs"; "Send" while something is missing), and Cancel; then the result, one name per line: "Assist sent to:" / "Helen (Test)", "Rolled for you:" / "Belinda", then "Assist card posted." |

Selecting or deselecting tokens while it's open redraws it at once, keeping Use and Against, the typed number, who is taken out or marked and the label (the Other flyout passes the redraw on as `selectionChanged`); a newly selected creature starts marked. A character deselected and selected again counts as new for its mark. No Blind, no bonus or penalty dice: the difficulty is worked out.

## What a player sees

A player helps any other character: an investigator, an NPC or a creature. **A, nothing yet:** "Help another character. Target who you're helping; the Keeper sets what you're up against." and one button, **⌖ Target who you're helping** (only their own character is refused: "You can't help yourself: target who you're helping."). **B, helping, waiting for the Keeper:** "Helping Cecil / Waiting for the Keeper", "With Cecil" and each helper with their STR ("Helen (you) STR 60"), "The Keeper sets what you're up against and who rolls.", and **Stop helping** (the investigator being helped sees "Being helped" and the helpers, without Stop). **C, sent, a helper:** the difficulty ("Hard STR", the label under it), "Assisting" and "Rolling" with their values, "One success is enough", "Cecil rolls. Nothing for you to do." **D, sent, a roller:** the same with **Roll Hard STR**, CoC7's own check made at once on their client (as Make check); "Rolled" once their card is in. No number, Use or Against, and no character list.

## Code

### `renderAssist(body, { actors, close, selectionChanged, cardChanged, targeting, pickTarget })` and `assistGroupChanged(actor, changes)` (`assist.js`, exported)
- **How it works:** The Other flyout's Assist tab (`OtherFlyout#show`). With `targeting` (a player's flyout) it draws `renderPlayerAssist` instead (below). The Keeper's: builds the whole tab from scratch. Its choices (Use, Against, the typed number, the sets of taken-out, target and already-seen characters by `actorKey`, the label, and the characters added with the picker, `extra`) live in one module-level object: new when the flyout opens (`selectionChanged` false), kept when it is drawn again for a new selection, so shift-clicking tokens updates it at once. The characters are the selection, then `extra`, then each one's waiting group helpers (`assist-group.js` `waitingHelpers`), each once (`uniqueActors`), less those taken out. **Add a character** awaits `pickTarget({ prompt: "Target a character to add", useCurrent: false })` (default: `player/targeting.js`, Foundry's own Target tool); the targeted token's character joins (and is no longer taken out); cancelling (null) or a failure (logged) changes nothing. Every change calls the context's `cardChanged()`, so the character cards' column follows. `assistGroupChanged(actor, changes)` (the Other flyout's `updateActor` hook) draws the open tab again, keeping its choices, when an update touches `flags.keepers-rollbox.assistGroup` (a player joined or left, or a sent group was cleared; `assist-group.js` `groupChanged` tests for the key, so a removal, which Foundry v14 sends as the key with a `ForcedDeletion` value, counts, and another flag merely holding the word doesn't); it returns true when it drew, and the hook then skips `playerAssistChanged`.
- **Connects to:** the Other flyout (`index.js`), which passes the menu host's `selectionChanged` and `cardChanged` ([panel.md](panel.md)); `player/index.js` (`pickTarget`); `assist-group.js`, `player-assist.js`.
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing known. A redraw while the Keeper is typing in the label loses the focus (the tab is rebuilt), but not the text. **Assumed.**

### `assistChoices(characters, against)` (`assist-split.js`, exported; re-exported from `assist.js`)
- **How it works:** Pure functions, no Foundry or CoC7 calls (skill-values' `withLevels`, which they use, is arithmetic too). `characters` are `{ value, … }` in character-card order (the tab passes `{ actor, name, value }`); one without a number value takes no part. They are sorted lowest value first, with equal values keeping their order (a stable sort). For each number of helpers from none up to all but one: each helper takes their value off; what's left must stay above 0 (once it doesn't, more helpers only take more off, so the search stops) and every remaining character must be able to try it, i.e. what's left is no more than 100 + their value (p. 88). The difficulty is shared's `difficultyFor(left)` ([shared.md](shared.md)): below 50 Regular, 50+ Hard, 90+ Extreme. The chance that at least one roller succeeds is 1 − the product of each roller's chance to fail, each roller's chance being their value, half or fifth (skill-values' `withLevels`, rounded down as CoC7 does), out of 100. The split with the best chance is used; a chance within 1e-9 of a better one counts as equal, so ties go to fewer helpers. Returns `{ helpers, rollers, left, difficulty, chance }`, or `null` when no split works ("Too heavy even together"), when nothing is to be beaten (0 or less), or with nobody taking part.
- **Rulebook rafter** (p. 88–89): 150 against Harvey 20, Cecil 40, Martin 45, Helen 60, Belinda 75 gives Harvey, Cecil and Martin helping, Helen and Belinda rolling at Regular with 45 left: 90% that one succeeds (1 − 0.40 × 0.25). With one or two helpers 130 or 90 is left (Extreme, about 37% and 32%); with four, nothing is left.
- **No helpers is a split too:** when everyone can try it alone and that gives the best chance (e.g. against 20, or two characters bracing against something light), nobody helps and everyone rolls; the result then reads "Assisting: No one" and the card says "Helping: no one." ([DESIGN.md, "Assist"](DESIGN.md#assist)).
- **The chance is an estimate** for choosing and for the Keeper's eye: it ignores criticals, fumbles and the 01 that always succeeds. CoC7 rolls and judges every real roll. Rollbox writes no dice or success-level code.
- **Connects to:** `AssistFlyout#plan`; skill-values' `withLevels`; `shared/` (`difficultyFor`, which combat's notice cards use too, [combat.md](combat.md)). `characters` are the participants only: targets and left-out characters are never passed.
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing (pure). **Confirmed** by tests only.

### `STATS` and `valueOf(actor, key)` (`assist-split.js`, exported)
- **How it works:** `STATS` is the eight characteristics in sheet order, `{ str: "STR", con: "CON", siz: "SIZ", dex: "DEX", app: "APP", int: "INT", pow: "POW", edu: "EDU" }` (any characteristic; Physical Human Limits uses STR and SIZ): the Use and Against tiles, and the words on the caption, the result, the column, the card and a player's view. `valueOf(actor, key)` is a character's value of one, read through `skill-values/` `characteristics`, or null when the sheet has none (not a finite number). Both live beside `assistChoices`, so the Keeper's Assist (`assist.js`) and a player's (`player-assist.js`) read them from one place and those two files don't import each other in a circle.
- **Connects to:** `AssistFlyout`, `assistColumns`, `assistCardHtml` (`assist.js`); `renderPlayerAssist` (`player-assist.js`); `skill-values/` (`characteristics`).
- **Could interact poorly with:** a characteristic CoC7 or another module stores as text rather than a number reads as missing ("—"). **Assumed.**

### `assistColumns(actors)` (`assist.js`, exported; the Other flyout's `columns` while the Assist tab is shown)
- **How it works:** The character cards' value column while Assist is open: one column headed by the Use characteristic ("STR"), showing each participant's (and each left-out character's) Use value and each target that counts its Against value (`countsAsTarget`); when a target counts and Against isn't Use, the header names both, Use/Against ("STR/SIZ"). A missing value shows "—". It reads the same module-level choices as the tab. The character cards have no controls.
- **Connects to:** `otherColumns` (`index.js`), then `MenuHost#columns` and the panel's character cards ([panel.md](panel.md)).
- **Could interact poorly with:** nothing outside Rollbox. **Assumed** (the "STR/SIZ" header reaches left into the title's room).

### `AssistFlyout` (`assist.js`)
- **How it works:** Built for the characters with the carried choices. The constructor forgets characters no longer present (dropped from the three sets) and, for each character not seen before, adds it to `seen` and, if it is a CoC7 creature (`actor.type === "creature"`, CoC7 `system.json` `documentTypes.Actor`) with the Against value, marks it as a target.
  - `whatSection()`: the number line (`valueLine` with `againstNumber`: a whole number 0–999, tidied, "007" → "7"; empty counts as 0) with its caption, the Use and Against tiles (`statTiles`: eight `rollbox-check-tile` buttons in a `rollbox-check-tiles-characteristic` grid labelled "Use" or "Against", the picked one `aria-pressed`), and one row per character (`nameRow`: the target button `rollbox-assist-target`, the name and value, the ✕ `rollbox-assist-remove`, both drawn with shared's `glyphButton`, [shared.md](shared.md), taken through `flyouts/flyout.js`, each with its accessible name); with fewer than two characters, `addRow` (Add a character, a `glyphButton` with the crosshair and the words).
  - `remove(actor)` (✕) puts the character in the taken-out set, unmarks them, drops them from `extra`, and draws the tab again; `mark(key)` toggles the target set; `typed()` stores the typed text and unmarks every target, unless the line just shows the targets' sum (clicking away from it changes nothing). `countsAsTarget` is the one rule for a target that counts: marked, not taken out, and with the Against value.
  - A right click on a character's row (`nameRow`) targets that character's token (shared's `rightClickTargets`). That is Foundry's target; Assist's own target marking stays on the row's crosshair.
  - `plan()` reads who takes part (everyone in who isn't marked as a target and has the Use value, through `skill-values/`' `characteristics`), the targets and the number to beat (their Against values added together, or the typed number; null while the typed text is refused), and calls `assistChoices` when someone takes part and the number is above 0.
  - `update()` marks the toggles and rows, writes the line and caption, draws the result (`drawOutcome`) or the red line (`missing()`, in that order: refused text, nothing to beat, nobody taking part, no split), names the send button (`sendLabel` of the rollers once there is a split and nothing is missing, else "Send"), greys it out while something is missing, then calls `cardChanged()`.
- **Adding the targets together** is the Keeper's ruling: the rule only describes investigators combining (p. 88–89).
- **Sending** (`send()`): `checkRequest({ checks: [{ kind: "characteristic", name: <the Use characteristic> }], difficulty, label })` (any of the eight, `str` … `edu`), sent with `send(request, rollers, { involved: helpers })` ([roll-requests.md](roll-requests.md)): each roller with a player gets CoC7's whispered request ("… make a Hard STR · lift the rafter roll" with Use STR; Regular reads "STR · …"), each NPC roller is rolled privately for the Keeper, as for any check. `involved` names the helpers, so a player's request through the Keeper is accepted when the player owns any character taking part. With no label typed the request's label is "Assist", so players read "STR · Assist". Then, if at least one roller was asked, rolled or rolls from their panel, the Assist card (`postCard`), and each investigator here with a group gets the sent assist on it, replacing one sent before, or has it cleared when this assist leaves it out (`tellGroups` → `assist-group.js` `markSent`, `clearGroup`), for its players' views. A roller `send()` skipped isn't named on the card; the result says why. The result (`runSend` and `showResult`, [flyouts.md](flyouts.md)): "Assist sent to:" with each player's roller on its own line, "Rolled for you:" with each NPC roller, "Not sent:" with reasons, then "Assist card posted."
- **Cancel** clears every players' assist group among the characters shown (`clearGroup`, waiting or sent), then closes the Other flyout (the host's `close()`); it is marked `data-keep`, so it is never greyed out. Closing the flyout any other way keeps the groups.
- **One way to roll each** (`send()`): when a players' group is in the assist (`groupsIn`), a roller whose player will see the group's Roll button (`rollsOnPanel`: a player owns them, the Player Rollbox and its Assist feature on, and one of those players has their own panel on, `player/` `panelOnFor`) gets no chat request; the other rollers get CoC7's chat request. The result adds "Rolls from their Rollbox panel: <names>." `tellGroups` then marks each group taken in as sent (replacing a group sent before) and clears a group the new assist leaves out.
- **Connects to:** `roll-requests/` (`checkRequest`, `send`); `assist-split.js` (`assistChoices`, `STATS`, `valueOf`); `assist-group.js` (`groupOf`, `waitingHelpers`, `markSent`, `clearGroup`, `rollsOnPanel`); `flyouts/flyout.js` (`actorTag`, `button`, `glyphButton`, `noneSent`, `runSend`, `sendLabel`, `sendSection`, `showResult`, `DIFFICULTY_LABELS`); `shared/index.js` (`valueLine`, `captioned`, `valueBox`, `rightClickTargets`, `statusLine` for the red line under the result, passed to `sendSection`); `utils.js` (`element`, `actorKey`, `NO_VALUE`, `escapeHtml`, `uniqueActors`, `warn`).
- **Could interact poorly with:** CoC7's own handling of each request is as for the Check flyout (BUILD-GUIDE "CoC7 integration"): a player can change the difficulty in CoC7's roll dialog (Gap 9). **Assumed.** Creature sheets made by other modules or importers with another actor type aren't marked automatically; the Keeper marks them. **Assumed.** Two tokens of one linked actor count as one character (the panel keeps each actor once), so two linked "Zombie" tokens give one target; unlinked tokens are separate. **Assumed.**

### `assistCardHtml({ use, against, label, left, difficulty, helpers, rollers })` (exported) and `postCard(card)` (`assist.js`)
- **How it works:** The public Assist card: "Assist", the label (italic, if any; the Keeper's own text only, not the "Assist" fallback), "STR against SIZ: 45 left, **Regular STR**." (Use against Against), "Helping: Harvey, Cecil, Martin." (or "no one"), "Rolling: Helen, Belinda.", and "One success is enough." Every name and the label go through `escapeHtml`. `postCard` posts it with `ChatMessage.create({ speaker: { alias: <the sending user's name> }, content })`, with no `whisper`, so everyone sees it: as the user who sent it, the Keeper or a player, never a selected token (the alias). It is static: Rollbox doesn't watch or read the rolls. The card doesn't show the number they're up against or name the targets (a creature's STR stays the Keeper's), only what's left.
- **Connects to:** Foundry's `ChatMessage.create` (public API); `assist.css` styles it in the chat log (`.rollbox-assist-card`, outside the panel, so every client styles it).
- **Could interact poorly with:** chat-styling modules and CoC7's chat CSS: the card uses plain `h3` and `p`. **Assumed.** Modules that change every chat message's speaker. **Assumed.**

### `assist-group.js`: `groupOf`, `standing`, `waitingHelpers`, `joinForPlayer`, `leaveForPlayer`, `helpersInGroup`, `markSent`, `clearGroup`, `rollsOnPanel`, `onRollerCard`, `registerAssistGroups`, `joinGroup`, `leaveGroup`, `canBeHelped`, `groupChanged`; private `GROUP_FLAG`
- **How it works:** A player's group lives in Rollbox's flag on the helped character, `flags.keepers-rollbox.assistGroup` (an unlinked token's character keeps it in its token's delta): `{ helpers: [actorUuid…], sent: null | { use, difficulty, label, helpers: [{uuid, name, value}], rollers: [{uuid, name, value}], rolled: [actorUuid…], at } }`. Every client reads it (`groupOf`; `standing(actor)` finds where an investigator stands: helping, being helped, or named in a sent group). The groups are looked for on the world's actors and on every scene's unlinked tokens' own characters (`holders`: `game.scenes`, `TokenDocument#actorLink` false, `TokenDocument#actor`), so a creature placed from a shared sheet can be helped. A player can't write another investigator's flags, so joining and leaving go through Rollbox's relay to the Keeper's client (`joinGroup` → relay type `assistJoin`, `leaveGroup` → `assistLeave`; [roll-requests.md](roll-requests.md), relay). On the Keeper's client (`registerAssistGroups`, from `main.js` at ready, even with the Keeper's panel off): `joinForPlayer` checks the Player Rollbox and its Assist feature are on, that the helper is the sending player's own investigator (`player/` `ownCharacter`, by Foundry's sender id) and the helped one any other character (`canBeHelped`: any actor with a uuid but their own), takes the helper out of any other waiting group and adds them (a group already sent starts afresh); `leaveForPlayer` takes the player's investigator out of every waiting group, with the same Player Rollbox and Assist check as joining. Both read that check through `player/` `playerFeatureOn("assist")`, which reads the same on every client. `helpersInGroup(rollers, helpers, user)` is registered with `roll-requests/` (`registerHelperCheck`) for a player's relayed check with helpers: true only when each roller stands in a group (`standing`) whose helpers (waiting, or the sent ones) hold every listed helper and the sending player's own investigator, the one they joined with. Helpers are found with utils' `actorFromUuid` (without waiting, a token giving its actor, null for one that is gone or a lookup that throws). `markSent` (the Keeper's Assist sending) writes `sent`. `onRollerCard` (`createChatMessage` and `updateChatMessage`, active Keeper only) counts a roller's Assist roll: their CoC7 check card (`roll-requests/` `checkCardActor`: the message's CoC7 `load` flag names it a check card and gives its actor) with a rolled result (`checkResult`, through CoC7's `game.CoC7.messageResults`) of the group's characteristic (`isCheckOf(result, sent.use)`: the result's key is that characteristic's short name, `str`, `pow`…); another check rolled meanwhile (a Spot Hidden) and CoC7's standby card not yet rolled don't count, and a standby card counts once it is rolled. The group is cleared once every roller has rolled. `groupChanged(changes)`: whether an `updateActor`'s changes hold the flag's key under `flags.keepers-rollbox` (set, or removed with a `ForcedDeletion` value), for the two `…Changed` redraws.
- **Connects to:** `roll-requests/` (`askKeeper`, `onKeeperRequest`, `registerHelperCheck`; `checkCardActor` and `isCheckOf` from its `check-cards.js`, `checkResult` and `ownersOf` from its `coc7.js`), `player/` (`ownCharacter`, `playerFeatureOn`, `panelOnFor`), `utils.js` (`actorFromUuid`, `dotted`, `isKeeper`, `isActiveKeeper`, `warn`), the Keeper's tab (`groupOf`, `waitingHelpers`, `markSent`, `clearGroup`, `rollsOnPanel`) and `player-assist.js` (`standing`, `canBeHelped`, `joinGroup`, `leaveGroup`). Every write of the flag goes through one private `writeGroup` (Foundry's `Actor#setFlag`, or `unsetFlag` to clear it; a failure is logged and returns false), and `leaveAll` takes a helper out of every waiting group through it.
- **Clearing:** besides every roller's card, `clearGroup(helped)` (Keeper only) unsets the flag when the Keeper presses Cancel in the Assist for that investigator or sends a new assist that leaves the group out; a new assist that takes it in replaces the sent one (`markSent`). No timer. `rollsOnPanel(actor)`: whether a roller rolls from their panel's Roll button instead of a chat request: the Player Rollbox and its Assist feature on, and at least one of the character's player owners (`ownersOf`: CoC7's `actor.owners` without the Keeper) has their own "Rollbox panel" on, read on the Keeper's client through `player/` `panelOnFor`. A character no player owns, or whose player owners have all turned their panel off, has no Roll button to use, so it gets CoC7's chat request.
- **Could interact poorly with:** a sent group whose roller never rolls stays on the flag until the Keeper presses Cancel or sends a new assist for that investigator, or someone joins the group again (it starts afresh). A roller whose player is offline has no chat request to come back to; they roll from their panel when they log in. **Assumed.** Unlinked investigator tokens: the group is on the token's own actor. **Assumed.**

### `renderPlayerAssist(body, { actors, pickTarget })`, `playerAssistChanged(actor, changes)` (`player-assist.js`)
- **How it works:** A player's Assist tab for their own investigator (`actors[0]`), drawn from `standing`: A (`pickView`), B (`waitingView`; its helpers found with utils' `actorFromUuid`, so a deleted token's uuid is skipped, not thrown), C/D (`sentView`), as above. **Target who you're helping** awaits `pickTarget({ prompt, useCurrent: false, restore: true })`: the next token the player clicks (a token already targeted isn't used; their targets are put back after); pressed again while it waits (`choosing`), it cancels the pick (`player/` `cancelPick`), as Escape does; it refuses only their own character (`canBeHelped`), and asks the Keeper's client to join (`joinGroup`); a refusal or no answer is one line under the view. **Stop helping** asks to leave (`leaveGroup`). **Roll <difficulty> <stat>** makes the player's own check at once (`roll-requests` `makeCheck` with the sent characteristic and difficulty: CoC7's actor check with `fastForward`, [roll-requests.md](roll-requests.md)). `playerAssistChanged` (the Other flyout's `updateActor` hook) draws the open tab again when an assist group changes (`groupChanged`, as above), so the view follows the Keeper.
- **Connects to:** `assist-group.js` (`standing`, `canBeHelped`, `joinGroup`, `leaveGroup`), `roll-requests/` (`checkRequest`, `makeCheck`), `flyouts/flyout.js` (`button`, `DIFFICULTY_LABELS`), `assist-split.js` (`STATS`, `valueOf`), `player/` (`cancelPick`), `utils.js` (`actorFromUuid`, `dotted`, `element`, `glyph` for the crosshair, `warn`). `assist.js` calls it; it doesn't import `assist.js`.
- **Could interact poorly with:** the roller can also roll from the whispered request in chat (CoC7's link): two cards if both are used; the group counts the first. **Assumed.**

### `assist.css`
- **How it works:** Listed in `module.json` `styles` after `shared.css`, which gives the generic button look (`rollbox-btn`) to the tiles, target and ✕ buttons, the label box, the result line, the sections, value line, status line and action buttons. Tab rules are under `.rollbox-panel` and use only the panel's `--rollbox-*` variables, so the theme, Panel colour, transparency and size apply, with the theme's coral (`--rollbox-target`, [panel.md](panel.md)) for targets. A target row is tinted coral. The Use and Against tiles take the Check flyout's tile look (`check.css`) with the name in the strong text colour. The target and ✕ buttons are narrow (`flex: none`, the icon with a little padding each side), as tall as the name box; the ✕ turns red on hover. The character list shows ten names before it scrolls, sized by rows, not pixels: `--rollbox-row-height` on the list is a button's border, padding and one line (so it follows Foundry's font size), every row is exactly that tall, and the list's `max-height` is `10 × --rollbox-row-height + 9 × --rollbox-row-gap`. The result's difficulty-and-chance line is tinted with the accent. Add a character (`rollbox-assist-add`) and a player's Target who you're helping (`rollbox-assist-help`) put the crosshair beside the words; a player's view has the big title box (`rollbox-assist-player-head`, `-title`) and the same lists. The chat card's rules are under `.rollbox-assist-card`.
- **Connects to:** `panel/panel.css` (variables), `shared/shared.css`.
- **Could interact poorly with:** Foundry's global `button` and `input` styles: overridden under `.rollbox-panel` in the `modules` cascade layer (BUILD-GUIDE "The panel and CSS"). **Assumed.**

## Hooks, overrides, keybindings
- Hooks: `updateActor` (installed by the Other flyout: an assist group changed, so the open tab is drawn again); on the Keeper's client `createChatMessage` and `updateChatMessage` (`onRollerCard`) and the relay types `assistJoin` and `assistLeave` (`registerAssistGroups`).
- Class overrides: none.
- Keybindings: none. Enter in the number line confirms it; it sends nothing.

## Settings
