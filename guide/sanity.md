## Making a Sanity check

1. Select the character (or several) and click **Sanity**. Each character's current SAN is shown under the title.
2. Set the loss: the large line reads **success / failure**, for example **0 / 1D6**.
   - Click a **preset**: 0/1, 0/1D3, 0/1D6, 1/1D4+1, 1/1D6, 1D3/1D10, 1D6/1D20 or 1D10/1D100.
   - Or click either side of the line and type, such as `1D4+1`. Press Enter or click away to confirm; an empty side becomes 0, and anything that isn't a loss turns red.
   - Or click **Custom success or failure loss** for a small dice picker for both sides.
3. Optionally fill in **What caused it** and **Creature** (below).
4. Click **Request from player** (or **Send to each**), or **Send to chat**.

## What caused it

The player sees this instead of the numbers: *"Sanity check · seeing the Deep One"*. Leave it empty and they see *"Sanity (0/1D6)"*. The loss itself is never shown to them before the roll.

## Creature

If the loss comes from a creature, put its kind here, such as *Deep One* or *Ghoul*. The system remembers which creatures each investigator has already seen and caps later losses from the same kind, as the rulebook describes ("getting used to the awfulness"). Use it only for creatures, never for a description.

## What happens next

Everything after the request is the system's: the Sanity roll, the loss (applied when you click **Apply** on its card), the INT roll for temporary insanity, bouts of madness and indefinite insanity.

## Good to know

- Sanity rolls can't be blind or pushed.
- Non-player characters' Sanity cards are made and rolled for you.
- Prefer typing? Turn on **Simplified dice UI** in the [settings](panel.md#settings).
- Losses for creatures are listed in each monster's entry in the Keeper Rulebook; losses for events are the Keeper's call, with examples in the Sanity chapter.
