# Assist

[← Back to the guide](README.md)

Assist is a feature you won't need often. It follows the Keeper Rulebook's **Physical Human Limits** rule (p. 88–89), and is for the moments when several characters put their strength or weight together: lifting a fallen rafter, holding a door shut against something on the other side, or pinning down a struggling cultist.

## The Physical Human Limits rule

This rule covers a corner case: an investigator can't attempt a STR or SIZ check against an opposing value more than 100 points above their own. No roll is allowed.

To get past that, investigators work together. Each helper reduces the opposing value by their own STR (or SIZ), starting with the lowest. Once the opposing value is low enough to be physically possible, the remaining investigators roll. The difficulty comes from what remains: under 50 is Regular, 50 or more is Hard, 90 or more is Extreme. One success is enough.

## How Keeper's Rollbox streamlines assist checks

1. Select all investigators or NPCs involved, and click **Assist**.
2. Input the opposing value to beat, or mark targets (step 5).
3. **Use:** choose what your characters put in, **STR** or **SIZ**.
4. **Against:** choose which of the targets' values counts, **STR** or **SIZ**.
5. Check the list of characters:
   - Click a **name** to leave that character out; click again to bring them back.
   - Click the **crosshair** beside a name to make it a target. Creatures start as targets, and several targets add together.
6. Read the result: who's **Assisting**, who's **Rolling**, the difficulty, and the chance that at least one roller succeeds.
7. Add a **label** if you like, and click **Send to rollers**.

## Who helps and who rolls

Rollbox works out which investigators help reduce the opposing value and which ones make the roll, choosing the split with the best chance that someone succeeds. More helpers make the obstacle lighter and the roll easier, but leave fewer people to roll.

## What the players see

Each roller's player gets an ordinary STR (or SIZ) check at the worked-out difficulty, labelled *"STR · lift the rafter"* (or *"STR · Assist"* without a label). Non-player rollers roll privately for you. A public **Assist** card tells the table who is helping, who is rolling, and that one success is enough. Read the rollers' cards to see how it went.

## Good to know

- Select or deselect tokens with the menu open and it updates at once.
- Assist uses no Blind and no bonus or penalty dice: the difficulty is worked out for you.
- The rule only describes investigators working together; adding targets' values together is a Keeper's ruling Rollbox applies for you.
