# Other

[← Back to the guide](README.md)

The **Other** button holds the checks that don't fit Check or Sanity. It has two sections at the top, like tabs: **Item** and **Luck**.

- With **one** character selected, Other opens on **Item**.
- With **two or more**, it opens on **Luck**, and **Item** is greyed out (items are for one character at a time).

The menu's title follows the section: "Item check" or "Luck check".

## Item

Weapons and items aren't only used in combat. You may want a roll for target practice, a shot at a padlock, an empty pistol thrown at a fleeing cultist, or a candlestick snatched up as an improvised weapon. The system's attack rolls need a target and start combat, so these moments usually mean building the roll by hand. The Item section does it for you.

### When to use it

- Target practice, or a shot at a lock, a rope or a lamp.
- Throwing a weapon that's out of ammunition, or a knife at something that isn't a token.
- Grabbing whatever is to hand as an improvised weapon, with damage.

### Using a weapon

1. Select **one** character, click **Other** and pick **Item** if it isn't already open.
2. Their weapons are listed with the skill each one uses and that skill's Regular, Hard and Extreme values. A weapon with two skills, such as a knife that can also be thrown, has a row for each.
3. Pick one, set the difficulty, dice and Blind, and send it.

The player gets a skill check labelled with the weapon: *"Firearms (Handgun) with the .38 Revolver"*.

### Using an improvised weapon

1. Pick **+ Improvised weapon** and type what it is, such as *Candlestick*.
2. Choose the skill: **Brawl** (the default), **Throw**, or **Other…** for any skill.
3. Add **damage** if you like:
   - a quick-pick: **1D3+DB** up to **1D10+DB** (DB is the character's damage bonus);
   - or type it on the damage line;
   - or open **Custom damage** for the dice picker.

   With **Throw**, the damage bonus is halved (**½DB**), as the rules ask for thrown objects.
4. Send it. The player gets the check plus a **damage** button to roll.

Nothing is added to the character's sheet.

## Luck

A Luck check for the selected characters: is the cab still there, does the torch have batteries?

1. Select one or more characters, click **Other** and pick **Luck** if it isn't already open. Each character's Luck is listed.
2. Choose the **difficulty** and any **bonus or penalty dice**, and switch on **Blind** if you like.
3. Add a **label** if you want to tell the player why they're rolling.
4. Click **Request from player** (or **Send to each**), **Send to chat**, or the link button.

Clicking **Luck** on the [mini sheet](panel.md#clicking-the-mini-sheet) opens this section too.

### Group Luck roll

When something bad could happen to one of the group, the rulebook's Group Luck roll is made by the character with the lowest Luck (Keeper Rulebook p. 89–90).

1. Select the group, click **Other** (it opens on Luck).
2. Tick **Group Luck roll**. Rollbox names the character with the lowest Luck and sends the roll to them alone.
3. If two or more are tied for lowest, it asks **Who rolls?**: pick one. The send buttons stay greyed out until you do.

## Good to know

- Attacks on other tokens stay in the system's own combat: the attacker picks a target and uses the weapon on their sheet. Rollbox doesn't start or change combat.
- A damage bonus of 0 is left out of the damage.
- Luck rolls can't be pushed (Keeper Rulebook p. 84).
