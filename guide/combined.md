# Combined checks

[← Back to the guide](README.md)

One roll compared against two or more checks at once, as in the Keeper Rulebook (p. 92).

*[Animation: a combined Spot Hidden or Listen check — coming soon]*

## Making a combined check

1. Select the character and click **Check**.
2. Tick two or more checks. They can be on different tabs: ticks stay when you switch tabs.
3. Choose **All of them** (the roll must succeed against every check) or **Any one** (one success is enough).
4. Set the difficulty, dice and label as for any [check](check.md), and send it.

The player rolls once and the system compares that roll with each check.

## When to use it

- **Any one:** the rulebook's way of letting someone use the better of two skills. For example, Spot Hidden or Listen to notice someone sneaking up (p. 203).
- **All of them:** a task that needs two skills at once, such as Mechanical Repair and Electrical Repair on a turbine.

## Good to know

- A combined check is always one character's single roll. With several characters selected, each gets their own combined check.
- Combined checks can't be blind: the system always shows the card, so Blind is greyed out.
- A non-player character rolls once, against their highest value (Any one) or their lowest (All of them).
