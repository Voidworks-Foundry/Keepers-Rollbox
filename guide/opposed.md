# Opposed rolls

[← Back to the guide](README.md)

Two characters pit their skills or characteristics against each other: Stealth against Spot Hidden, STR against STR.

*[Animation: creating an opposed roll — coming soon]*

## Making an opposed roll

1. Select **exactly two** tokens and click **Opposed**. The panel shows both characters side by side.
2. For each side, pick a characteristic or a skill from its two tabs. The two sides can use different kinds.
3. Give either side up to two bonus or penalty dice if the situation favours or hinders them.
4. Click **Create opposed card**.

Rollbox makes the system's opposed card with both sides filled in. A non-player side is rolled at once; each player rolls their own side from the card.

## Who wins

The higher level of success wins: critical, extreme, hard, regular, failure (Keeper Rulebook p. 90–91). On a tie, the side with the higher skill or characteristic wins. If those are equal too, it's a stalemate or both roll again. The system's card says "Tie" without naming a winner, so Rollbox shows you, before you create the card, which side would win a tie.

## Attacker and defender

The system's card labels its two sides attacker and defender even outside combat. **⇅ Swap sides** changes which character is which. Outside combat it doesn't change who wins.

## Good to know

- Opposed rolls have no difficulty, because the other side's roll is the difficulty, and they can't be pushed (p. 90–92).
- If a side's skill isn't on that character's sheet, the system adds it at its base value when the card is made; the menu warns you.
- **Cancel** takes you back to the list of names.
- From the mini sheet: right-click one character's characteristic, select the other character's token and right-click theirs, and Opposed opens with both sides filled in. See [Clicking the mini sheet](panel.md#clicking-the-mini-sheet).
