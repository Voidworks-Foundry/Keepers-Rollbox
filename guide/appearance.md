# Appearance and themes

[← Back to the guide](README.md)

**For:** everyone. Every setting here is personal: it changes only your own screen, and applies at once.

![The three themes, then transparency in the Foundry theme, size and menu side](../media/appearance-themes.webp)

Find them in **Configure Settings → Keeper's Rollbox → Appearance**.

## Themes

**Theme** changes how the panel looks. The menus, buttons, places and clicks stay the same in every theme.

- **Foundry** (the default): Foundry's own look, Dark or Light as Foundry's interface is set. It follows a change at once.
- **Art Deco:** CoC7's decorated character sheet: the page texture, the coloured HP, MP, SAN and Luck blocks, the menu as coloured ribbons, the green window bar and the art-deco ornament on the flyouts. Action, Check and Other start with their tabs, sitting in the dip of the ornament (no title bar or name line: the send button says who it goes to, and "Combined · …" for a combined check). Values read CoC7's way: 55 (27/11).
- **CoC7 minimal:** CoC7's compact dark sheet: black bars, red values, MOV · BUILD · DB along the bottom.

The two CoC7 themes use CoC7's own images and fonts from the installed system. Without them, the panel shows the Foundry theme. Chat cards are always CoC7's.

## Colour, transparency and size

- **Panel colour** (Foundry theme only): the background of the panel, its buttons and its flyouts. If a colour would make the text hard to read, the panel shows a readable shade of it. Under Foundry's Light interface, a dark colour shows as its light shade. The CoC7 themes keep CoC7's colours, so the setting is greyed out while one is chosen ("Foundry theme only.").
- **Panel transparency** (Foundry theme only): how much of the map shows through, from 0% (solid) to 70%. Text stays fully readable. In combat the turn controls (Delay, Flee, End turn) follow it exactly as the turn order's cards do, on the Keeper's and the players' panels. Art Deco and CoC7 minimal are always solid, turn controls included, so the setting is greyed out while one is chosen.
- **Panel size:** 80% to 250%. The panel grows from its bottom-left corner, so it stays beside the players list. **While you drag the panel**, turn the mouse wheel to change it in steps of 10% (up: bigger); it grows from the corner it would be anchored to where you drop it, and the new size is saved when you let go. The map doesn't zoom while you hold the panel.
- **Meters on the small cards:** one tick per meter, **HP**, **MP**, **SAN** and **Luck**, all on by default. It decides which bars the character cards (three or more selected), the Investigators list and the turn order's cards show. The full character card always shows all four.

Theme, Panel colour, Panel transparency and Panel size each have a **Reset** button that restores only that setting (applied on Save).

## Where the panel sits

The panel starts beside Foundry's players list, for the Keeper and players alike: its bottom edge is as far from the screen's bottom as the list's, with the same gap between them. It doesn't move up as players join.

- **Allow moving the panel** (on by default, kept per computer): drag the header to move the panel. Off, it stays beside the players list. **Reset position**, beside it, puts it back there at once.
  - While you drag, the menu and flyouts switch side as soon as the panel's centre crosses the middle of the screen (with **Automatic**), so you see where they will open before you drop it.
  - A panel dropped on the right half keeps its distance from Foundry's sidebar: it moves out when the sidebar expands and back when it collapses. It may overlap the chat.
- **Menu and flyouts open to the:** **Automatic** (towards the middle of the screen), **Right** or **Left**. Beside the players list they always open to the right. In the top half of the screen they line up with the top of the character panel and grow down. A flyout that would run off the screen shifts the panel just enough, and it goes back when the flyout closes.
- **Panel layer:** whether Foundry's windows (sheets, journals, roll dialogs) cover the panel.
  - **Bottom:** windows always cover it.
  - **Top:** the panel stays above every window. Tooltips, notifications, right-click menus and pop-up questions still show above it.
  - **Top when active** (the default): like a window, clicking the panel brings it to the front, and clicking a window brings that one forward.
  - **Top when active or selected:** the same, and the panel also comes forward whenever you select tokens.

## Auto-hide

- **Auto-hide the character card** (off by default): after a while without using the panel, the full character sheet drops to the compact card, the one a character gets when several are selected. Hover over the panel to bring it back.
- **Auto-hide after:** how long, from 1 second to 5 minutes (default 10 seconds).

## Settings on this page

All in the Rollbox panel section (Look and Placement), all personal: **Theme**, **Panel colour**, **Panel transparency**, **Panel size**, **Allow moving the panel**, **Menu and flyouts open to the**, **Panel layer**, **Auto-hide the character card**, **Auto-hide after**, **Meters on the small cards**. Defaults are in the [settings reference](settings.md).

## Shortcuts

Drag the header to move the panel. A click on a value never moves it.
