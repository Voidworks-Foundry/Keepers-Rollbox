## Making a group check

1. Select every character who should roll (drag a box around their tokens, or shift-click them).
2. Click **Check**. With several characters, the Skill tab lists every skill A to Z.
3. Tick the check, set the difficulty, dice and label, and click **Send to each**.

Each player gets their own request in chat. Non-player characters are rolled privately for you, straight away. The menu then lists who was asked, who was rolled for you, and anyone skipped, with the reason.

## Group Luck roll

When something bad could happen to one of the group, the rulebook's Group Luck roll is made by the character with the lowest Luck (Keeper Rulebook p. 89–90).

1. Select the group and click **Check**.
2. On the **Attribute** tab, tick **Luck** only.
3. Choose **Group Luck roll**. Rollbox finds the lowest Luck and sends the roll to that character alone. If two or more are tied, it asks you to pick.

The names list shows each character's Luck, so you can see who it will be.

## Good to know

- Sanity checks for a whole group work the same way, from the [Sanity](sanity.md) menu.
- To have the strongest people work together on one task instead, see [Assist](assist.md).
