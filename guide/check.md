# Check

[← Back to the guide](README.md)

Ask a character for any check: a characteristic, Luck, Sanity, Idea, Know or a skill.

## Making a check

1. Select the character's token and click **Check**.
2. Pick a tab: **Characteristic** (STR, CON, SIZ, DEX, APP, INT, POW, EDU), **Attribute** (Luck, Sanity, Idea, Know) or **Skill**.
3. Tick the check you want.
4. Choose the **difficulty** and any **bonus or penalty dice**, and switch on **Blind** if you like.
5. Add a **label** if you want to tell the player why they're rolling.
6. Click **Request from player**, **Send to chat**, or the small link button to copy the request as a link you can paste anywhere.

## The skill list

With one character selected, the list shows their trained skills first, highest first, with the Regular, Hard and Extreme values (full, half and a fifth). Under a divider come all the other skills, A to Z, at their base value from the Keeper Rulebook.

Type in the search box to find a skill fast. If what you type isn't listed, such as a specialisation like *Pilot (Boat)*, a **+ Add** row appears: click it to use that skill at its base value.

A skill missing from a non-player character's sheet is rolled at its base value, and nothing is added to their sheet. For a player's character, the system adds a missing skill to the sheet at its base value before rolling (rare, since investigator sheets list every skill).

## Difficulty and dice

- **Regular, Hard or Extreme:** the player needs their full value, half or a fifth.
- **Hidden:** the player rolls without knowing the difficulty, and you set it on the result card afterwards.
- **Bonus and penalty dice:** up to two either way. The rulebook keeps these mainly for opposed rolls; on a normal check, set the difficulty first and use dice as the exception (Keeper Rulebook p. 91–92).
- **Blind:** the result is hidden from the player and shown only to you.

## Labels

The label appears after the check's name, so the player knows what's going on: *"Hard Spot Hidden · notice the hidden knife"*. Keep it short; the system shows it on a button in chat.

## Idea and Know

On the **Attribute** tab, **Idea** is an INT roll and **Know** an EDU roll, as in the Keeper Rulebook (p. 90). The player's request names Idea or Know.

## Good to know

- There's no "pushed" option: if a roll fails and the player justifies a push, they push it from the **Push** button on the system's result card. Luck and Sanity rolls can't be pushed (p. 84).
- Ticking two or more checks makes a [combined check](combined.md). Selecting several tokens makes a [group check](group.md).
