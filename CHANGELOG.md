# Changelog

## 0.9.0

**A pre-release.** Keeper's Rollbox is a panel for Call of Cthulhu 7th Edition in Foundry VTT: the Keeper's control desk for the table's rolls, and each player's shortcut to their own investigator, with the CoC7 system doing all the rolling through its own links and cards. 0.9.0 is the first release since 0.1.1 and opens the feedback rounds before 1.0 (0.9.1, 0.9.2, …); install it from the manifest link and Foundry offers each later release as an update. In short:

- **Players get a panel of their own** (on by default): their own investigator, their own checks straight into chat, and one action a round on their turn.
- **Combat runs from the panel:** attacks, aiming, reloading, maneuvers and spells through CoC7's own cards; a turn order with End turn, Delay, standby and Flee; the weapon-drawn notice, About to attack and the first blow; being-attacked alerts that answer in one click.
- **Checks reach further:** combined checks, group checks, Sanity with one-click losses, Luck and the Group Luck roll, Assist, and opposed rolls with the target picker.
- **Three themes** (Foundry, Art Deco, CoC7 minimal), a settings page with the main features on top, and a setup check that asks how custom guns load.
- **Audited and tidied:** players can't roll for others' characters through Rollbox, combat rules keep running across scenes, and the code, tests and documentation were checked against each other.

Everything since 0.1.1, in plain words:

- **For the Keeper and the players.** For the Keeper, Rollbox is a control desk for the table's rolls: ask for rolls, roll for NPCs, set up two-sided rolls and run combat. Each player gets a panel of their own investigator, to make their own checks and take their one action a round.
- **The panel** sits beside the players list and no longer moves up as players join. With nothing selected, the Keeper's panel lists each player's investigator with HP, SAN and Luck as small vertical bars; click a row to select that token (turn it off with **Show the players when nothing is selected**). One character: their character sheet (HP, MP, SAN, Luck, the eight characteristics). Two: one sheet for both, the second character in tan. Three or more: a character card each, with the same bars; hover a card and click its × to deselect that token. The menu beside it is **Action · Check · Other**, with **Opposed** on top unless exactly one is selected. Action shows with one or two selected.
- **One send button**, named for who gets the roll: **Send to all players** (nothing selected), **Send to <player> (<name>)**, **Send to each (N)**, or **Roll for <name>** / **Roll for NPCs** when only NPCs are selected. A player's roll is always a private whisper; an NPC is rolled for you at once. There is no public request; for an open link, Ctrl + click a check's row in Check to copy its CoC7 roll link.
- **Click the character sheet.** A characteristic opens Check with it ticked, SAN opens the Sanity tab and Luck opens Other on Luck. Shift, Alt and right clicks work as on CoC7's own sheet: Shift + click sends a plain check at once, a right click starts an opposed roll. Ctrl + click opens the flyout as a plain click. All are listed in the guide's Shortcuts page.
- **Action.** Attack with a weapon or unarmed, aim (in combat), reload, load one round and fire, an improvised weapon, a maneuver with the Build dice (its button names the goal: "Attempt to knock down"), or a spell through CoC7's own Cast. Out of combat, Reload is one full reload. With two selected, the first acts on the second, with **Swap**. The target box shows the target's distance, Build and statuses, and Rollbox sets the bonus or penalty dice on CoC7's own attack card. A first casting's Hard POW marks the spell as cast when it succeeds.
- **Check.** Characteristic, Skill and Sanity tabs. Any skill, trained first, the rest at base value. Difficulty (with Hidden), bonus or penalty dice, Blind and a label. Tick two or more for a combined check (All of them or Any one). Select several for a group check, or none to send it to every investigator. Sanity with one-click losses, a cause the player sees and a creature for CoC7's record. The rows take CoC7's shortcuts too.
- **Other.** Item (one character: their weapons as skill checks, or an improvised weapon with damage), Luck (with the Group Luck roll: only the lowest Luck rolls) and Assist (characters combine a characteristic, or one character against a typed value; Rollbox works out who helps and who rolls).
- **Opposed rolls** from the Opposed button or a right click on a characteristic. The other side waits in red until you select someone or pick a target. CoC7's opposed card is made and an NPC side is rolled at once.
- **Combat in the panel.** The turn order with the current turn as the full sheet, readied firearm and this round's notes. Turn controls: End turn, Delay (arrows to choose how far down the order), Flee and End combat, each asking once. Readied firearms get CoC7's Draw gun at the start of each round.
- **Weapon drawn out of combat:** a notice card only you see, even when a player drew it: Roll to notice (Spot Hidden), Nobody notices, Everyone notices, Start combat or Ignore. Each investigator who notices is told privately. Turn it off with the Gameplay setting **Notice when a weapon is drawn**. Drawing in combat posts no card. An attack with no combat running gives you a surprise card, with Ignore too.
- **Status cards** above the character sheet: CoC7's conditions and Rollbox's own (aiming, dived for cover), with what each means on hover.
- **Player Rollbox** (on by default; a world where you turned it off keeps that): each player's panel shows only their own investigator, which they must both own and have assigned (a player without one sees a short message saying how to assign one), with only the features you tick. **Make check** rolls their check straight into chat, with no request to themselves; Other offers **Make opposed card** and Assist (helping another character); there is no Sanity or Luck tab (Sanity checks and calling for Luck are yours). One action a round: they can get ready any time and act on their turn, and after acting or diving everything greys out, though a weapon can still be picked for next round. They pick opponents, helpers and targets by targeting. When a CoC7 attack names their character they get an alert: Dodge, Fight back or Counter in melee or against a maneuver; Dive for cover or Stand your ground when shot at.
- **Themes:** Foundry (the default, Dark or Light with Foundry's interface), Art Deco (CoC7's decorated sheet) and CoC7 minimal (CoC7's compact sheet). A saved Dark or Light theme becomes Foundry.
- **Appearance:** Panel colour and transparency for the Foundry theme (the CoC7 themes are solid, and the two settings are greyed out while one is chosen); Panel size from 80% to 250%; move the panel anywhere; menus open to the left, right or automatically; Panel layer (whether Foundry's windows cover the panel); auto-hide to the compact card.
- **Settings, features on top:** five switches open the page (Rollbox panel, Player Rollbox, Turn order and turn controls, Gameplay rules, Setup check at login), each with its own section below that greys out while it's off: Rollbox panel (look, placement, behaviour, menus), Player Rollbox (what players can do, and each player's Roll my attacks at once), Turn order and turn controls (your view), Gameplay rules (starting a fight, firearms, melee, and rolling attacks at once) and Setup check (Check now). "Menus open to the" is now called "Menu and flyouts open to the"; your saved choice stays. Players don't see Rollbox's settings while the Player Rollbox is off, and never see the ones that only work for you.
- **No keys taken:** Tab never stops on Rollbox. Escape closes an open Rollbox flyout (turn that off with **Escape closes the flyout**); with no flyout open it is Foundry's.
- **Documentation:** a new guide, one page per subject, with an animation or screenshot for each, made for this version.
- **For contributors:** code in one folder per part (flyouts, combat, status, player), an end-to-end check in Foundry, and CI on Node.js 26.

First test release, for Foundry VTT 14 and Call of Cthulhu 7th Edition (CoC7) 8.15.

- **The Keeper's panel.** Select tokens and a small panel appears on top of the players list. One token: a mini sheet (portrait, owner, HP, MP, SAN, Luck and the eight characteristics). Several: a list of names with the values that matter for the open menu. Only the Keeper sees it.
- **Check.** Characteristics, Luck, Sanity, Idea, Know and every skill (trained first with Regular · Hard · Extreme values, then untrained at base value), with search and "+ Add" for unlisted specialisations. Difficulty (Regular, Hard, Extreme, Hidden), up to two bonus or penalty dice, Blind and a label. Request from player, Send to chat or Copy link.
- **Combined checks:** tick two or more checks for one roll, "All of them" or "Any one".
- **Group checks:** Send to each selected character; non-player characters are rolled privately for the Keeper.
- **Group Luck roll:** only the character with the lowest Luck rolls.
- **Sanity:** one-click presets or any loss, a cause the player sees instead of the numbers, and a creature name so CoC7 can cap repeated losses.
- **Item:** a character's weapons as skill checks (for shots at a lock and the like), or an improvised weapon with optional damage (half damage bonus when thrown).
- **Opposed rolls:** two characters, each with their own check and dice; CoC7's opposed card is made and a non-player side is rolled at once.
- **Assist:** several characters combine STR or SIZ against something too heavy for one (Physical Human Limits); Rollbox works out who helps and who rolls for the best chance, and posts a card saying so.
- **Results:** after sending, the menu lists who was asked, who was rolled for you and anything not sent.
- **Settings (all personal):** show or hide the panel; Simplified dice UI (type losses and damage instead of using presets and pickers); Dark or Light theme, Panel colour, transparency and size, each with a Reset button.
- Rollbox only sends CoC7's own roll requests and cards; it never changes a roll, a card or a rule.
